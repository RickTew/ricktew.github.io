How I build

The three steps I follow.

  1. 1The foundation firstSystems that work like ordinary software: bookings, payments and records that run the same way every time, without AI.
  2. 2Then AI where it helpsDrafting a reply, sorting a message, answering from the business's own written answers. Each helper has one job.
  3. 3A person at the helmBy default nothing goes out to a customer without a press. Sending on its own is the owner's choice, task by task, after it has been tested.
