From California, working across Asia, the U.S. and Europe

My work has one aim: help people get a Black Belt in what they do. It started on the mat. Today it also means AI (some now say SI) and computer work: building the systems a business runs on, and training the people who run it. Here is the history, then what I build now.

My history

Teaching, building and running things, from the backyard dojo to today.

  • From 13I fell in love with the ninja art of ninjitsu. My first training center was the backyard, with carpet wrapped around a plum tree for a striking dummy. Scouting gave me trips into the outdoors and a system for setting goals.
  • At 15On TV for my ninja skills. Around then I drew Arena RPG on paper: hand-drawn boards, character sheets and hand-cut tiles.
  • At 16Teaching. From that point on I have been teaching, sharing and living the martial arts.
  • At 19Opened my own dojo. Then I took a break to study more martial arts, joined stunt school and learned Tony Robbins' tactics.
  • At 21Moved to Europe alone. The only person I knew there moved to another country within weeks, and I stayed: more than six years in and out of the Netherlands, in magazines and on TV, teaching seminars across the country. I was offered a part in a popular Dutch film and turned it down to spend time with students in Spain.
  • My own systemRTMS, Rick Tew's Martial Science: Modern American Ninjitsu crossed with MMA, built to teach movement, not moves. Five ranking levels from beginner to instructor, with a mental training system of its own.
  • Camps and toursFirst in the martial arts industry to run live-in Ninja Training Camps. Ran the CMS ninja camp near Yosemite, then BLAST Tours: travel and training on four continents. I have brought students from the US to Europe and from Europe to the US, and the first group on my California tour was Dutch.
  • Business and the webBuilt MartialArtSupply.com from the ground up, in the same years as the camps, into a business many people copied. Built my own websites, video and online tools from the start, and built and sold websites and domain names. Stunt choreography for film.
  • Schools and gymsFounded and ran martial arts schools, dojos and gyms in the US, Europe and Asia. Classes for groups of 10 to 30, year after year, where speaking, coaching and teaching happen at once.
  • Speaking and coachingSpoke at HP, TU Delft and the University of Rotterdam, and ran workshops for teams, including the BLAST workshop. Tony Robbins Certified Strategic Intervention Coach. In Black Belt magazine and on TV in the US and Holland. Martial arts as therapy: using the training to work on fear, pain, confidence and aggression.
  • WritingThe five books of The Mental Martial Art: Be a Black Belt in What You Do (ACE, MAK, MBS, NRG and CMT). Today they live on as WinJitsu, my pocket dojo app with five belts and 90 lessons, and as the Tewtors workbooks for grown-ups, teens, parents and teachers.
  • ThailandFounded NinjaGym on Koh Samui, an indoor ninja playground for kids, and built the app it runs on. NerdHostel, a gamer hostel with co-working, sits across the street.
  • NowAI and computer work for small businesses, all of it remote, and games under my TewBit Games brand: TEWGO is live on the App Store, and I am building Arena RPG for real.

How I build

The three steps I follow.

  1. 1The foundation firstSystems that work like ordinary software: bookings, payments and records that run the same way every time, without AI.
  2. 2Then AI where it helpsDrafting a reply, sorting a message, answering from the business's own written answers. Each helper has one job.
  3. 3A person at the helmBy default nothing goes out to a customer without a press. Sending on its own is the owner's choice, task by task, after it has been tested.
