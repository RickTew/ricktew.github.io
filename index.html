1 · My story

Get a Black Belt in what you do.

Hi, I'm Rick Tew. Even though my last name says otherwise, there is only one of me. But I do have a split personality. I believe we all do, and the mix of those traits is what makes us who we are.

My passion is helping people find their key strengths and put them together, so they get more out of life. It started on the mat. Today it also means building the systems a business runs on, and training the people who run it.

I only teach from experience, and I learn by doing. The martial arts, the camps, the books, the gyms and the apps: this page is all of it, in the order it happened.

Roots

It is in the doing.

While others talk, I try to walk. That walk began when I was a boy and ran away from home in Indiana to be in California. It worked, and a new journey began.

As a young teen I joined the Boy Scouts. I still remember working with my Dad to finish the sit-ups for another award on my belt. Scouting gave me trips into the outdoors and a system for setting goals, and it is why I have taken students to places like Yosemite.

I connected with shows like Godzilla, where the one everybody saw as the bad guy was often the good guy. The underdog is misunderstood, but endures and wins. That is how I saw the ninja, and it pulled me into the martial arts.

In my teens I fell in love with the ninja art of ninjitsu. My first training center was the backyard, with carpet wrapped around a plum tree for a striking dummy. A TV crew filmed me there throwing stars, climbing ropes and running my obstacle course.

My goals were simple: get the splits, earn a black belt, open my own dojo. Done, done and done.

I also drew a game by hand: the boards, the character sheets, the cut-out tiles. Today it is Arena RPG, and I am building it for real with AI.

First dojo

I was a teenage ninja.

By 16 I was already teaching. From that point on I have been teaching, sharing and living the martial arts.

At 19 I opened my own dojo, a martial arts training school.

Then I took a break to study more martial arts and join stunt school. I learned Tony Robbins' tactics too, and my goals grew into a new dream: to move and live in Europe.

Holland

A ninja in the Netherlands.

I spent more than six years in and out of the Netherlands, mostly in the Naarden Bussum area. I was in magazines and on TV, taught seminars across the country, and trained students outdoors at Naarden Vesting, an old war fortress with a lot of grass and places to jump around.

I was even offered a part in a popular Dutch film. I turned it down to spend time with students in Spain. My tour idea was born in Holland too: the first group on my California tour was Dutch.

2 · RTMS

Rick Tew's Martial Science.

A martial science, not a style: Modern American Ninjitsu crossed with MMA, built to teach movement, not moves. Five ranking levels, beginner to instructor.

Nine areas in every level: stances, rolls, falls, strikes, kicks, body movement, knowledge, combat and weapons.

The mental training is its own system, not a by-product of the physical skills. And we do most of our training outside.

4 · Camps

CMS, College of Martial Science.

From Europe I kept thinking about my dream: a live-in, multi martial arts ninja training camp. So I created CMS, the College of Martial Science, to teach students what I had learned, and started it near Yosemite National Park.

I was the first in the martial arts industry to run live-in Ninja Training Camps.

Mind, body and spirit: WinJitsu for the mind, martial arts and fitness for the body, and the outdoors, travel and real human interaction for the spirit.

After my dream came true in the States, I set a new goal: a program in Asia. I picked Thailand.

5 · WinJitsu

WinJitsu, the mental martial art.

"Win" plus "Jitsu": the True Art of Winning. What I learned on the way became a system, and five books: Be a Black Belt in What You Do. One book per finger of the hand: ACE, MAK, MBS, NRG and CMT.

Today it is one app: the WinJitsu books for the mind, Pocket Dojo for the body, and member strategies, members helping each other and daily updates for the spirit.

Pocket Dojo is my home study program: Rick Tew's Martial Science, belt by belt, on your phone. Five belts, Yellow to Red, and 90 lessons.
