Say this, in this order

Press record, read each line, finish the sentence in your own words. Two minutes is plenty. On video, point the camera at the thing you are describing while you say it.

  1. "My name is ..., my business is ..., and we are in ..." Who you are and where.
  2. "The job that eats my time is ..." What. The inbox, the bookings, the invoices, the renewals. Name it.
  3. "Right now it is done by ..." Who. You, a staff member, an accountant, nobody.
  4. "It happens every ... and I want it fixed by ..." When. Daily, every Monday, month end. And your deadline.
  5. "It lives in ..." Where. Which app, which inbox, which spreadsheet, which drawer or wall.
  6. "It matters because ..." Why. What it costs you when it goes wrong, and what you would do with the hours back.
  7. "The steps today are: first ..., then ..., then ..." How. Walk through it once, slowly, the way you would train a new employee. On a computer, do it on screen while you talk.
  8. "If you fixed one thing first, make it ..." Your pick. Then anything else on your mind.
