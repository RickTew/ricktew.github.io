The difference nobody names

Using AI and operating with it are not the same thing.

Almost every business that says it uses AI means this: a few people type questions into a chat box, each in their own way, and whatever they work out stays in their own head. It helps a bit. It is not something the business owns.

Using AI

Everyone has their own trick, and nobody can see any of them.

  • Ten people, ten different ways of asking, ten different qualities of answer.
  • The one person who got good at it is the only one who got good at it.
  • A new hire starts from a blank box and works it out again from scratch.
  • When that person leaves, it leaves with them.
  • Something starts coming out wrong and nobody can tell you what changed.

Operating with AI

One way of working, written down once, and every worker has it.

  • The same instructions for everybody, so the answer is the same quality every time.
  • One person makes it better and everyone has the better one the next morning.
  • A new hire starts on day one with your best way of working already loaded.
  • People come and go and the know-how stays in the business.
  • A change makes things worse and it gets put back in a minute.

This is just how a dojo has always worked. Nobody invents their own private kata in the corner. The whole school trains the same form, the sensei sharpens it, and the week it gets sharper, everybody got sharper. One person improving is one person improving. One form improving is the school improving.

So that is what I set up in your business. Your best way of doing a thing gets written down properly, handed to every AI worker you have, kept current, and it belongs to you. Not to me, and not to whoever happened to be good at chatbots.

I run my own shop this way first, the same as everything else here. Everything my ninjas know sits in one place, kept like code with every version saved, and it installs everywhere I work. Sharpen one and they all get sharper the same day.
