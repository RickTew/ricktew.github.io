Not without your say. Every ninja starts at white belt: propose only. Drafts pile up for your review, and a person presses every send, pay and post. By default nothing goes out without a press. Sending on its own is your choice, task by task, and only after it has been tested.

Can I get just one desk?

A desk is one AI ninja on one job, like the Front Desk drafting your support replies. And yes: that is how every Dojo starts. The free look picks the one job that eats the most hours, usually the inbox, and one ninja takes it. More join once it has proven itself, and the price stays $2,222 a month, flat. Can somebody's AI assistant deal with you instead of them?

Yes. The solutions list and both prices sit in a tool an assistant can read, and the mailbox is a tool it can call. Does anyone test it before my customers see it?

Yes, and not by me clicking around. Simulated players go first: hundreds of rounds, every button pressed, looking for stalls, dead ends and bad sums. Real-life testing is yours: nobody knows your business and your customers like you do. Testing with hired people is an added cost.
