What does ACE stand for?

Achieving Combined Excellence. The thumb stands for it: achievement, synergy (your combined parts) and a thumbs up attitude, together.

What if I cannot name a strength?

Ask what you do well without trying hard, or what people come to you for. Look at work, home, fun and the hard times you turned around.

Should I ignore my weak spots?

No. Everyone has weak spots. Keep your strengths at the front, and ask someone who is good at it to help.
