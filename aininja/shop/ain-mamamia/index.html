MamaMia

One little page for every little first.

SKU AIN-MAMAMIA

Baby photos lost in the family chat?

I built this for one baby girl and her mom. Want one for your baby? I build it.

One page for your baby. Mama fills it in on her phone, and the whole family opens it from one link. No app to install, no account to make.

  • The age, live at the top. Tap it to count in days, weeks or heartbeats.
  • Photos and videos, the firsts, month by month, a growth chart and the first words.
  • The family waves or says hello with a photo or a short video. It waits for Mama’s yes.
  • Messages for Mama go to Mama’s phone, never onto the page.
  • One button turns the photos into a short movie with music, to save and share.
  • Letters sealed until a birthday, and cards for the sitter that only Mama and the sitter see.
  • A secret link, and a family code if you want one.
