By consciously practicing positive creativity, we can balance our thoughts and emotions.

Does being positive mean ignoring problems?

No. You still look at the bad experience. You choose to learn from it and go on, instead of dwelling on it.

What is wrong with saying don't?

The mind thinks in pictures. Say "Don't eat the pizza" and it pictures pizza. Say what you want instead: "Drive safely."

What is a thumbs down attitude?

Rick's name for an angry, negative mood. When you notice it, ask what is funny, what is useful, and what the positive spin is.
