Questions

The ones I get asked.

What does $222 actually build?

The first version of one thing: a website of a handful of pages with a contact or booking form, or a small app with one job. It is scoped on one page before I start, and that page is the deal. Bigger builds are quoted separately, and the hosting price does not change.

Whose name is the site in?

The domain is yours and stays yours. The hosting runs on my accounts, which is the whole point: no logins to share, nothing for you to renew. If you ever want to leave, you keep your domain and your content.

Do I have to talk to you first?

No. The Intake replaces the first call: twenty to forty minutes of buttons, your own words, and a voice note or a short video of how the work runs today. A plan comes back. If a call is the faster way after that, I will say so.

How do I pay?

An invoice arrives by mail, monthly or yearly, with a card payment link and a PDF. Both live installs also see their bills inside their own app, with a paid archive. The build fee is paid once, when the scope is agreed.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. Monthly ends at the end of the paid month; yearly runs to the end of the paid year. The domain and the content are yours.

Is this the Dojo?

It is the entry door to it. The Dojo offers on the AI Ninja page build and run an AI workforce for a business. R2 Hosting is smaller: the site or app, hosted and kept running. Many will only ever need this one.
