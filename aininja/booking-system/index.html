Questions

The ones I get asked.

What does $222 actually build?

The first version of one thing: a website of a handful of pages with a contact or booking form, or a small app with one job. It is scoped on one page before I start, and that page is the deal. Bigger builds are quoted separately, and the hosting price does not change.

Does it replace the apps I already pay for?

Not by default. It works alongside what you already use, like your booking app or your till. The free look maps what connects to them, and whether anything is worth replacing. Guest messages from Booking.com, Agoda or Airbnb arrive as email, and the Front Desk drafts replies to them like any other mail.

Do I have to manage any of the tech?

No. Both plans are built on my stack and stay there: the hosting, the database, the mail plumbing, every setting that holds it together. It is the same stack my own businesses and my clients' run on, and it is why the price is flat. For you that means no dashboard, no console, no certificate to renew, no password to keep somewhere safe, and no bug that is yours to fix. When something needs changing, you tell me. Yours, always: your domain, your content and your data.

How do I pay?

Two invoices to begin: the $222 build, paid once when the scope is agreed, which includes your first month of hosting. Then the hosting bill, monthly or yearly, starting the month after, by mail with a card payment link and a PDF. Both live installs also see their bills inside their own app, with a paid archive.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid month, or on a yearly R2 Hosting plan at the end of the paid year. All sales are final, so nothing already paid is refunded. Everything runs on my stack, so there is nothing to hand back or log out of. Your domain, your content and your data leave with you, and I do not keep a copy. The build and the hosting end with the subscription, and nothing is charged after.
