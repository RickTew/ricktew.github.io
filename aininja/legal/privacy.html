Privacy

The Digital Dojo, operated by Tew's Inc · Last updated 13 August 2026

Short, because there is not much to disclose. This site is built to know as little about you as possible.

The offer page and the quiz

  • No tracking. The page sets no analytics cookies and runs no third-party trackers.
  • The quiz computes in your browser. Your answers never leave your device unless you choose to send them. The result shows without asking for an email.
  • If you press a contact button, your own mail app opens with your quiz answers pre-filled so you can see exactly what you are sending, edit it, or not send it at all. Nothing is transmitted until you press send in your own mail program.

What we hold if you write or subscribe

  • Email: if you write in, we have your email and what you sent. It is used to reply and to serve you, never sold, never added to a list you did not ask for.
  • Payments: subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Card details go to Stripe, not to us; we see what Stripe shows a merchant (name, email, subscription status).
  • Client work: what we learn about your business while building your Dojo is covered by the confidentiality section of the Terms of Service.

Deletion

Ask, and correspondence and records we hold about you are deleted, except what payment law requires Stripe and us to keep.

Questions about any of this: write to me and a human answers.
