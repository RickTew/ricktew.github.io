Questions

What can I see before I pay?

Every lesson in all five belts is listed. Four are open free: Horse Stance, Cat Stance, Forward Roll and Reverse Roll.

Are the lessons on video?

Not today. Each lesson is the written method, the applications and the steps, with a step-by-step guide; the stances come with footprint diagrams.

How do I ask Rick a question?

On any lesson you can open, press Ask Sensei Tew. The answer comes back right there on the lesson.

Can my kids use it?

Sign-up is for adults, 18 and over. It is built for training with a parent, never for kids alone.

What are the Ninja Tools lessons?

The ninth area of every belt. Those lessons open after you accept a waiver.

What is the system behind it?

Rick Tew's Martial Science, the system I teach.
