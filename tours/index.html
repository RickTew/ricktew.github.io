BLAST Travel & Training

2-Week martial arts and group adventures

BLAST is an acronym for Breathing, Laughing, Adventuring, Stretching, and Thinking.
All integrated as part of Rick Tew's Travel & Training Tour

Make life a BLAST

Spirit and the BLAST experience means living

Rick Tew put in a lot of effort to bring a Mind-Body-Spirit balance to the martial art camps. Our courses provide an equal balance of all three areas. But few understand what we mean when we say, “Spirit.”

For us, it represents Life-Spirit and our definition of this Life Spirit is:

  • Spending quality time with others of like mind
  • Getting out into nature and the great outdoors
  • Sharing space with animals and other creatures

Items owned and the stuff we buy will come and go in our lives, but experiences will last us a lifetime.

The money spent on activities and experiences can also bring you more joy than investing those same funds into just more things.

Psychology professor Dr. Thomas Gilovich proved this with a in-depth study spanning over two decades. He compared the feelings associated with buying stuff vs the feelings associated with buying an experience. The results showed that spending money on travel, active adventures, and other events, helps one to not only achieve a higher level of satisfaction and happiness, it also helps one to shape their personality. In addition to feeling more fulfilled and the molding of ones character, the joy from this strategy also lasts longer.

“Travel is the only thing you buy that makes you richer.”

Tew's Travel & Training Thailand

Travel with other like minded students as you train daily with the Master Instructor Rick Tew

You will dive into a complete Multi-Martial Art system and train daily in any one of the key 5 Levels of the Martial Arts designed by Rick Tew.

We currently offer long-term training on the island of Samui in Thailand and tours in California.

Mr. Tew has been running martial arts camps and tours for more than 20 years. The students have the most fun and memorable experiences when they travel and train together under the guidance and teaching of Rick Tew. Rick has brought students from the US to Europe and students from Europe to the US for amazing adventures, stories and friendships. Many students who attended a program many years ago will still remember how awesome the travel adventures were. Now Mr. Tew is bringing students from the US and Europe to Thailand. But all new students from around the world are welcome to join in on the adventure and get the experience.

Training

Each VIP student will be able to submit request for specific training and focus that they would like to receive. We then create a unique and intensive seminar taught by Rick Tew that includes each of the student's request. This will ensure that no two classes are ever the same. In addition to the requests, we will also be sure to include the base training and introduction to beginners along with the seminar program training. Usually this will be about 2-6 hours each day in the following format:

Class 1:

BASE training session: Stances, Strikes, Kicks, Rolls, Falls, and Self-Defense.

Class 2:

Student submitted requests for what they would like to learn on their visit.

Winjitsu

In addition to the above physical classes, VIP members will have ample time for conversation and training in other areas of interest. They will be conducted at dinners, organized sessions or during travel when Mr. Tew is available and under a non-strict format. In other words, it is in our leisure time and managed via conversations out and about.

Camps and Long-term training

Students interested in continuing their training can discuss with Mr. Tew during the program about returning or continuing to learn the system. Often a discount will be available for students who take advantage of an additional program.
