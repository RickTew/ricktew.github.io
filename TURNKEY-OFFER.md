# The Turn-Key Console offer (working brief for an /aininja/ page)

Working material for a future AI Ninja offer page. Not a live page: excluded
from Pages in `_config.yml`, so it is repo-only. The build-side template
(packaging checklist, offer shape with no figures) lives in the Dojo; THIS
file carries the pricing and the page copy, per Rick's 27 Aug split: the Dojo
does the build, RickTew does the pricing and the landing page.

## The offer, in the buyer's words

You run a coaching, healing or course business. Your world is in pieces: the
course on one platform, payments on another, the replay in an email, support
in a fourth place. I run businesses on one console I built: programs,
customer care, bookings, payments, community, affiliates, one login. You can
have the same console, with your name on the door, running this month.

## The three tiers (each stands alone, each upsells the next)

1. **The Setup: $2,000 to $20,000.** The ready-to-run console with your name
   on it. Your team logged in and working within days, quick-start guide
   included.
2. **The Customization: +$2,000 to $20,000.** Your look, your programs, your
   flows. The console bent to your business.
3. **I Run It: +$2,000 to $20,000.** You do the coaching; the machine is
   operated for you: the inbox answered, the numbers read, the fixes shipped.
   You buy the outcome, not software.

## Where in the band: priced by size, not by features

| Band | Who | Setup |
|---|---|---|
| **Solo** | Just you, maybe an assistant | from $2,000 |
| **Team** | 2 to 10 people | $5,000 to $10,000 |
| **Academy** | More than 10, multiple programs | $12,000 to $20,000 |

The same size sets the band for every tier. Within the band, three needs
position the exact quote: how many programs they sell, how many languages
they serve, and how heavy their support load runs. Never per-person linear
pricing: a per-head meter punishes growth and turns every hire into a
re-quote; bands keep the quote a one-line answer, and the exact number is
the conversation that attaches tiers 2 and 3.

## The landing page skeleton (built in this site's own style)

- **Hero:** "Your coaching business, ready to run." Sub-line: one console,
  one login, everything in one place. One CTA.
- **The pain strip:** three lines the buyer recognizes instantly (links all
  over the planet; paid-but-locked-out customers; the founder routing the
  inbox at night).
- **The console strip:** what it does (programs, care, bookings, payments,
  community, affiliates), each as an outcome line, zero tech talk, no vendor
  names.
- **The three tiers** as three cards with the bands above.
- **The size bands** as a small "what would mine cost" row, or a sizing quiz
  (five questions: size, programs, languages, who answers support, what
  hurts most; band shown ungated, details capture below it).
- **Proof line:** "I run my own companies on it every day."
- **CTA:** this site's rule stands: every contact action goes through The
  Letter Slot (`/aininja/#opt-8c`), never a mailto and no email address in
  page source. A sizing quiz on this site would need its own capture rail
  through the same edge function.

## The launch play

Before the page is even polished: one five-sentence email to the warm list
of coaches and owners (the pain, the console, the month timeline, the
starting band, "reply and I will show you yours"), with the offer link so
every click can land while the conversation starts.
