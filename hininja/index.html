The belt program

Want a belt? Earn it here.

For the serious: 2 months per belt, yellow belt first, up to 5 belts in a year. You earn each belt in a test with Rick at the end of its 2 months. The art is Rick's own system, Modern American Ninjitsu (most people spell it ninjutsu).

Mastery

Moving to Thailand? Earn all five belts.

Most people come for two weeks. Mastery is for the ones who stay: all five belts, yellow to red, over 1 to 2 years, with Rick.

How Mastery works

  1. Rick shows you

    A tactic or technique, in a private or small-group session.

  2. You practice

    On your own and in the group classes, until you master it.

  3. You earn the belt

    Each belt ends in a test with Rick. Then the next one.

It is not daily private lessons, which run $222 an hour or more. Rick teaches, and you put in the hours: the old-school way.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need experience?

No. It is built for beginners. Trained before? Welcome too. Everyone trains at their level.

Who teaches the classes?

Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week, mornings and evenings in turn. On the other days you practice with Guides: people who trained here before you and are a step or two ahead.

Am I too old?

No. There is no upper age limit: the relaxed plan is for everyone, and every drill has an easier version. Tell me about an old injury before you come.

Got an injury, or not very fit?

Tell me before you come. Every drill has an easier version.

I am 16 or 17. Can I come on my own?

Yes, with a parent's signature on the papers: the enrolment, a travel consent letter and a medical consent. Write to me before you book.

When can I start?

Any day from 15 January 2027: classes run all year, Monday to Friday. The next group starts 22 January 2027. At checkout you add your arrival date.

Can I come for one week, or stay longer?

One week is half the price, $1,111: write to me first. Longer: 3 weeks is $3,222, the full month $3,997, and a belt in the belt program is $7,994 for 2 months. Moving here? Mastery is all five belts over 1 to 2 years, $15,988.

How much time with Rick do I get in Mastery?

200 hours a year, guaranteed: private and small-group sessions, booked when his time is free. In between, you practice what he showed you, and you can join every group class.

Already have a place, or want your own room?

That is fine. The price is the same: you pay for the training.

Is there a private room, or a room for two?

No private rooms. The stay is a shared men's or women's dorm. Want your own room? Book one nearby; the price stays the same.

Can I bring my family or kids?

The dorms are not set up for families to stay together. Book your own place nearby, and the kids can train at NinjaGym Kids while you are in the program.
