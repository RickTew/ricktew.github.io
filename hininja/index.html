How WinJitsu came about

Rick took the old-school approach and moved to the mountains of Thailand (Pai) to write from his experience as a martial arts instructor, motivational speaker and traveler, and to formulate the 5-system foundation of WinJitsu.

In the martial arts, we have learned that there is a mind-body connection. It is why Rick wrote the book series WinJitsu, The Mental Martial Art. In modern times, with our challenges being more mental than physical, your mind is most likely what needs to be managed and trained.

The system

Rick Tew's Martial Science.

RTMS for short: the art you train here. A martial science, not a style: Modern American Ninjitsu crossed with mixed martial arts, built to teach movement, not moves.

Read the book freeRick's Philosophy and System, 20 pages, PDF. Know the art before you arrive.
Ninjitsu or ninjutsu? Jutsu or jitsu?

A jutsu is a method: the shared form, the drill, what a teacher hands down. A jitsu is a truth: the method made true to you, through use, repetition and your own natural strength.

An easy way to remember it: the U in jutsu is YOU, the method handed to you. The I in jitsu is I, ME: my own truth.

So ninjutsu is the ninja method as it is handed down, and Ninjitsu is that method made your own. Rick's system spells it Ninjitsu; most people spell it ninjutsu. WinJitsu is Win plus Jitsu: the winning truth.

What is RTMS?

Rick Tew's Martial Science (RTMS) is not just another martial art. RTMS is geared towards the enhancement of mind, body and spirit, and it is a lifestyle. We focus on developing a strong character, self-esteem, life enhancing goals, self-confidence, self-discipline, physical conditioning and building knowledge through the martial arts experience.

Why a martial science, not a martial art?

A typical martial art has already decided what makes up that style: when the student arrives, the instructor teaches the package and molds the student to it. The Martial Science develops around the student instead. In Rick's words: "It is my belief that the man makes the art and not the other way around." The student IS the style.

Five ranks, nine areas

There are 5 ranking levels to instructor status. Each level includes two skills from 9 areas of focus: stances, rolls, falls, strikes, kicks, body movement, knowledge (the mind), combat and self-defense strategies, and weapons. What the logo means

The logo has three parts. The yin yang in the background stands for balance: "Just as it rests in the background of our logo, it should rest in the background of your life." The triangle is mind, body and spirit: the left side is the mind, where all our knowledge and skill is given birth; the right side is the body and physical skills; the bottom is the spirit of all that we are and strive to become. Around them, the name: Rick Tew's Martial Science.

Questions

Do I need experience?

No. It is built for beginners. Trained before? Welcome too. Everyone trains at their level.

Am I too old?

No. There is no upper age limit: the relaxed plan is for everyone, and every drill has an easier version. Tell me about an old injury before you come.

Got an injury, or not very fit?

Tell me before you come. Every drill has an easier version.

I am 16 or 17. Can I come on my own?

Yes, with a parent's signature on the papers: the enrolment, a travel consent letter and a medical consent. Ask about the papers in the question form before you book.

When can I start?

Any day from 15 January 2027: classes run all year, Monday to Friday. The next group starts 22 January 2027. At checkout you add your arrival date.

Can I come for one week, or stay longer?

One week is half the price, $1,111: book one week. Longer: 3 weeks is $3,222, and a belt in the belt program is $3,997 for 2 months. Instructor is all five belts in 1 year, and learning to teach, $8,994: reserve a spot. Moving here? Mastery is all five belts over 2 years, $15,988.

How much time with Rick do I get in Mastery?

200 hours a year, guaranteed: private and small-group sessions, booked when his time is free. In between, you practice what he showed you, and you can join every group class.

Already have a place, or want your own room?

That is fine. The price is the same: you pay for the training.

Is there a private room, or a room for two?

No private rooms. The stay is a shared men's or women's dorm. Want your own room? Book one nearby; the price stays the same.

Can I bring my family or kids?

The dorms are not set up for families to stay together. Book your own place nearby, and the kids can train at NinjaGym Kids while you are in the program.
