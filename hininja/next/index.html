FAQ

Questions

Do I need experience?

No. It is built for beginners. Trained before? Welcome too. Everyone trains at their level.

Who teaches the classes?

Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week, mornings and evenings in turn. On the other days you practice with Guides: people who trained here before you and are a step or two ahead.

Got an injury, or not very fit?

Tell me before you come. Every drill has an easier version.

I am 16 or 17. Can I come on my own?

Yes, with a parent's signature on the papers: the enrolment, a travel consent letter and a medical consent. Write to me before you book.

When can I start?

Any day: classes run all year, Monday to Friday. The next group starts 22 January 2027. At checkout you add your arrival date.

Can I come for one week, or stay longer?

One week is half the price, $1,111: write to me first. Longer is $1,000 for each extra week, so 3 weeks is $3,222.

Is there a private room, or a room for two?

No private rooms. The stay is a shared men's or women's dorm. Want your own room? Book one nearby; the price stays the same.

Can I bring my family or kids?

The dorms are not set up for families to stay together. Book your own place nearby, and the kids can train at NinjaGym Kids while you are in the program.
