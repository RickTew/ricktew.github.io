01 // The file

The martial artist who turned movement into a science.

Rick Tew ran away to California at eight. He fell for ninjitsu at fourteen, was on television at fifteen, started teaching at sixteen, and opened his first dojo at nineteen. At twenty-one he moved to Europe to build his own system, and has taught since across Holland, Spain, California, Thailand, Latin America and the Philippines. He is a Tony Robbins Certified Strategic Intervention Coach, has been featured in Black Belt magazine, and has spoken at HP, TU Delft and the University of Rotterdam.

02 // The method

Movement, not moves.

RTMS is a martial science, not a style: Modern American Ninjitsu crossed with MMA, built so the system changes daily and the student stays self-powered. WinJitsu turns the same discipline on the mind, to beat the negative Ninja of fear, doubt and stress. Life is an obstacle course. This is how you learn to run it.

“Get a Black Belt in what you do.”
