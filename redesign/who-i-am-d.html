The short version. I have spent my life teaching people how to move, and how to win.

I ran away to California at eight years old. The Boy Scouts gave me the rest, goal systems, the outdoors, and my first taste of leading people.

I fell in love with ninjitsu at fourteen. By fifteen I was on television for it. At sixteen I was teaching. At nineteen I opened my first dojo.

At twenty one I moved to Europe to build RTMS. Six plus years based in Holland, then Spain, then the world, California, Thailand, Latin America, the Philippines. Along the way I built martialartsupply.com into a real business and founded NinjaGym in Thailand.

The method

RTMS

The martial science

Rick Tew's Martial Science is a science, not a style. Modern American Ninjitsu plus MMA, five ranking levels to instructor. It teaches movement, not moves. The student is self powered. The system changes daily.

WinJitsu

The mental martial art

Win plus Jitsu equals the True Art of Winning. It trains the mind to beat the negative Ninja, fear, doubt, negativity, stress, and laziness. Life is an obstacle course. WinJitsu is how you run it.
