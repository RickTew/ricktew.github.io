The story

Rick ran away to California at age 8. His Boy Scouts background gave him goal systems, the outdoors, and an early sense of leadership.

He fell in love with ninjitsu at 14. At 15 he was on television for his martial arts skills. He started teaching at 16 and opened his first dojo at 19.

At 21 he moved to Europe to develop RTMS. He was based in Holland for more than 6 years, then later in Spain. Over the years he has taught across Holland, Spain, California, Thailand, Latin America, and the Philippines. Today he teaches globally.

Rick is a Tony Robbins Certified Strategic Intervention Coach through the Robbins-Madanes Training Center. His work has been featured in Black Belt magazine, KO Karate, Fighting Spirit Zendokan, and Fighting Sports. He has spoken at HP, Dutch Art and Design, the Humaniversity, TU Delft, and the University of Rotterdam. He also created martialartsupply.com and grew it into a business.

The method

RTMS, Rick Tew's Martial Science

A martial science, not a style. It combines Modern American Ninjitsu with MMA and runs 5 ranking levels to instructor.

The idea is movement, not moves. The student is self-powered, and the system changes daily so it never goes stale.

WinJitsu, the mental martial art

"Win" plus "Jitsu" equals the True Art of Winning.

WinJitsu trains the mind to beat the negative Ninja: fear, doubt, negativity, stress, and laziness. The core belief is simple. Life is an obstacle course.

"Just because you have a job doesn't mean you have a life."
