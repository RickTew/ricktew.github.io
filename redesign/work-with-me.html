03 · When

Honest timelines. Fast starts, real finishes.

↑ Map

AI makes the first version shockingly fast. It does not make the last ten percent free, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something. The pattern from my own projects:

A day or two
A working app, landing page, or prototype you can click through and show your team. People react to something real instead of debating a spec.
1 to 2 weeks
A polished tool or site, ready for real people: accounts, content, mobile-friendly, deployed.
1 to 3 months
A full product with backend, payments, and edge cases handled. Most of the work hides here.
Ongoing
The mode that works best: I embed with your team like a part-time technical partner, shipping every week. This is what I do now for the NEWNEI team.
