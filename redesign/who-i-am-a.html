01 Presenting history

One life, read the way he teaches a body: movement first, then meaning, back to the root.

RTMS teaches movement, not moves. So does this file. Here is the arc that made the method, in his own order.

age 8USA

At eight, Rick Tew ran away to California, chasing a bigger world than the one he was handed. The Boy Scouts gave him his first taste of goal systems, the outdoors and leadership, the raw material of nearly everything he built later.

at 14The hook

At fourteen he fell in love with ninjitsu.

Not the costume, the craft: the idea that a person could train the body and the mind as one instrument. At fifteen he was on television for his martial arts skills. By sixteen he was already teaching.

at 19First dojo

By nineteen he had opened his first dojo. While other people were still deciding what to do with their lives, Rick was already handing his to students. Teaching was not a job he found. It was the one he built.

at 21Europe / RTMS

At twenty-one he moved to Europe to build his own system. Over six years based in Holland, and later in Spain, he developed RTMS, Rick Tew's Martial Science: an open, multi-art approach, Modern American Ninjitsu crossed with MMA, that teaches movement, not moves, so the system changes daily and the student stays self-powered.

the mindWinJitsu

Out of the training came a second art, a mental one. WinJitsu, the True Art of Winning. Where RTMS trains the body, WinJitsu trains the mind to beat its own negative Ninja: the fear, doubt, stress and laziness that stop most people. He wrote it down as a five-book series, one book per finger of the hand.

todayNinjaGym

Today Rick teaches globally: Holland, Spain, California, Thailand, Latin America, the Philippines. He runs NinjaGym in Samui, leads live-in camps and BLAST training tours, and coaches as a Tony Robbins Certified Strategic Intervention Coach. The mission has not changed since the first dojo: help you get a Black Belt in what you do.

03 The method // principle

RTMSprinciple
“Get a Black Belt in what you do.”

Rick Tew, in his own words

RTMS is a martial science, not a style. It teaches movement, not moves, so the system stays alive and the student is self-powered, not a copy of the master. WinJitsu turns that same discipline on the mind. Life is an obstacle course. This is how you learn to run it.
