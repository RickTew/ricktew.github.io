Rick Tew's NinjaGym® Samui Thailand

Martial Arts Fitness, climbing, parkour and ninja kids programs
Join or sign in at ninjagym.com Hours, prices and questions

Rick Tew's NinjaGym Samui Thailand is on the Ring road (near Starbucks) inside the Big C mall on the ground floor right next to the entrance.

Open every day, 10am to 7pm, weekends too. Book ahead and we can open from 9am and stay open until 9pm. Kids aged 3 to 9 drop in any time we are open, and a session is 55 minutes. The Half Day camp runs 10am to 2pm, and the Full Day is any 6 hours, usually 10am to 4pm; both are drop-off only. Adult programs are 8am or 8pm, by advance booking only.

Today's prices, and the answer to almost anything a parent asks, are on ninjagym.com/faq.

What you do at Rick Tew's Ninjagym

Sign up in the app before you come, so check-in at the desk is quick.

It takes about 2 minutes. One account covers the whole family, and you get your NinjaGym membership and a 4-digit PIN to check in. We take cash and PromptPay only, no credit cards.

Sign up at ninjagym.com/join

Gift cards are at the front desk, or order one in the shop and pick it up at NinjaGym.

Birthdays and group events start at 2pm or later, every day, for up to 20 guests.

Book a birthday at ninjagym.com/birthdays or a group event at ninjagym.com/private. Both pages show today's hourly rates and the refundable deposit.

Need more time or activities? Get 2 hours with the Game & Train combo or book 1-to-1 with a Guide
