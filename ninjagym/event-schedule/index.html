Event Schedule

Open every day, 10am to 7pm, weekends too. Book ahead and we can open from 9am and stay open until 9pm.

Birthdays and group events

Parties and group events start at 2pm or later, any day, and run 1 to 4 hours, for up to 20 guests. Book a birthday at ninjagym.com/birthdays or a group event at ninjagym.com/private. Both pages show today's hourly rates and the refundable deposit.

School outings

For registered schools, on weekdays: arrive from 9am and finish by 2pm. Book at ninjagym.com/schools.
