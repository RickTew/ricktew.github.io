## Answer these, in character

1. Before scrolling: what is this, who is it for, what is it asking me to do?
   How long did that take?
2. THE COLOUR. The page uses a strong red (buttons, headings, accents) on white.
   How does the red make you feel, honestly: energetic, urgent, aggressive, warning,
   cheap, martial, Asian, warm, nothing? Does it make you trust the page more or
   less? Would the same page in a calm blue feel better, worse or the same for YOU,
   and why? Be specific about where the red bothers you or works (buttons,
   headlines, the price card, the dark form at the bottom).
3. THE OFFER. In your own words to a friend: what is the training like (hard?
   relaxed? how many hours?), what is included, what does it cost, who is it for?
   Does it feel like a relaxed holiday with some training, or a hardcore camp?
   Which pictures or words gave you that feeling?
4. The pictures: do they look current or old? Do they match what the words say?
5. Would you buy, write first, or leave? What would you need to see to buy?
6. Which page fits you better, this one or the Gooffy version, and why?
