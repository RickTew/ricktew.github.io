# Head-to-head, 21 Oct 2026: Priya, the holiday traveller (priya-beginner)

Started on ricktew.com/hininja/ on an iPhone 13, then both pages on a laptop. No video on ricktew.com/hininja/, just a still of Rick jump-kicking over a bay.

## Verdict in one line

/hininja/: it reads like a calm, grown-up holiday and tells me I will not get hurt or put in a fight, but it never shows me the gym or my bed, so I would write before paying.

## First 10 seconds

- **ricktew.com/hininja/:** two weeks on Koh Samui training with Rick Tew, about 2 hours a day, "Not crazy serious like the camps of the old days." For holiday people like me. It wants me to press Enrol. Real training, I think; "ninja" made me smile (kids' party?). The "HI Ninja door / Take the AI Ninja door" bar confused me: why AI on a holiday page?
- **ricktew.com/gns/:** a cartoon ninja, "SHHT!", "Zero seriousness." First thought: a stag do or a gamer hostel for 22-year-old lads. It wants Enroll Now or a quiz. Gimmick, until I scrolled.

## Legit or gimmick

- **ricktew.com/hininja/: LEGIT**, decided late, at "What's included" and the FAQ ("Not a fight camp. No sparring unless you ask."). Held back by no photo of the gym, other students or the hostel; Rick with a microphone does not show me a class.
- **ricktew.com/gns/: UNSURE.** The training is real: the photo grid of adults, men and women, on red mats is the most convincing thing on either page. But worth it for me? The cartoon, "ShhT", the gaming dojo and the free T-shirt say not my kind of holiday.

## The same thing?

Yes, early: ricktew.com/hininja/ has "Our Gooffy version" in the hero, and ricktew.com/gns/ repeats the $2,222, the four 2s and the day word for word. A little confusing: ricktew.com/hininja/ also says "The goofy is the whole point. Every session is silly", so is the sensible page just different paint on the same silly classes?

## The price as I understood it

$2,222 (about £1,700, I worked it out myself) for 2 weeks: training, a bunk in a shared hostel dorm (women's dorm on request) and "simple, hearty" meals, number per day not given. 8am to 10am Monday to Friday plus an optional 2-hour evening, so 2 to 4 hours a day, weekends free. Flights and airport transfer not mentioned, so I assume not included. Dates: "All sales are final; your dates can move to another two weeks, within a year of buying." No refund, but I can shift (once? more?) within a year. For a bunk bed at 35 it feels steep, and "Skipping the dorm does not change the price" stings.

## My own question

"Will I get hurt or shouted at, and where exactly do I sleep?"

- **Hurt:** ricktew.com/hininja/ answers it well: the hero ("relaxed"), "Do I need experience?" ("built for beginners ... Not a fight camp") and "Got an injury, or not very fit?" ("Every drill has an easier version"). ricktew.com/gns/ has no injury question.
- **Shouted at:** neither says it outright; "relaxed" and "silly" imply it.
- **Where I sleep:** neither answers well. "A small hostel nearby": no name, photo, distance, bathroom, air conditioning or private room.
- **Flights cancelled, injured in week 1, camp cancelled:** not answered anywhere.

## Which page I would book from

**Vote: /hininja/.** "It's the one that sounds like a proper relaxed holiday and actually answers whether I'll get hurt." I would forward ricktew.com/hininja/ to my yoga friend.

## What would make the other page win

ricktew.com/gns/ would need to open on its photo grid of real adults training instead of the cartoon "SHHT!" ninja, and drop "ShhT" from headings ("A Day in the ShhT" reads like swearing).
