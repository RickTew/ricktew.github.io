## The one change

Give the one-desk start a price and put it where the inbox is first mentioned. The quiz, the "One ninja, one job" card and the FAQ all say the right first step for someone like me is one ninja on the inbox, and the quiz result even says $2,222 "does not add up yet" for me and points at "one desk on its own, The Front Desk". Then it stops. One line, "Just the inbox? The Front Desk on its own is $X to build and $Y a month, and it grows into a Dojo when you are ready", on that card and in the quiz result, is the difference between Dana closing the tab and Dana writing "yes, that one". If a fixed price is not possible, a "from" figure or a range does the same job; "priced in writing first" with no number reads as "more than you can afford".
