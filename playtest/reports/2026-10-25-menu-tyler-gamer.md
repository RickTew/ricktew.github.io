# Ninja Menu round, 25 Oct 2026: Tyler (gamer, beginner, phone, Instagram ad)

## Verdict in one line

The skill picker is the most fun thing on the page, but a plain text first screen almost lost me and the Book step felt like an unfinished form, so I'd write first, not buy.

## First 10 seconds

Big black text: "Two week intro to martial arts by Rick Tew". No picture, no ninja, nothing gamer. I don't know who Rick Tew is, so his name in a yellow box means nothing to me. "Sensei Seminar" shows up before I know what it is. I got: Koh Samui, beginners, "Take the quiz". I'd press the red button, but only because the quiz tiles peeked in under it. With just the headline I'd have scrolled on. "Quiz" made me expect "which ninja are you". It's a menu, which turned out better.

## The quiz

Fun, not a chore. Five picks is right. Straight to Ninja Tools: Sword, Blinding Powder ("the ninja's metsubishi", smoke bomb, sold), then Stealthing ("hidden in plain sight", hello Sekiro), Climbing, Forward Roll. The one-liners were the best writing on the page and helped me choose.

Confusing: I read the belt colours as rarity tiers. I picked red and blue belt things as a total beginner and nothing said whether I'll actually learn them in two weeks. Natural Reactions, Combat Reality, Kip and Tonfa meant nothing. After each pick the screen stays on ten cards and my Ninja Week box sits below them all, so I never saw my plan fill unless I scrolled.

## The moment to book

"Your Ninja Week is ready", then the stays: 2 weeks $2,222, 3 weeks $3,222, 1 month $3,997, a belt (2 months) $7,994. Under my picks: a Week 2 row of empty dashed circles and "5 of 10 picked". My QA brain read that as an unfinished form. Book sat below it, off screen. "Book my two weeks" showed the stay picker again, then "Add another week for only $1,000" and two more price buttons, then name and email, then "Save my plan and book, $2,222". Same prices three times before the form. "Only $1,000" put me off: that's half of what's left of my trip money. The month didn't tempt me. I did know what happens next: plan to Rick, checkout, arrival date, all sales final.

## The program as I understood it

- Sensei Seminar: morning class, 8 to 10, Monday to Friday, mixed from everyone's picks. Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week, "Guides" (earlier students) the rest.
- Hours: 2 a day, 4 with the review class.
- Review class: optional, 8 to 10 pm, practise the morning.
- WinJitsu: Rick's "mental martial art", coaching on focus and goals. Included, plus a month of the app. The books start at orange belt, so not mine (guessed).
- Sleep: a 6-bed bunk dorm at NerdHostel, a gamer hostel across the street, no added cost. Food: "simple meals if you need a little boost" (guessed: some meals, not all).
- Belt program: 2 months per belt, $7,994, yellow first.

## Make it pop

**The one change:** make the first screen sell the build. A real photo of people training, "Build your ninja loadout: pick 5 skills" instead of "Take the quiz", and one line under it: "Stay at a gamer hostel: Switch on 75-inch screens, Xbox Game Pass". Hooked in 3 seconds, not 15.

1. **Technique lists:** a bar stuck to the bottom, "2 of 5 picked · See my week", so I see the plan fill.
2. **Ready screen:** hide empty Week 2 circles until I pick 3 weeks or more, put Book right under the stays, and say plainly what a beginner gets from a red belt pick.
3. **Page order:** NerdHostel, the dorm photo and the Gaming Zone right after the quiz, Rick's face near the top. They're my reasons to go and sit 60% down.

**Cut:** "only" before $1,000; the second stay picker on the Book screen; "New to AI? Rick can help with that too"; the ACE, MAK, MBS, NRG, CMT acronyms.

## Legit or gimmick

**UNSURE**, leaning legit. Pulled in by the "Inside NinjaGym on Koh Samui" photo grid (real people, real mats) and the dorm photo. Pulled back by: the headline sells "by Rick Tew" but the FAQ says 2 classes a week, Guides the rest; photos that look 10 years old; a 7:37 intro video of old footage; no recent student reviews; no group size; About says 30+ years, this page 25+.

## My own question

"Will I be the worst one in the room, and will anyone laugh?" Partly answered: "No sparring unless you ask", "Every drill has an easier version", "Everyone trains at their level". Not answered: how big the group is and who usually comes (ages, levels, first-timers like me). That's the part I need.
