Round seven. Headless Chromium, laptop viewport 1280 x 800, which suits Helen: she reads on a desktop. Started at https://ricktew.com/aininja/ and read all of it: 14,347 px, about 18 screens. ## Verdict in one line
Helen would write, but only to ask one question. By screen three she had the price and the plan for her note to the owner. Patient privacy is never answered plainly, and she will not say "safe near patient mail" until someone tells her in writing whether he signs a HIPAA business associate agreement.

The two sentences she would send the owner after this session:
> "One man, Rick Tew, sets up AI that drafts our inbox replies and handles bookings and reminders, and our front desk approves every send. It is $2,222 a month flat for any number of staff, after a free audit, cancel monthly. Not near patient mail yet: the page never mentions HIPAA or a business associate agreement, and the AI runs on his own accounts, so I have written to ask."
