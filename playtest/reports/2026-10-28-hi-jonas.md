# HI round, 28 Oct 2026: Jonas (28, German, remote tester, gamer at the Samui hostel)

## Verdict in one line

A real gym across the street from my hostel and a fun menu, but Book now shows me $15,988 first, bounces me back up the page, and the earliest start is 15 January 2027: I would walk over and ask, not book.

## First 10 seconds

Big headline "Martial Arts Introduction by Rick Tew", a yellow "FREE" bar, a red Book now, a cartoon girl in a ninja hood. No photo, no price, no dates. "HI Ninja"? Like "hello ninja"? Never explained. What: martial arts on Koh Samui. Who for: unclear. It wants me to book. I would not press Book now on a page I have not seen; I would scroll for a price and a photo.

## How it works, said back

1. Pick moves on the "Ninja Menu": 8 areas, about 10 moves each tagged by belt (I took 3 kicks, nunchaku, sword; blinding powder is on there), or one quick pick that fills a week.
2. Pick a stay: 2 weeks $2,222, 3 weeks $3,222, 2 months plus a yellow belt test $3,997. One week $1,111 by message. Instructor (1 year, $8,994) and Mastery (2 years, $15,988) by message.
3. Name, email, a friend's name. The plan goes to Rick, then a checkout where I add my arrival date. All sales final; dates can move within a year.
4. Arrive any day from 15 January 2027. Free airport pick-up. Dorm bed at NerdHostel across the street; no discount if I already have a bed.
5. Monday to Friday 8 to 10 am "Sensei Seminar" from the whole group's picks, optional 8 to 10 pm review, afternoons and weekends free.
6. Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week; "Guides" (former students) the rest.

Guessed: whether my picks get taught if the group picks others; what week 2 holds (the plan shows Week 1 only); group size; meals ("simple meals if you need a little boost"?); whether WinJitsu coaching is a session or just the app.

What would have cleared it, near the top: "2 weeks = 10 mornings, at least 4 classes with Rick, start any day from 15 Jan 2027, $2,222."

"Ask Kuno" would be worse: Kuno means nothing to me. "Ask the Ninja" says what it is.

## Legit or gimmick
