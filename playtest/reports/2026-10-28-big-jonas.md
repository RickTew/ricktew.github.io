# Jonas, 28, German software tester, staying at NerdHostel (phone first, then laptop)

## Verdict in one line

The menu is fun and the dorm photo is literally my dorm, so I believe it is real, but I am on Samui now and the first date is January, there is no way to try one class, and "12 spots left" plus "all sales final" on $2,222 smells like a funnel.

## First 10 seconds

A martial arts course on Koh Samui by Rick Tew. A real-looking class photo, but from an old camera. Who it is for: not clear yet. What it wants: "Book now". I pressed it expecting dates and a price; it scrolled me to a quiz ("Pick 5 tactics"). Surprise, but the icons pulled me in. "HI NINJA": hi as in hello? Hawaii? No idea. The round Kuno face covered "Koh Samui" in the first sentence.

## Would I book

Ask first, then probably leave. If I booked: 2 weeks, $2,222, and only with a hostel friend free in November ($1,111 each sounds human).

What stops me:
1. "Start any day from 15 January 2027." I am across the street right now. "Classes run all year" but not now?
2. No trial. I asked the chat, it had no answer. $2,222 non-refundable without seeing one class is a no.
3. Already have my NerdHostel bed? "The price is the same." So I pay for a bed I do not use.

To book I need one class I can try or watch this week, and the 2 weeks in hours (I worked out 10 mornings of 2 hours myself).

## The photos

Mostly real. Convinced me: the NerdHostel bunk bed photo (my room, the colour foam floor), the newer gym with blue mats and climbing wall, the guy in a white tee with sticks (that is me), the ninjas on scooters with swords (I would post that), the testimonial faces.

Off: most class photos look 10 or more years old (low resolution, black uniforms, wooden hall). Is it still like this? Rick at the red bag is pixelated. The Grand Canyon jump: real or Photoshop? On the phone the Rick banner is cut to "ICK TEW" and "FOUNDER OF NINJ".

Someone like me: yes, guys in their 20s, a few women, one older man.

The changing boxes: I mostly scrolled past before they changed. Kicks was an empty grey box on first look, and two caught mid-fade looked like a blurry double image. Neutral to slightly annoying.

## The bonuses

WinJitsu, in my words: Rick's mindset and self-help side. Some coaching with him on focus and goals, a five-book series that only starts at the orange belt (so not for my 2 weeks), and a free month of an app. Pocket Dojo: a phone app with every lesson per belt, foot diagrams, a "mark as practiced" counter and a box to ask Rick, so you keep training at home.

Both say "with every stay", so they read as bonuses. Pocket Dojo made it a bit more worth it (a progress counter is gamer brain). WinJitsu did not: "beat the self-doubt that holds you back" and the passion quote read like a motivational speaker, and the books I do not even get. Pocket Dojo ends on a big red "See the Home Study Program" button: an ad for another product inside my stay.

## Welcome

Yes, more than I expected. "GAMERS AND NERDS: Nerdy ninja conversations and a game room." "Not a fight camp. No sparring unless you ask. Not a boot camp." The Gaming Zone (race car simulator, Switch on 75-inch screens, Xbox with Game Pass) sold me more than the kicks. 8 to 10, day free, weekends free fits a remote job.

No: the dark Mastery band ("Move to Thailand", $15,988), four phone screens in the middle; the price cards start at $15,988 and mine is last, saying only "The introduction."

## The chat

Asked: "I'm staying at NerdHostel right now. Can I try one class before I book?" Answer: "I do not have that one written down, and I would rather say so than guess. Send it to me, then check your inbox." "trial class?": same. "can I start this week": "Any day from 15 January 2027". Honest and fast, but no help. I would not email; I would walk across the street.

## Legit or gimmick

Real person, real gym: decided at the bunk bed photo ("across the street from NinjaGym") and the student quotes. Worth $2,222 for about 20 hours of group class: not sure. The urgency stack ("Only 12 spots left for 2027", "save $2,000", "All sales are final") and "first in the martial arts industry" pulled me toward gimmick.

**Vote: UNSURE**
