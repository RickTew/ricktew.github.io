# Maria, arriving from a Google search (round six) - 2026-10-01

Phone session, headless Chromium as an iPhone 13 (390 x 664, touch). Started at
https://ricktew.com/aininja/done-for-you/ as the persona file says, then followed
its links into https://ricktew.com/aininja/ and read that page top to bottom
(43 phone screens). Used the chat box (7 questions), the quiz (all 12) and the
mailbox (filled, never sent). The two mail endpoints were blocked and logged
before any page loaded: zero requests reached them, and the send button was
never pressed. About 20 minutes in character.

## Verdict in one line
I would write a short note tonight, but because the quiz told me straight that
the $2,222 plan does not add up for a company my size yet, not because of the
long page: the search page had already told me everything by its seventh screen,
and the main page mostly said it again and then lost me in the owner's life story.

## Moments of friction (the core of the report)

1. **Search page, top bar "Which ninja? / Take the HI Ninja door".** Expected:
   the page I tapped on Google. Got: a black bar asking which ninja I want, with
   a red door. I do not want a ninja, I want my evenings. **Cosmetic.**
2. **Search page, first screen: "Your team checks the work and presses send."**
   Expected: an answer to "is this one more app I have to run?" Got: mostly yes,
   "I build it, host it and manage", then this line, which sounds like my
   evenings again: checking what the AI wrote. It took the chat box to make me
   feel better about it (see 17). **Annoyance.**
3. **Search page, "I manage all of it": "It runs on my stack".** Stack of what?
   Skipped it. **Cosmetic.**
4. **Search page, "Why a person runs it": "White belt first ... propose only."**
   Belts, on a page for business owners. I got the meaning from the second line,
   not the first. **Cosmetic.**
5. **Search page, "Two plans": $222 then $99, or $2,222 a month.** Nothing in
   between. The small one is a website, which I do not need; the big one is
   about what a part time office person costs me. I pay maybe $60 a month for my
   two apps now. **Annoyance** (it is the question I carried for the rest of the session).
6. **Search page, every button ("Tell me the job", "Tell me where your hours
   go", "Drop it in my mailbox").** All three drop me at the very bottom of a
   different, much longer page, and the blue button in the header changes name
   from "Tell me the job" to "I want this Tew!!". I had to think about what a
   "Tew" is. The landing itself worked: the subject was already picked for me
   ("I want my hours back: the inbox, the bookings, the admin"). **Annoyance.**
7. **/aininja/ top screen.** I knew in about ten seconds that this is a man who
   builds AI helpers for businesses, but because I came from the search page, I
   knew that already. One thing jarred against the search page: "either you run
   it yourself or I run it with you". The search page said he manages it. Which
   one? (No change proposed to the top screen, per the brief.) **Annoyance.**
8. **/aininja/, just under the top: the photo caption "Rick Tew, AI version".**
   The whole search page sold me "managed by a real person", and the first
   picture of the person says it is the AI version. I squinted: is he real?
   This sits in the hero, so I only report it. **Annoyance.**
9. **/aininja/, "Five words · five boxes".** An empty rectangle on a line, a
   Replay button, and a vocabulary lesson (Dojo, ninja, seat, belt, sensei). I
   did not ask to learn words. Scrolled past. **Annoyance.**
10. **/aininja/, "Five months. Solutions and goals. One reason."** The first
    line got me ("The one reason: I wanted my evenings back") and the gym admin
    paragraph was my life. Then came the books he wrote in his twenties, a board
    game from his childhood, a console called TewBeDo with buttons named BE, DO,
    INTEL, TRIP, and a martial arts method app. This is where I would have
    closed the tab in real life. **Would-quit** (at "The books I wrote in my 20s
    came off the shelf", about 13 screens down).
11. **/aininja/, "Just a few of the apps I've made recently".** Two screens of
    sliding phones: dungeon games, robots and cyborgs, a hostel. Nothing that
    looks like a cleaning company. **Annoyance.**
12. **/aininja/, "The website is not the machine. It is the handle."**
    Waterline, engine, handle: three pictures for one idea, explaining technology
    at me. One line landed hard: "you are the system, and the business cannot
    run a day without you." That is me. The rest I skimmed. **Annoyance.**
13. **/aininja/, "The solutions. Every one ran on me first."** Eleven rows. The
    first three (Front Desk, Money Desk, Booking Desk) I had already read on the
    search page as "Replies drafted", "Payment mail filed", "Bookings without the
    back and forth". The rest (Ad Room, Publishing House, Video Room, Colleagues
    by Email, The Notebook, The Quiz Funnel) are not my business. Then "The
    helpings", another list. **Annoyance.**
14. **/aininja/, "Somebody told you to get Claudeforce?"** Nobody told me. I
    have never heard of it, or of seats, CRMs and metered jobs. Three screens of
    someone else's bill. Worse, at the bottom: "Marlow sells one full-time
    assistant plus an AI trained on your business: $2,650 a month". For $428 more
    than the Dojo I get a person doing the work all day? That line pushed me
    toward the other company. **Annoyance.**
15. **Quiz, question 11 "Once it exists, who runs it day to day?"** I did not
    know if pressing send on drafts counts as "running it". I picked "Nobody here
    has the spare capacity. That is the problem", because that is true, and the
    result then said "me running the whole day for you is a custom price". I
    did not ask for that; I meant I am too busy to babysit. **Annoyance.**
16. **Quiz result: "a smaller start first, one desk scoped in writing or R2
    Hosting".** Honest, and I liked it, but the smaller start has no number. R2
    is a website. "One desk scoped in writing" means I still do not know what I
    would pay. Also "Not a business number at all, and I respect that more than
    the other three": the other three what? **Annoyance.**
17. **Chat box, "how is this different from the AI apps I already pay for".**
    It gave me the same "Is there a real person behind this" answer it had given
    one question earlier. The best answer to that question is on the search
    page ("Most AI tools hand you the tool and leave the rest with you"), and the
    box did not use it. **Annoyance.**
18. **Chat box, "clients text me all day to move their cleaning to another day.
    can it handle that".** It answered a WhatsApp, LINE and Facebook question:
    "Email first ... Another channel is a build, not a switch." So my biggest
    time sink, texts, is not covered out of the box. LINE means nothing to me.
    Honest, but it took the wind out of me. **Annoyance** (close to would-quit).
19. **Mailbox.** The sticky "Take the quiz / Ask" bar sits over the bottom of
    every screen and covers half the "Drop it in my mailbox" button and the note
    under it until I scroll. The field I was typing in got a red outline, which
    read like an error. The subject is cut off on my phone ("the inbox, the bc").
    After the quiz the message box was a long printout ending in "Seat matched:
    none", which I would not want to send without understanding. **Cosmetic.**

## Moments that worked
- **Search page headline:** "Done-for-you AI for small business owners. Managed
  by a real person." Exactly what I typed into Google. I stayed.
- **Search page, "Why a person runs it":** "Most AI tools hand you the tool and
  leave the rest with you: setting it up, learning it, keeping up with which
  model is best this month. That is still your time and your thought." That is
  my spring and summer in two sentences. This is the moment I believed "real
  person" was not a slogan.
- **Search page, "Nothing to log into":** "No dashboard, no AI account, no key,
  no meter. When something needs changing, you tell me." The opposite of my two apps.
- **Search page, "Bookings without the back and forth":** "Reminders,
  reschedules and cancels handle themselves." My "can you come Thursday
  instead" messages, named.
- **The search page is short (9 screens) and has prices on it.** I had my four
  answers before I left it.
- **The plan cards say what is not included:** "Not in the price: ad spend, and
  software your business already pays for on its own." No surprise later.
- **FAQ "What happens if you disappear?"** "You lose the window, not the room."
  My customer list stays mine. That calmed a fear I had not said out loud.
- **Chat box "is there a real person behind this": "Yes, me."** Two words, like
  a person would say it.
- **Chat box tip on checking drafts:** "Reading ten drafts takes minutes;
  writing ten replies took your evening." That turned "babysitting" into
  something I could live with. It is not on either page.
- **Chat box honest miss on payroll:** "I do not have that one, and I would
  rather say so than guess." I trusted the rest more after that.
- **Quiz, question 2 has "a cleaner" in it.** Someone thought of me.
- **Quiz result:** "by your own numbers that is about $1,300 a month back
  against $2,222 a month. It does not add up yet, and I would rather say so than
  sell it." Nobody selling software has ever told me that. This is why I would write.
- **"I want this Tew" landing:** the mailbox came up with the subject already
  chosen and "My company:" and "What I would love off my plate:" waiting for me.
  I only had to fill the blanks.

## The one change
Say what the owner's own day looks like, in one sentence, the same way
everywhere the plans appear, starting with the search page cards. Right now I
read four versions: "I build it, host it and manage" (search page), "either you
run it yourself or I run it with you" (top of the main page), "I help you run it;
I am not on your staff" (the cards), and "who runs it day to day?" in the quiz,
which sent me to a custom price I did not ask for. The chat box already has the
sentence that settled it for me, and it is on neither page: "Reading ten drafts
takes minutes; writing ten replies took your evening." For someone tired of
babysitting AI apps, that line answers "is this another app I have to run?"
better than anything on the plan cards.
