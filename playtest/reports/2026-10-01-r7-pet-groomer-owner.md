# Carla, the pet groomer - 2026-10-01 (round seven)

Read on a phone: headless Chromium as an iPhone 13 (390 x 664, touch), live page https://ricktew.com/aininja/ . The page is about 29 phone screens long. About 20 minutes in character, at night, tired, after a day of grooming.

Safety: both mail endpoints (`functions/v1/ricktew-contact` and `ricktew-intake`) were blocked in every script before the page loaded. Zero attempts to reach either were logged, and the two scripts that pressed "I want this Tew" and the quiz's "Send me the breakdown" logged zero non-GET requests of any kind. "Drop it in my mailbox" was never pressed. Only visible, labelled fields were typed into, with a made-up name and carla@example.test.

## Verdict in one line
Real-life Carla closes the tab around screen 7 without ever seeing the word "reminders"; reading on to the end, the folded Booking Desk row and the honest quiz result make her willing to write one short line, but she still does not know whether the $222 plan stops no-shows.

## Moments of friction (the core of the report)

1. **Top bar, "← Which ninja? / Take the HI Ninja door →"** (screen 0). Expected: the name of whatever my sister sent me. Got: a riddle about ninjas and doors before anything else. Felt: "is this a game?" **Cosmetic.**

2. **Top screen, "I'm not a marketing expert. I'm the person behind your AI ninjas."** (screen 0, not judged for change). It took me about 20 seconds of reading the paragraph under it to get "he builds something for a business and runs it with you, so the owner gets time back." What the something is, and whether it does bookings, only came 3 screens later at the prices, about 2 minutes in. "AI ninjas" did not tell me it was about admin or bookings. **Annoyance.**

3. **Under the photo, "RICK TEW, AI VERSION"** (screen 1). Expected: a real photo of the man. Got: told it is an AI picture. Felt: "so is he even real?" A small trust wobble right where I was deciding whether to trust him. **Annoyance.**

4. **Number strip, "24+ years a ninja / 22+ years a nerd / 6,242+ rounds of changes since March / 100+ solutions built"** (screens 1 to 2). None of these mean anything for my salon. "Rounds of changes" sounds like a laundry setting. The first number also counts up as I scroll (I saw 24+, then 26+), which made me look twice. A whole phone screen of numbers. **Cosmetic.**

5. **The two price cards in "Hate tech, tired of it..."** (screens 3 to 4). The $222 card says "Your website or small app, with the forms that bring work in." I have a Facebook page, not a website, and I do not know if I need one. Nowhere does it say no-shows, reminders or texts. Then "$2,222 a month, flat": my stomach dropped. That is more than my rent. I kept going only because the $222 card was there. **Annoyance** (close to would-quit on the $2,222).

6. **"Basic plan · R2 Hosting"** (screen 3). "R2" sounds like the Star Wars robot, and "hosting" is a computer word. "R2 is me, Rick Tew" is cute, but I did not need the name explained; I needed to know what it does for a booking business. **Cosmetic.**

7. **"Your team checks it and presses send"** and **"I help you run it; I do not run it for you"** (screens 4 and 21). My sister said "he does the admin so you can just groom." This reads like my groomers, with wet dogs on the table, now have to check drafts and press send. And "Want the whole day run for you? That is a custom price, and we talk first" sounds like "even more money." **Annoyance.**

8. **"Five words · five boxes" storyboard and "Read the five in one paragraph"** (screens 6 to 7). A ninja at a desk, then "A seat is a job a few ninjas fill together. Marketing, the books, customer care: one job, one number it is judged on." This is a vocabulary class. It is the first thing that sounded complicated, and it runs straight into the next headline "Five months. Solutions and goals. One reason." **Would-quit: this is where I would have closed the tab.** (The rotating sign said "The salon's Dojo" for a moment, which I liked, but it did not save it.)

9. **Gym numbers, "30,000+ sessions since opening / 850+ kids served each month / 5,000+ app bookings / 1,000+ members"** and later **"6+ hours of teaching / 1 human press / 1,900+ rounds of changes / 3 businesses"** (screens 8 and 10). His gym is much bigger than my salon. This made me feel the thing is built for big places. **Annoyance.**

10. **"Just a few of the apps I've made recently"** (screens 11 to 12). Video games (Dungeon Hole, AstroHold), a hostel, a sliding row of app icons. Felt: "he makes video games? I came here about my no-shows." **Annoyance.**

11. **"The website is not the machine. It is the handle."** (screens 12 to 16). The spa example started well ("my customers would rather call, we can do this by hand" is exactly me). Then: engine, waterline, handle, "Shop two is turn-key", and finally "The website is the ten percent above the water. Buy only that and you bought a brochure." But the $222 card up top said the website is "all the system they ever needed" for a lot of small businesses. So is the plan I can afford a brochure? 19. **The mailbox after pressing "I want this Tew" on the $222 card**. It scrolled me down and the "Your name" box lit up with a **red** outline before I had typed anything. Red means "you did something wrong." It was only the cursor. **Cosmetic.**

20. **Phone screen space**. The sticky "DIGITAL DOJO / I want this Tew!!" bar on top and the "Take the quiz / Ask / ×" bar at the bottom cover about a quarter of my phone screen the whole way down. **Cosmetic.**

## Moments that worked

- **"Hate tech, tired of it, or no time for it. You still want it to work."** and the ticks under it, especially "You would rather be with a customer than at a screen." I felt seen. That is me with a dog on the table.
- **"You press one button and I think about the rest: the tech, the AI, which model is best this month, the thing that broke at 2am. None of it is yours to carry."** Warm, not talking down.
- **The $222 card said plainly**: "Built, hosted and kept running by me. Nothing for you to log into." and "First month included. Cancel anytime." A number a small shop can look at without fainting.
- **"The 'I want this Tew' button writes your pick into my mailbox at the bottom of this page. You add a line and send it."** This answered "is the button safe?" before I pressed it. Keep it.
- **"How it works", four steps.** Short, plain, no ninjas.
- **The gym paragraph**: "Bookings, memberships, belts, birthday parties, questions, payments. All of it landed on me." and "I geek out on the machine, you get your evenings back." He has had my problem.
- **The spa example**: "my customers would rather call, we can do this by hand" and "Even when every booking still comes by phone, it lands in the engine instead of a notebook." My clients call and text. This line was for me.
- **The Booking Desk, once opened**: "People book you without an account or email ping-pong. Reminders, reschedules and cancels handle themselves." The best sentence on the page for a salon, and it is folded.
- **"You run the business. I run the ninjas that do your admin."** (the card above the quiz). The clearest one line on the whole page.
- **The quiz result's honesty**: "by your own numbers that is about $500 a month back against $2,222 a month. It does not add up yet, and I would rather say so than sell it." That made me trust him more than anything else on the page. Then it offered two smaller starts.
- **The chat box on Facebook messages**: "Another channel is a build, not a switch... tell me which one and what they usually ask, and I scope it in writing." Not the answer I wanted, but honest.
- **"Will an AI talk to my customers without me knowing? Never."** Calmed a fear I had.
- **The mailbox prefill**: "I want this Tew: R2 Hosting, $222 to build, then $99 a month." plus three blanks (What I do / What eats the hours right now / When I would like it running). Easy to fill on a phone.

## The moment I knew

**Section:** "Hate tech, tired of it, or no time for it. You still want it to work.", at the end of the Your Dojo card and the line under it ("The 'I want this Tew' button writes your pick into my mailbox... You add a line and send it."), **about 4 to 5 screens down of 29**, roughly 3 minutes in.

At that moment I could say all four:
- **What he sells:** a website or small app with booking forms that he builds and runs, or (bigger) the admin handled by AI he manages.
- **Who does the computer work:** him ("Built, hosted and kept running by me. Nothing for you to log into.").
- **What it costs a business like mine:** $222 then $99 a month, or $2,222 a month.
- **What to do next:** press "I want this Tew", it fills the mailbox, add a line, send.

**Still missing at that moment:** whether either plan does appointment reminders or anything about no-shows (the only "Reminders" is folded inside The Booking Desk at screen 17, and it does not say which plan it belongs to); whether it works with texts and Facebook messages, which is how my clients reach me (the chat box says email first, other channels are a separate build); and what "one desk on its own" would cost a small shop, since the page only says "quoted in writing after the free audit".

**Where I would have left in real life:** screen 7, at the "Five words · five boxes" storyboard ("A seat is a job a few ninjas fill together") running into "Five months. Solutions and goals. One reason." I would have texted my sister "too expensive and it is for gyms." I kept reading to the end for this report.

## Did I understand what is sold, what it costs, and what happens next?

- **What is sold: Yes, mostly.** "Your website or small app, with the forms that bring work in. Built, hosted and kept running by me." and "All of that, plus your admin handled as a service. I manage the AI ninjas that draft every reply and file every payment." **Gap:** nothing in the open says it handles reminders or no-shows; that is folded in The Booking Desk at screen 17.

## Would I write?

**Yes, but only because I read to the end.** The real me would have closed the tab at screen 7. What tipped it: the Booking Desk's "Reminders, reschedules and cancels handle themselves", the quiz saying the big plan does not add up for me instead of pushing it, and knowing the button only fills the box and does not send by itself.

First line I would actually type into the mailbox (after pressing "I want this Tew" on the $222 card):

> Hi, I have a dog grooming salon with 3 groomers. Can the $222 one text my clients a reminder the day before so they stop no-showing, and answer the "can you fit us in" messages that come at night on text and Facebook?

## The one change

Say "reminders" and "no-shows" in the open, next to the $222 card, in plain words, and say which plan they come with. Right now the only answer to my real question, "Reminders, reschedules and cancels handle themselves", is folded inside The Booking Desk 17 screens down, and the chat box answers "can it stop no shows" with "I do not have that one."
