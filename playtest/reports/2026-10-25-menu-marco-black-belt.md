# Ninja Menu round, 25 Oct 2026: Marco, the returning black belt

iPhone first, then a 1280 x 800 laptop; a quick look at /about/ to check who Rick is.

## Verdict in one line

The technique menu reads like a real syllabus and pulled me in, but "Sensei Seminar" turns out to mean Rick teaches 2 classes a week, and at $1,111 a week that is where I stopped and decided to write first.

## First 10 seconds

"Two week intro to martial arts by Rick Tew", Koh Samui, relaxed, not a boot camp. For beginners, I thought. "Sensei Seminar" sits in the first paragraph with no meaning yet. I saw "Take the quiz" and would start it: eight real-sounding areas right under the headline. On the laptop it sits beside the headline, better.

## The quiz

Fine, close to fun. Five picks is right. The one-line explanations helped, and the belt dots let me pick at my level. I picked Combat Reality, Elbow (blue), Stick / Tonfa, Pivot Shuffle Shift & Step, Triangulation. Nothing stopped blue and red belt picks in a beginner intro; will I actually get them?

What made me roll my eyes: "Sweep the air with sensitivity, the way wind sweeps the earth", "sink into shadow and out of sight", "Stealthing: the art of invisibility", Blinding Powder, Superman Roll, Stunt Falls "for the camera". Body Movement is cartwheels, kip-ups and handsprings, and Rolls and Falls worry my knee; nothing marks what is knee-friendly.

## The moment to book

Yes, I knew: "Now pick how long you stay". 2 weeks $2,222 (preselected), 3 weeks $3,222, 1 month $3,997, A belt, 2 months $7,994. "Add a week for only $1,000" put me off: "only" is a salesman word, and $1,000 is two Muay Thai weeks with a room. The month ($775 for week four) is the better deal, but the total scared me more than it tempted me. Book opened a plain form (name, email, "Save my plan and book, $2,222") saying checkout and arrival date come next. Clear.

## The program as I understood it

- **Sensei Seminar:** morning class, 8 to 10, Monday to Friday, built from everyone's picks. I assumed Rick teaches it. The FAQ says Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week; Guides (former students "a step or two ahead") take the rest.
- **Hours:** 2 a day, plus an optional 2-hour evening review. The ready box says "up to two hours", which sounds like less.
- **Review class:** 8 to 10 pm, optional, practise the morning, or go instead of the morning.
- **WinJitsu:** Rick's "mental martial art", coaching on focus, goals and self-doubt, included. Guessed: how many sessions, one-on-one or group.
- **Housing:** shared 6-bunk men's or women's dorm at NerdHostel across the street, no extra cost. My own room is fine, same price. Food: guessed. "Simple meals if you need a little boost" could mean included or not.
- **Belt program:** 2 months a belt, yellow to red, $7,994 each, yellow syllabus listed. Guessed: is there a test, what comes after red, does it count anywhere else, does my karate count.

## Legit or gimmick

The menu sold me first: fourteen angles of attack with a checking hand, knife "mindset and survival before any disarm", inside and outside line, jam block and run. That is real. The intro video at 25 seconds shows Rick in a gi teaching an arm control to a full room: real. Then I fell back: his own system, no teacher or rank named anywhere (I checked /about/), photos that look ten years old, and Rick on 2 classes a week.

**Vote: UNSURE.** Legit man, unsure the two weeks are worth $2,222.
