# Nok, the spa owner on Koh Samui (English second language) - 2026-10-01 (round seven)

Read on a phone: headless Chromium as an iPhone 13 (390 x 664, touch), starting at https://ricktew.com/aininja/. About 22 minutes in character. The page is about 29 screens long on this phone. Both mail endpoints (ricktew-contact, ricktew-intake) were blocked before every page load and every attempt was set to be logged: no request to either one was ever made, so nothing got through. The "Drop it in my mailbox" button was never pressed. I typed only into the visible, labelled fields (Your name, Your email, the message box, the chat box).

## Verdict in one line
I would write to him, but only after I message my friend on LINE first. The price is in plain numbers early, he runs a gym here on Samui, and he built a spa app on Samui. But nothing on the page says if it works with LINE and WhatsApp, where all my tourist bookings come in. The chat box knows (it is email today, LINE or WhatsApp is extra work he quotes first), but it gave me that answer only after I typed the single word "LINE".

## Moments of friction (the core of the report)

1. **Top screen, "I'm not a marketing expert. I'm the person behind your AI ninjas."** I expected the word "booking", because my friend told me he built her booking system. I read it two times, slowly. After about 40 seconds I knew he builds something with AI for a business and runs it with you. I did not know what the thing is (a website? an app? booking?) or the price. "AI ninjas" made me think of a game. I did not leave, because my friend sent the link. Severity: annoyance. (Reported only. I do not propose changes to the top screen.)
2. **The "I want this Tew!!" button (top bar, hero, and almost every card).** I do not know what "Tew" means here. Is it his name? Is it "I want this to..."? I was afraid it was a buy-now button that takes my card. I did not press it until I read, two screens later: "The 'I want this Tew' button writes your pick into my mailbox at the bottom of this page." Severity: annoyance.
3. **Stats under the photo: "26+ years a ninja", "22+ years a nerd", "6,242+ rounds of changes since March", "100+ solutions built".** "Rounds of changes"? I do not know what this counts. I skimmed. Severity: cosmetic.
4. **The two price cards in "Is this you?"** "$222 to build, then $99 a month" is clear. Good. Then "$2,222 a month, flat" for the Full plan. That is about 80,000 baht every month, more than five of my therapists' pay. So I thought: the big one is not for me. Then I was not sure which plan has the booking system. The small card says "a contact or booking form". Later the page has "The Booking Desk" with no price. Is that the $2,222 one? I never found out for sure. Severity: annoyance.
5. **The paragraph under the cards: "Want the whole day run for you? That is a custom price, and we talk first. Smaller than that? One desk on its own, without the monthly plan, is quoted in writing after the free audit."** Too many ideas in two sentences. "Desk", "audit", "custom", "quoted in writing". I read it three times. Severity: annoyance.
6. **"I stick to text on purpose: everything we agree is on the record. A call is a custom step."** Text is good for me, I live on LINE. But "on the record" is an idiom, and "A call is a custom step" sounds like a call costs extra money. He is on Samui: can I meet him? The page does not say. Severity: annoyance.
7. **The sticky bar at the bottom ("Take the quiz", "Ask", x) and the top bar.** On my phone the header with the big "I want this Tew!!" button and the bottom bar together cover about one third of the screen, the whole way down. I read slowly, so I scroll a little at a time, and the text hides behind them often. Severity: annoyance.
