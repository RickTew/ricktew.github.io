Chloe: "two of us for $2,222 if we book in November? That's actually doable", then the page never says whether a couple counts, where a couple sleeps, or whether that's US or Aussie dollars, so we'd write before paying.

## First 10 seconds
Chloe, from Instagram: "Martial Arts / Ninja Course / by Rick Tew", Koh Samui, a bright gym photo with a bloke, a woman and Rick in guard. So: a martial arts course on Samui, for anyone, and it wants me to Book Now. I scrolled instead, hit the yellow "Attend with a friend for free if you book in November" and screenshotted it for Liam. Liam's first word was "Ninja?" with a smirk, but he kept reading.

## Would I book
Ask first, by the question box. We'd pick 2 weeks ($2,222), with Liam as the "friend", arriving late January (the "Start any day from 15 January 2027" line fits us).
What stops us:
- Nothing says a partner counts as the friend. The chat couldn't confirm it.
- "No private rooms. The stay is a shared men's or women's dorm." We'd book our own room nearby, fine, but there's no hint where or how far.
- No currency anywhere. To us "$" means AUD. If it's USD, it's roughly a third more in Aussie money.
- "All sales are final" on a two-person trip. "Your dates can move, within a year" helps.

## The photos
They made it real: real classes, many places, many years, not stock.
- Convinced Chloe: the woman laughing on the red mat in a partner drill, the woman kicking the pad, the woman doing the splits in the stretching row, the women in the outdoor stance line, and Sibel K.'s circle photo. Someone like me, not a room of blokes.
- Convinced Liam: the gi groundwork shot (side control) in the self-defense box. "Okay, he can roll."
- Off: the Grand Canyon splits in budgie smugglers got a laugh, with him or at him, unsure. The purple old-film leap looks ancient. The "ICK TEW ... FOUNDER OF NINJ" banner is cut off.

## The bonuses
- WinJitsu, in Chloe's words: Rick's mindset and goal-setting coaching, plus his five self-help books and a free month of an app. Labelled "with every stay", so it reads as included. But it runs four phone screens of "beat the self-doubt", "motivational speaker", "5-system foundation" and a passion quote. Liam: "this is where it goes a bit guru." And "They join your belts from orange on" made us wonder if a two-weeker gets the books at all. It made the page feel more salesy, not more worth it.
- Pocket Dojo: an app with the belt lessons, to keep practising at home. Makes sense, adds a little value. But its red "See the Home Study Program" button looked like something extra to buy, and we couldn't tell if WinJitsu.com and Pocket Dojo are the same app.
