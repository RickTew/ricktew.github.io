## Legit or gimmick

- **/gns/: LEGIT.** I decided at the belt tabs: L1 to L5, each with named stances, rolls, falls, kicks and a book. A skill tree: someone built a real system. The sealed file sealed it: press list, 25+ years, and the RTMS 2000 tape really played.

## The same thing?

Yes, within three screens of /hininja/: the same opening sentence, the same Four 2s, the same $2,222, and then "Its name is Gooffy Ninja ShhT!!" with a link back. It did not confuse me, it reassured me. What jarred: the calm page says "Not crazy serious", then "Every session is silly".

## The price as I understood it

$2,222 for 2 weeks, "all in": hostel bunk, simple meals, a 2-hour taught class 8am to 10am Monday to Friday (Rick or a NinjaGym Guide; Rick at least 2 a week), plus an optional 2-hour evening review. So 10 taught classes, 20 hours, up to 40 with evenings. Extra weeks are $1,000 each. Arrival date at checkout. All sales final; dates can move to another two weeks within a year.

Fair for me? Not quite. I already pay for a room in Lamai. Both FAQs say skipping the dorm does not change the price; I get a 1-on-1 with Rick instead. That is about $110 per taught hour if I skip evenings. Value-minded me needs a train-only price or a clearer reason the 1-on-1 is worth a bunk.
