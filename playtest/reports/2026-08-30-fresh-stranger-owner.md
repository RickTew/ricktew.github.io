## Moments that worked

- "The app now has 1,000+ members. It was not meant to be a product. It was meant to give me some time back. It did." Plain and believable.
- The gym photos: the birthday banner, the kid on the tyres, the boy mid-jump. A real gym, not stock.
- "None of my customers want to use an app." Fine. The engine was never for them. And "Most people do not want an app. They want the job done." Both true of me and said before I could object.
- The pair "You do it" and "We do it": one glance and I know which I am.
- The prices are on the page, in big type, with "Monthly subscription. Cancel anytime, effective at the end of the paid month." I did not have to write to find out. The "Not included" lists are honest.
- "Honest line: if three of you need a contact list and nothing else, buy their Starter at $25 a seat. I will not beat that." A man telling me when not to buy from him is a man I would listen to.
- "If you only read one section, read this one": hook, problem, solution, proof, ask, five short lines. This is the whole page in the length I would actually read, and it is the block I would forward to my husband. It should be at the top.
- The FAQ answer "Do I need to understand AI to use this? No, and that is the point."
- The quiz answers are written in my language: "Paper, honestly", "Honestly, I do not enjoy computers. I want the result, not the tool", "Nobody here has the spare capacity. That is the problem". Every question had an option that was me. "No email needed" was kept.
- The quiz shows its maths ("hours times a 60% draft-and-file share times the hourly value you gave") and the result was computed from what I tapped, not from what he wanted me to buy. The number was honest even though it hurt his sale.
- The chat answer to "can it answer my emails for me": "the AI drafts from what you wrote, it never sends on its own", and the tip "Collect the ten replies you send most. That is the training set, and it is already written." I could do that tonight.
- The chat answer to "I hate computers honestly, is this still for me": "The staff at my gym and my spa never touch the builder tools: they see a draft, a booking, a payment to confirm, and press."
- The mailbox is short, does not open my mail app, and the quiz filled it in so I did not have to compose anything.
