## By type: who prefers which page

- **/gns/ wins every fun-nerd and traveller type, 7 of 7:** the gamers (Tyler, Jonas),
  the skeptic nerd (Ken), the nomad (Ravi), the backpacker (Sofie), the group (Marcus)
  and the couple (Liam and Chloe). Their reason is the same: it shows the proof and
  it talks like them ("train by day, game by night"). It is also the link they would
  forward: Discord, the hostel chat, the D&D group, the nomad group.
- **/hininja/ wins every careful type, 5 of 5:** the woman alone (Aisha), the careful
  holiday beginner (Priya), the parent (Dana), the 54-year-old (Henrik) and the man
  with the bad knee (Marco). Their reason is tone: "Zero seriousness" read as

  my kid" (Dana). Every one of the five still said the proof that convinced them was

- Nationality made no pattern. Age and worry did: under 40 and curious went /gns/;
  safety, injury, a child or age went /hininja/.

## What all twelve agreed on

1. **The proof is on /gns/ only.** 12 of 12 named the "Inside NinjaGym on Koh Samui:
   red mats, real training" photo grid as the moment it looked real (women training
   in it mattered to Aisha, Sofie, Chloe, Priya). 10 of 12 named a tape in the sealed
   file (the RTMS 2000 tape or "Meet the sensei"). 11 of 12 said /hininja/ shows no
   class, no student and no gym: two photos, both of Rick alone.
2. **The black bar on top of /hininja/ ("You are in the HI Ninja door / Take the AI
   Ninja door") confused 12 of 12.** "Is this an AI company?", "wrong ad?". An ad
   visitor's first line on /hininja/ is about AI. /gns/ has no bar.
3. **They would all write before paying,** so the ask path is the real conversion
   path. On /hininja/ the form is on the page (8 of 12 said they would write from
   it). On /gns/, the footer Contact lands on the blue AI Ninja mailbox ("Tell me
   where your hours go", "goes to my Ninja Agent"): 8 of 12 hit it, 6 rated it
   would-quit ("another company", "an AI sales funnel", "scam alarm").
4. **"Every session is silly" and "The goofy is the whole point" on /hininja/
   contradicted its own calm tone** for 11 of 12 ("so is this one silly or not?").
5. **"Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week" plus unnamed Guides:** 11 of 12 asked
   who the Guides are, or what rank. /hininja/ saying it up front earned trust;
   /gns/ having it only inside "not a morning person?" read as bait (Tyler, Marco,
   Jonas, Liam).
