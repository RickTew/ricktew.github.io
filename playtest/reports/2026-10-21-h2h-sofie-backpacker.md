## Verdict in one line

/gns/: the goofy one showed me real people training and a tape of the guy; the plain one only told me it was legit.

## First 10 seconds

- **ricktew.com/hininja/**: Clear. Two weeks on Koh Samui, live-in ninja training, about 2 hours a day, "not crazy serious". Wants me to press Enrol. Real but thin: big words, white page, one photo of a man jump-kicking over a bay. The black top bar ("You are in the HI Ninja door, take the AI Ninja door") confused me. Is this an AI thing?
- **ricktew.com/gns/**: Cartoon ninja, "SHHT!", "Zero seriousness", comic font. First thought: party hostel with a gimmick. To a Dutch reader "ShhT" reads as "shit". I almost closed it.

## Legit or gimmick

- **ricktew.com/hininja/: UNSURE.** Specific (8 to 10am, Big C Mall, dates, refund rule) and honest in tone. But it shows almost nothing: two photos, both of Rick. The kick photo has limestone cliffs and a twin bay; it looked like the Phi Phi viewpoint to me, not Samui. The other is Rick with a microphone, like a seminar speaker. No gym, no students, no dorm, no food, no video. "The training is legit" is what you say when you can't show it.
- **ricktew.com/gns/: LEGIT** (the training is real). I changed my mind on screen 3: the "Inside NinjaGym" collage, adults on red mats, women included. Then "Declassify the file": the "Meet the sensei" tape played, and the Asia file had Rick in a real Thai gym and "Jungle training, beach kicks". That is the story I want to take home. Worth the money is a separate question.

## The same thing?

Yes, fast. ricktew.com/hininja/'s "What it is" says "Its name is Gooffy Ninja ShhT!!" and links the Gooffy version; the price box is word for word the same. Not confusing, but the plain page also says "The goofy is the whole point. Every session is silly", so which tone is real?

## The price as I understood it

$2,222 for 2 weeks, all in: hostel dorm bunk (women's dorm on request), "simple, hearty" meals (how many a day?), a 2-hour taught class Monday to Friday at 8am, an optional 2-hour evening review, weekends free. About 10 real classes; Rick teaches at least 2 a week, a Guide the rest. Extra weeks $1,000. All sales final; dates can move within a year.

That is about $159 a day against my $50. A Muay Thai week with a bed costs a fraction; my PADI on Koh Tao was a few hundred. Not fair for me unless clearly special, and no page proves that (no reviews, no past students). "Move within a year" is useless to someone flying home in three months.
