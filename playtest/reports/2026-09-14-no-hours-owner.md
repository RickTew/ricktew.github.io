## Moments that worked

  and "You have no time to learn it, and none to fix it." That line kept me
  reading.
- "You press one button and I think about the rest: the tech, the AI, which
  model is best this month, the thing that broke at 2am. None of it is yours
  to carry." The best sentence on the page for me. It should be higher.
- "Most people do not want an app. They want the job done."
- The engine list: "Every booking, recorded", "Invoices that write
  themselves", "The books, filing as you go", "Inventory that counts itself",
  "Staff, named on every transaction". The first list I stopped and read.
- The Booking Desk row: "People book you without an account or email
  ping-pong. Reminders, reschedules and cancels handle themselves." Concrete.
- The price card's "NOT INCLUDED" list, especially "Running your daily work
  for you. I help you run it; I am not on your staff." Blunt, and it corrected
  my friend.
- "Do I have to manage any of the tech?" "No. ... no dashboard, no console, no
  certificate to renew, no password to keep somewhere safe, and no bug that is
  yours to fix. When something needs changing, you tell me." That is exactly
  what my card reader, my tablet and my rota app do not give me.
- The quiz result in hours: "about 10 a week", and then "It does not add up
  yet, and I would rather say so than sell it." Nobody selling me software has
  ever said that. It is why I would write to him at all.
- "Monthly subscription. Cancel anytime, effective at the end of the paid
  month."
- The two small cards in the Claudeforce band: "$222 to build, then $99 a
  month" and "$2,222 a month, flat" with "Want the whole day run for you? That
  is a custom price, and we talk first." The whole offer in six lines.
- The mailbox: four fields, no email app, and "The thing you do every week
  that you wish you did not have to." as the box's prompt.
- The chat box did not bluff on four of seven questions. "I do not have that
  one, and I would rather say so than guess." I believed the misses.
