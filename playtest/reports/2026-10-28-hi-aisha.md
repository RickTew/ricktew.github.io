# HI round 28 Oct 2026: Aisha (31, Toronto, solo traveller, wants real self-defense)

## Verdict in one line
The training looks real and gentle enough for me, but the page sells "by Rick Tew" and then tells me Rick teaches 2 classes a week, and nobody says how much hands-on contact there is with strangers, so I would write first and not book.

## First 10 seconds
Phone. "Martial Arts Introduction by Rick Tew", a relaxed group workshop on Koh Samui, a red Book now, a yellow friend line, a cartoon girl's face. It is martial arts on holiday for beginners, and it wants me to book. I would not press Book now yet; I would scroll to see a real class and who teaches.

## How it works, said back
1. Pick 5 "tactics" from the Ninja Menu. 2. Choose a stay: 2 weeks $2,222, 3 weeks $3,222, 2 months with a yellow belt $3,997 (Instructor and Mastery are for people moving there). 3. Book: name, email, friend's name, then a checkout where I add my arrival date; all sales final, dates can move within a year. 4. Arrive any day from 15 January 2027; free airport pick-up. 5. Day: 8 to 10am group workshop built from everyone's picks, free daytime, optional 8 to 10pm review class, weekends free. 6. Sleep: shared women's dorm at NerdHostel across the street, no extra cost, simple meals "if you need a little boost" (guess: some meals, not all).

Who teaches: Rick at least 2 classes a week, the rest "Guides, people who trained here before you." That surprised me after the headline. Guessed: what weeks 2 and 3 cover (I only filled Week 1), and whether "next group starts 22 January" means I wait if I arrive on the 15th.

What would clear it: one plain block on who teaches self-defense on Guide days, how partner drills work, and whether women train together.

## Book now
Expected: dates and price for the two-week intro, or a checkout. Got: "Pick your stay" with $15,988 Mastery first. I scrolled to the 2 weeks card, pressed Pick and book, and was thrown back up to the menu. Then Self-defense tile, 5 picks, "Your Ninja Week is ready", 3 weeks, "Book my 3 weeks", then the form.
Count: 10 taps and about 7 screens to the form, with a checkout still after it. The up and down jump made me feel lost. I did not want payment sooner: a straight "pay now" at $3,222, all sales final, before my questions are answered, would worry me.

## The title
"Introduction" says beginners, but sounds like a one-evening taster, not two weeks at $2,222. My ranking: Course (structured, a qualified teacher), Training (sounds real), Workshop (fine but short), Introduction (vague), Retreat (yoga and smoothies, not self-defense), Camp (boot camp or kids), Holiday (makes safety sound like a joke). My own: "Martial Arts and Self-Defense Course for Beginners, Koh Samui". I would google "self defense course for women Thailand", "beginner self defense classes Koh Samui", "learn self defense while travelling Thailand".

## The photos
Yes, I noticed: the only face on my first phone screen is a cartoon. A real photo higher up, of a woman training with an instructor, would change my first read from "cute ninja holiday" to "real class". Real for me: the eight-photo grid (women doing pad work), Sibel K.'s and Gabriela Z.'s photos, the dorm photo, the strip of Rick demonstrating holds in a blue gi. Off: Rick at a desk with an old CRT monitor (feels 2005), the flying kick and the swords logo (a bit macho), and the grid looks low resolution and old. I do not know what Rick looks like now, and there is no photo of the Guides who teach most days.

## The chat button
Before opening: a cartoon girl in a yellow headband. I took it for a support bot or mascot, not Rick. On the laptop it says "Ask the Ninja". "Ask Kuno" alone would mean nothing to me; "Ask Kuno, Rick's assistant" would read better than "Ask the Ninja", which feels gimmicky. I asked "Are there other women in the group, and will I have to do partner drills with men I don't know?" It said it did not have that written down and offered to send it to Kuno. I liked the honesty. My follow-ups ("Is it safe for a woman travelling alone?", "Who teaches self-defense?") got page lines about the menu and the dorm. It did not help.

## The friend line
Clear: book in November, a friend comes free. The box lower down explains what it covers; on the first screen I did not know. "FREE" in capitals beside $2,222 makes me wonder if the price has room in it. I travel alone, so it does not move me.

## Legit or gimmick
Vote: **UNSURE.** I believe the training is real: the photo grid with women training, the two women's quotes, the press list and "Not a fight camp. No sparring unless you ask." I am not sure it is worth $3,222 for me, and that doubt arrived at the When card: "Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week; on the other days you practice with Guides." For self-defense I want someone who knows what they are doing, every day.

Write first, with the "Ask first" box. Good answers and I would book 3 weeks.
