# Dave, the owner who hates tech - 2026-10-01

Round five. Persona: Dave, 58, two-bay auto repair shop, three mechanics, wife on paper books. Read at the counter on a 1280 x 800 laptop, headless Chromium (Playwright), live page https://ricktew.com/aininja/ . About 20 minutes in character. The page measured 39 laptop screens top to bottom.

Safety: both mail endpoints (`ricktew-contact`, `ricktew-intake`) were blocked before every page load, with a log line for any attempt. Across all page loads the block log stayed empty: no request to either endpoint was attempted, so nothing was let through. The mailbox was filled and read, never sent. One tooling slip to own: on my first chat-box run the script typed my questions into the wrong field (a hidden one beside the mailbox, outside its form) and pressed Enter. Nothing was sent: the block log stayed empty and the browser was thrown away. The questions were then asked again in the real chat box.

## Verdict in one line
In real life Dave closes the tab at "Six Agentic Masters" (third strike, screen 21) without writing. He knew the offer by screen two, but nothing in the next 19 screens was about a garage, and the plan that fixes his phone and his paperwork ($2,222 a month) reads as too big for a two-bay shop. Made to read to the end, the quiz's "It does not add up yet, and I would rather say so than sell it" is the line that would have got him to write.

## Moments of friction (the core of the report)

1. **Top screen, "I'm not a marketing expert. I'm the person behind your AI ninjas."** I expected to learn what he does. I learned he is "behind" my "AI ninjas", and I don't have any. "I build the same for your business": the same what? After about 40 seconds of slow reading I could say "a man who does something with AI for businesses" and no more. The answer came two screens later, about two minutes in. "AI ninjas" is **strike one**. Felt: patient, a bit lost. **Annoyance.** (No change proposed. The brief keeps this screen as it is.)
2. **Stats strip under the top, "6,242+ ROUNDS OF CHANGES SINCE MARCH".** In a shop, 6,000 changes means it kept breaking. Is that good? Later the numbers were caught mid-count (24,740+ sliding up to 30,000+), so they look like they're still being made up as I watch. **Cosmetic.**
3. **"Is this you?" price cards, "BASIC PLAN · R2 HOSTING".** R2? Like the Star Wars robot? Nothing nearby says. The page explains it 22 screens later ("R2 is me, Rick Tew"). **Strike two** (see the strike notes under Words). **Annoyance.**
4. **Same cards: the gap between the two prices.** The $99 plan is a website. I don't need a website, I need the phone and the books off my wife. The plan that does that is $2,222 a month: close to a mechanic's wages for a month, on top of what I already pay. So the plan I can afford fixes a problem I don't have, and the plan that fixes my problem reads as made for a bigger outfit. The middle ("one desk on its own") only shows up 32 screens down in the FAQ, as "a custom job, quoted in writing". **Annoyance, close to would-quit.**
5. **"I want this Tew!!" (21 times on the page, plus the bar that follows you down).** It reads like a Buy Now button and never says what pressing it does. I don't press things that might sign me up for something. I never pressed it. **Annoyance.**
6. **Five words, five boxes, first caption: "A Dojo is the setup I build for one business. Yours, on my stack."** Stack? I read it as "his pile of gear" and let it go, because the same line says nothing for me to log into. The word comes back four more times, and nowhere says what it is. The boxes also play slowly (one picture every few seconds) and the example is a bakery, not a shop like mine. **Cosmetic** (near-strike).
7. **"What you see on social right now."** Billionaire thumbnails, a "$1,997" webinar, "The Side Hustle Summit". I don't watch that stuff, and for a minute this page looked like one of them. Why is it here, before I even know what he does for me? **Annoyance.**
8. **"Five months. Solutions and goals. One reason." (screens 5 to 12).** Seven screens about his gym, his books, his songs, his games, his console. Fine man, but this is his business, not mine. I skimmed it all except the gym owner buried in admin and the till. **Annoyance.**
9. **"TewBeDo: the console nobody sold, so I built it".** AGENTS, INTEL, TRIP buttons, "Tap the buttons; they are real." I don't tap. **Cosmetic.**

By the "Six Agentic Masters" I could not tell if these were new things to buy or the same ninjas in a new hat. **Annoyance, adding up.**
11. **"You run the business. I run the ninjas that do your admin." (screen 18).** The clearest sentence I read all day, and it shows up 18 screens down, then twice more. **Cosmetic** (it worked, but late and repeated).
12. **"The solutions. Every one ran on me first."** Eleven rows plus "The helpings". The first three (Front Desk, Money Desk, Booking Desk) are my whole problem. Then it's "Publishing House", "Video Room", "Quiz Funnel". This is a catalogue, and I'm not shopping a catalogue. **Annoyance.**
13. **"Six Agentic Masters. I run them. You hold the press."** Agentic is not a word I know. CMO, CTO, Chief of Staff, "On the mat". "You hold the press": I have a 20-ton press in bay two. **Strike three. Would-quit.** In real life Dave goes back to the bay here.
14. **"Your own Digital Dojo. Built by a sensei."** "Give every AI hire a 2015 job title and you get a 2015 org chart." No idea what that means. Belts, desks, white belt, propose only. **Annoyance.**
15. **The big price cards (second set, screen 24).** The order flipped: the cheap one was on the left at the top, now the expensive one is. I had to re-find which was which. "The audit first, free": to a shop owner, an audit is the taxman. Free audit is not a gift. **Annoyance.**

## Moments that worked
- **"Is this you?" checklist.** "You have tried an app, a tool, a 'system'. It is somewhere on your phone." Ha. Yes. It came close to telling me what I feel, but every line was right, so it landed instead of talking down.
- **"You press one button and I think about the rest ... None of it is yours to carry."** First read: a slogan. By the end I believed it, because the page kept backing it up: "A person checks it. Nothing goes out without a press.", the NOT INCLUDED lines, and the quiz admitting the math didn't work for me.
- **How it works, four steps.** "You tell me the job. My AI ninjas do the legwork ... A person checks it ... I manage all of it and report to you in writing." The clearest block on the page. That's what I'd tell my wife.
- **"Built, hosted and kept running by me. Nothing for you to log into."** That's the website-guy wound, answered in one line.
- **The till:** "The POS I bought charged $5 per staff member per month just so they could ring up a sale under their own name. So I built my own." Every shop owner has paid that fee and hated it.
- **"Another had run a factory on paper since 1998; front office and back, it became one system."** Finally, somebody else's business, and one older-school than mine.
- **"'None of my customers want to use an app.' Fine. The engine was never for them."** That's exactly what I'd have said, answered before I said it. The engine-and-body picture works for a mechanic.
- **NOT INCLUDED: "Running your daily work for you. I help you run it; I am not on your staff."** It goes against what my regular told me ("you never touch it"), but it's straight, and I trust straight.
- **"Monthly subscription. Cancel anytime."** No contract. Good.
- **"If three of you need a contact list and nothing else, buy their Starter at $25 a seat. I will not beat that, and I will not pretend to."** A salesman who sends you to the cheaper guy. Rare.
- **Quiz option "A trade. A garage, a plumber, a builder, a cleaner".** It named me. First time on the page.
- **Quiz result: "by your own numbers that is about $1,050 a month back against $2,222 a month. It does not add up yet, and I would rather say so than sell it."** The single most trusted line of the session. It then offered two smaller starts. That's the moment I'd have written.
- **"No testimonials yet, on purpose ... I will not invent a quote to fill a box."** Respect.
- **Chat box, "Do I have to learn anything":** "No, and that is the point. I do the computer work. You never log in, renew, fix or learn anything; when something needs changing, you tell me." Plainer than anything the page says on the same question. "I do the computer work" is the sentence I came for.
- **Chat box honest miss:** asked about car parts ordering, it said "I do not have that one, and I would rather say so than guess." Good. A person would say that.

## Did I understand what is sold, what it costs, and what happens next?
- **What is sold: Yes**, from screen two: "Your website or small app, with the forms that bring work in. Built, hosted and kept running by me." and "your admin handled as a service. I manage the AI ninjas that draft every reply and file every payment. Your team checks it and presses send." Gap: what that looks like in a garage (the "is my car ready?" calls, the estimates, parts) is never shown. The examples are a gym, a spa, a bakery and a factory.

- **What happens next: Mostly.** The mailbox note says an AI answers first within a minute and Rick follows up himself, usually within a day. The FAQ says the free audit comes first. Gap: the main button, "I want this Tew!!", never says what it does, so I didn't press it.
