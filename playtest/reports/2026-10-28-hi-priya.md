# Priya, the holiday traveller: /hininja/ "how it works" round, 28 Oct 2026

## Verdict in one line
A real teacher and a calm offer for beginners, but Book now sends me down and back up the page, and what I need before paying (who teaches most days, meals, what if my flights change) is scattered or missing, so I would write first, not book.

## First 10 seconds
Phone, evening. A beginner martial arts thing on Koh Samui, for beginners ("relaxed introduction"), and it wants me to press Book now. What I could not tell: one afternoon or a holiday? "Introduction" plus "workshop" sounded like a two-hour taster. No price, no length, no real photo, a cartoon girl in the corner. "HI Ninja" meant nothing (Hawaii?). I would have scrolled, not pressed Book now.

Guessed: meals ("simple meals if you need a little boost" reads as maybe); week 2 (I only filled week 1); group size; what "next group 22 January" means if I can arrive any day.

## The chat button
Before opening: a chatbot mascot. Not Rick: it is a young woman, Rick is a middle-aged man in every photo. On the laptop it says "Ask the Ninja"; on the phone it is only the face.
I asked: "What happens if my flights change and I can not come on my dates?" Answer: "I do not have that one written down..." and Ask Kuno. Honest, but the page already says dates can move, so it should know. "Are meals included?" got the "little boost" line, not a yes or no. Did it help? No.
"Ask Kuno" alone: worse, who is Kuno? "Ask Kuno, Rick's assistant" would beat "Ask the Ninja", which sounds like a gimmick.

## The friend line
Clear: bring a friend free. What it covers I only learned at the price section (same dates, same stay, training, dorm bed). Believable: half. A free second $2,222 place makes me think the price is padded, and "FREE" in capitals reads like a flash sale. It is October: do I wait until November to book? I would.

## Legit or gimmick
Rick and the training are real: photos, years of teaching, press names, quotes. Worth about £1,700 for 2 hours a morning, Rick twice a week, a bunk, meals unclear, all sales final? Not sure. "Only 12 spots left" and "save $2,000" made me trust it less.
Real: decided at the photo grid and the woman at the bag. Unsure: at the FAQ "Who teaches the classes?".
**Vote: UNSURE.**
