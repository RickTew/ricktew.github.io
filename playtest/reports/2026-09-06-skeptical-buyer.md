# Marco, the consultant who has been burned before - 2026-09-06

Round two. Desktop Chrome, fresh visitor (storage cleared, one reload). About 35 minutes in character. Read the whole page in order, the Terms of Service, the R2 Hosting page, asked the chat box eight questions, did all twelve quiz steps, filled the mailbox as Marco and did not send it.

## Verdict in one line
Marco would write, but he would not pay yet: the page is unusually honest about prices and about its own limits, which is why he trusts it enough to send a message, but the one thing he came for (a support desk that drafts replies, on its own) has no price anywhere, and the Terms of Service disagree with the price cards in five places, which is exactly the kind of thing that made him leave the last three vendors.
