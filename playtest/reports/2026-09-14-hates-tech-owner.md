## The one change

Put Dave's answer at the top of the page, above the fold, in the words the FAQ already has, and make the chat box find it. One block under the hero, before any ninja or Dojo: "Who does the computer work? Me. You never log in, renew, fix, or learn anything. If you ever want to see under the hood, I will show you. You never have to. Your team still presses send on the day's replies; if you want the day run for you too, that is a custom price and we talk first." Then teach the box the plain versions of that question ("who does the computer work", "do I have to learn anything", "what if you disappear"), because those are the three he typed and all three got "I do not have that one."
