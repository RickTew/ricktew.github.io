# Aisha, the cafe owner with a till full of apps - 2026-10-01

Round six, laptop (headless Chromium, 1280 x 800), https://ricktew.com/aininja/ as it stood on 1 Oct 2026. Page is 25 screens tall. About 20 minutes in character, after closing, glass of wine.

Safety: both mail endpoints (`functions/v1/ricktew-contact` and `ricktew-intake`) were blocked before every page load, and a POST listener ran on the mailbox script. Neither logged a single request in any run: nothing was let through. "Drop it in my mailbox" and "Send me the breakdown" were never pressed. The only fields typed into were the chat box, my name (Aisha) and a made-up address (aisha@example.test). The hidden "Leave this empty" field was never touched.

## Verdict in one line
In real life I would have closed the tab at the games chapter, about 8 screens down. The price was clear by screen 2, but nothing on the page or in the chat box told me whether this sits on top of my till, ordering and rota apps or replaces them. Having read to the end, I would send one short question, because the quiz told me straight that $2,222 does not add up for me yet, and that honesty earned a message.

## Moments of friction (the core of the report)

1. **Top screen** ("I'm not a marketing expert. I'm the person behind your AI ninjas."). I expected to learn what he does for a business. I got "the Dojo where my AI ninjas work. I build the same for your business". "AI" twice in the first two lines sets off my scepticism, and the caption "Rick Tew, AI version" under the photo made me wonder if the face is real. It took about 20 seconds to know he builds some AI setup and either I run it or he runs it with me. I did not know what it does or what it costs until I scrolled once, about a minute in. Felt: wary, but fair enough. **Cosmetic.** (No change proposed, per the brief.)

2. **The number strip under the top screen.** "6,242+ rounds of changes since March". A round of what? It means nothing to me. "30+ years a ninja" next to "25+ years a nerd" reads cute. **Cosmetic.**

3. **The two price cards** (in "Hate tech, tired of it..."). I jumped straight here, as I always do. Good: two flat prices. Problem: which one is me? "Basic plan · R2 Hosting" is a website or small app, and I already have a website and an ordering add-on. "Full plan · Your Dojo" at $2,222 a month is "your admin handled as a service". For reviews, supplier emails and rota swaps that is more than a part-time admin person. There is nothing in between for "just the inbox". "R2 is me, Rick Tew" made me laugh, but it is a strange name for a hosting plan. Felt: price is honest, fit is unclear. **Annoyance.**

4. **FAQ "Do I have to manage any of the tech?"** I opened it hoping for "does it plug into what I already pay for". It says "Both plans are built on my stack and stay there: the hosting, the database, the mail plumbing". Does that mean my till and ordering move onto his stack? The only hint is in the cost answer: "Not in the price: ad spend, software your business already pays for on its own". That implies I keep my software, but it never says what the ninjas connect to: my Google reviews? my supplier inbox? my rota app? This was my whole question. **Annoyance, close to would-quit.**

5. **Chat box, my first question** ("Does this sit on top of my till and online ordering, or replace them?"). It answered a different question, "Are there other costs? AI subscriptions, software?". I rephrased: "Will it work with the software I already use?" and got "Is there a real person behind this, or is it all AI?". The header says "Answers Rick wrote. No guessing." Two confident wrong answers to my key question make that line ring false. **Annoyance, trust hit.**

6. **Chat box, reviews** ("Can it reply to my Google reviews?"). It answered "Do you have testimonials or references?". It matched the word "reviews" and got the wrong meaning. If this is the same engine that would answer my customers, this is exactly what I am afraid of. **Annoyance.**

7. **"Then everything else at the gym went the same way"** (story, about 6.6 screens). "Then the till. The POS I bought charged $5 per staff member per month ... So I built my own." My alarm went off: is the pitch going to be "rip out your till"? Nothing nearby says "I do not touch your till." **Annoyance.**

8. **"Got an old idea in a drawer? So did I."** (story, about 7.8 screens). A board game, pixel art, TewBit Games, then songs, then books from his twenties, then his console, then WinJitsu. This is his life story and his hobbies. I came for cafe admin. **This is where I would have closed the tab in real life**, about 6 minutes in, price known, till question unanswered. **Would-quit.**

9. **"Just a few of the apps I've made recently"** (about 11.8 screens). A moving row of games, a hostel, a spa and "TEWBEDO, overseer access only". Pretty, and nothing in it is a cafe. **Annoyance.**

With the till story five screens earlier, this reads like he wants to be my till. Or does it mean the till I have is fine? I could not tell. **Annoyance (LOST).**

## Moments that worked

- **Prices on the second screen, flat, in plain dollars.** "$2,222 a month, flat" and "Any number of people". Twelve staff does not change the bill, and I did not have to hunt for it.
- **"Your team checks it and presses send."** One line, and I knew who does what.
- **"How it works", four boxes.** "You tell me the job. My AI ninjas do the legwork ... A person checks it. Nothing goes out without a press. I manage all of it and report to you in writing." This was the clearest thing on the page.
- **"Not in the price: ad spend, software your business already pays for on its own".** Straight, and it hints at the answer I wanted.
- **"What happens if you disappear? You lose the window, not the room."** My customer records and domain leave with me, and "I keep no copy". Good.
- **"Friends asked about AI, then asked me to do it for them."** "Now his support desk drafts a reply to every customer mail and a person presses send." That is my reviews and supplier mail in one sentence, and it is a real business.
- **The Front Desk row:** "An AI reads every support mail, day and night, and drafts the reply from your own written answers."
- **He says what he does not do.** "If you want someone doing your daily work all day, that is what they sell, and it is not what I sell." and "buy their Starter at $25 a seat. I will not beat that, and I will not pretend to." I respect that a lot.
- **The quiz result's honest sum.** "by your own numbers that is about $500 a month back against $2,222 a month. It does not add up yet, and I would rather say so than sell it." This was the single most convincing line on the page for me. It is why I would write at all.
- **"No email needed" was true.** The result showed without asking for my address.
- **Chat box honest misses.** "I do not have that one, and I would rather say so than guess" on the rota and on Square. Better than a made-up yes.
- **Chat box "What exactly is included in Your Dojo?"** A full answer with the "Not included" list, and the tip "Read the Not included lines first."
- **The mailbox tells me what happens next.** It will not open my mail app, an AI answers within a minute and says it is an AI, and Rick follows up within a day.

## The moment I knew

**"Hate tech, tired of it, or no time for it. You still want it to work.", at the "How it works" box, about 2.5 screens down, roughly a minute in.** By then I could say all four:
- What he sells: a website or small app he builds and hosts ($222 then $99), or the admin itself, drafted by his AI ninjas, as a monthly service.
- Who does the computer work: his AI ninjas draft and file, he manages them, my team presses send, and there is nothing for me to log into.
- What it costs a business like mine: $2,222 a month, flat, any number of people.
- What to do next: press "I want this Tew" and it writes into his mailbox, and the audit comes first and is free.

What was still missing at that moment:
- Does it sit on top of my till, ordering add-on, rota app and reviews inbox, or replace them? (Never answered, on the page or in the chat box.)
- Does it handle staff rota changes at all? (Not mentioned anywhere. The chat box said it does not know.)
- Which plan a cafe is: R2 is a website, which I have; Your Dojo feels heavy for reviews and supplier mail. There is no number for "just the Front Desk".
- What the free audit asks of me: how long, and in what form.

The 22 screens after that moment added one thing I needed (the quiz's honest sum) and two that confused me (the till story and the engine list).

## The one change

Say, once and plainly, what it plugs into. The closest the page gets is the cost answer's "Not in the price: ad spend, software your business already pays for on its own", which only implies that I keep my software. A cafe owner with a till, an ordering add-on, a rota app and a reviews inbox needs one sentence beside the Your Dojo card or in "The first questions": does it sit on top of the software I already pay for, or replace it? Right now the page argues the other way: the till story ("So I built my own"), the engine list ("Inventory that counts itself", "Staff, named on every transaction") and the quiz result ("it is the system") all suggest he would replace my till. The chat box could not answer it either: I asked three ways and got "other costs", "a real person" and "testimonials".
