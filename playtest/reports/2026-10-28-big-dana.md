# Big round, 28 Oct 2026: Dana (47, Austin, mom bringing her 16-year-old)

## Verdict in one line

The training looks real and the page says "16 and up, a parent signs the papers", but it never says who looks after my son, where the two of us sleep together, or whether anything runs over his winter break, so I would write, not book.

## First 10 seconds

"Martial Arts Ninja Course by Rick Tew", a man showing a move while students in black watch, "a go-at-your-own-pace program on Koh Samui Island, Thailand." A course in Thailand run by one named man; no age, price or length yet. "HI Ninja" read like "Hi, ninja". I scrolled, no Book now. The yellow "Attend with a friend for free if you book in November" caught me: could the friend be Eli?

## Would I book

Ask first. What stops me:
1. **Dates.** "Start any day from 15 January 2027." Eli's winter break ends early January. The chat could not answer "Can we come over Christmas break in December?"
2. **Supervision.** Nothing says who is responsible for a 16-year-old in class, at the evening class (ends 10pm) or after. "Rick often rotates ... when he is available": who teaches when he is not?
3. **Sleeping.** "No private rooms", a bunk-bed photo, "The dorms are not set up for families", book your own place and the price stays the same.

Pick: 2 weeks if the November friend offer covers my own son ($2,222 for both); if not, 1 week each at $1,111 over spring break. To book I need one line on supervising minors, a yes that a parent trains beside her teen, and a yes or no on Eli as "the friend".

## The photos

They made it real: many years, many places, real sweaty people, not stock. Convinced me: the bright gym where a teenager in a blue shirt and a young Asian student swing padded sticks (finally Eli's age), the laughing redheaded woman in a partner drill, the green-roofed hall that looks like Thailand, and the student quotes with faces. Off: many older-camera shots of men in black, and several plainly not Thailand (brick fortress, pine forest, park) with no place named. Nobody my age with a teen. The changing pictures showed variety, but one swapped mid-caption and I could not get it back: mild annoyance.

## The bonuses

WinJitsu, my words: Rick coaching the mind, focus, goals, "beat the self-doubt that holds you back", plus his books later and a free month of an app. Clearly a bonus with the stay, and honestly the part that sold me most for a boy who barely leaves his room. Letdowns: no books on 2 weeks ("from orange on"), and "a free month" hints at a bill later.

Pocket Dojo, my words: a phone app with every belt lesson so you keep practising at home, and you can ask Rick on a lesson. The top says "with every stay", but the box ends on a big red "See the Home Study Program" button that looks like a separate purchase, and the "Every stay" list under the prices names the WinJitsu app and a T-shirt, not Pocket Dojo. So I am not sure I get it. WinJitsu made the price feel more worth it; Pocket Dojo a little.

## Welcome

Half welcome as a parent. Yes: "16 AND UP: No upper age limit. At 16 or 17, a parent signs the papers."; the FAQ naming "the enrolment, a travel consent letter and a medical consent"; "Not a fight camp. No sparring unless you ask. Not a boot camp."; "Am I too old? No."; "every drill has an easier version"; the women's dorm. I would train too.

No: "Can I bring my family or kids? The dorms are not set up for families ... the kids can train at NinjaGym Kids." Is a 16-year-old a kid there or a student here? And "I am 16 or 17. Can I come on my own? Yes" means a minor may sleep in a shared dorm with adult strangers, exactly what I will not do.

## The chat

Asked: "My son is 16. Can he and I train together and share our own room, not a dorm with strangers?" It answered the room half: "No private rooms. The stay is a shared men's or women's dorm. Want your own room? Book one nearby; the price stays the same." Nothing on training together. Follow-ups "Who supervises a 16 year old during the program?" and "Can we come over Christmas break in December?" both got "I do not have that one written down, and I would rather say so than guess" and an Ask Kuno button (not pressed).

## Legit or gimmick

**LEGIT.** Decided at the "16 AND UP" box and the FAQ's three consent papers, then the student quotes and "Black Belt" in the press list. Worth $2,222 a head for us depends on the answers above.

## Moments that worked

- The "16 AND UP" box and the FAQ's three papers.
- "What it is not": no fight camp, no sparring unless you ask, no boot camp.
- A plain schedule: 8 to 10am, the day free, weekends free; free airport pick-up.
- WinJitsu coaching on self-doubt: the real reason I would bring Eli.
- Gamer touches (game room, Kuno's anime face): Eli's hook.
- "The old camps were hard core ... This intro is not that."
- The chat refusing to guess; the question form's topic "I am 16 or 17".

## Buy, write or leave

Write: the question form, topic "I am 16 or 17": December dates, who supervises, a room for two, and whether Eli counts as the free friend.

## The one change

Add one "Bringing your teen?" answer, in the FAQ and the chat: who is responsible for a 16 or 17 year old in class and after, that a parent can train beside them, where the two of you can sleep together, and whether your own child counts as the free friend.
