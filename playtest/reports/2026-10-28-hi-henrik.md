# HI round, 28 Oct 2026: Henrik (54, Copenhagen, beginner, resetting his life)

## Verdict in one line
A real teacher with a real history, but the headline sells "by Rick Tew" and only the FAQ says Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week; I would write before paying, and might take the 2-month belt instead of the "relaxed" introduction.

## First 10 seconds
Phone: the headline, "a private group workshop" on Koh Samui, a red Book now, a yellow FREE line, a cartoon girl. A beginners' martial arts course in Thailand that wants me to book. I would scroll first: no length, price or teacher facts yet. What is "HI"?

Guessed: group size, ages, how "start any day" fits "next group 22 January", week 2's picks, whether I may train more than 2 hours a day.

What would clear it: one block near the top: "10 mornings of 2 hours; Rick teaches at least 4; trained Guides lead the rest; groups of X, ages 16 to 60s."

Mine: "Two weeks of martial arts training on Koh Samui with Rick Tew. Beginners of any age."

## Buy, write or leave
