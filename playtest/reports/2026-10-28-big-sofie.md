# Persona round, 28 Oct 2026: Sofie (27, Utrecht, backpacker, phone only)

## Verdict in one line
The teacher looks real and the photos sold me, but I could not find a price I can afford, a date I can make, or a short way to try it, so I would ask one question and probably move on.

## First 10 seconds
A martial arts course on Koh Samui by Rick Tew, for beginners and tourists. Photo of him with a woman and a guy in a bright hall: normal people. It wants me to press Book Now. I would not; I scroll for a price and dates. Puzzled by "HI NINJA" (Hawaii?) and "private group workshop" (private or group?). The chat face covers "Koh" in the first sentence.

## Would I book
Not now. I would ask first, once. Three things stop me:
1. **Price.** $2,222 for two weeks is 44 days of my budget. Classes are 8 to 10 in the morning, so about $110 an hour of class, bed included. For a backpacker that is not "worth it", that is a different trip.
2. **Dates.** "Start any day from 15 January 2027." It is October. Backpackers do not book 11 weeks ahead, and "All sales are final" scares me when my plans change every week.
3. **No taster.** I want three days or a drop-in class. I found "1 week, half the price, $1,111" in small print near the bottom, but it is not in the menu, and "Book one week" opens a question form, not a booking.

I would pick 1 week, or 2 weeks with a hostel friend on the November friend-for-free offer ($1,111 each, closer). To book I need: a short option I can book directly, an earlier start, and reviews somewhere I trust (Google, Hostelworld).

## The photos
They make it real. Convinced me: the top photo (Rick, a woman, a man, guard stance, bright hall); the red-haired woman doing a wrist release while the class waits; women kicking pads; a group doing stances on pink mats in an open sala; the dorm with bunk beds and the play mats. I saw many women my age and level. Yes, someone like me.

Looked off or old: many are grainy compact-camera shots with a much younger Rick in a blue uniform, so I cannot tell what a class looks like now. Places jump around (pine forest, fortress, Grand Canyon); I lost track of which are Samui. One looks like a kids' play gym in a mall. The changing pictures were fine while scrolling, but when I stopped, one faded out under me and I could not swipe back.

## The bonuses
**WinJitsu:** Rick's mental training: coaching on focus and goals, five self-help books, and a free month of an app. **Pocket Dojo:** an app with the lessons and footprint diagrams, so I keep practicing at home.

Both clearly read as "with every stay". Pocket Dojo made it a bit more worth it: I would use that in Utrecht. WinJitsu read like a motivational speaker's product, with a long Pai story, and the books "join your belts from orange on", which I will never reach. More distraction than value. The red "See the Home Study Program" button inside the bonus box made me wonder if Pocket Dojo is free or something to buy.

## Welcome
Yes, as a beginner woman travelling alone: "Built for beginners: every drill has an easier version." "Women who want self-defense ... there is a women's dorm." "Not a fight camp. No sparring unless you ask. Not a boot camp." "Travelers and nomads: class, then the day is yours." And "more than six years teaching in Holland": I am Dutch, that made me smile.

Not for me, not hostile: gamers, Mastery, belts.

## The chat
I asked: "can i just drop in for a class or do a few days? im backpacking and 2 weeks is a lot." Answer: "I do not have that one written down... Send it to me" with an Ask Kuno button. Miss. I rephrased: "is there something shorter than 2 weeks?" It answered fast: "One week is half the price, $1,111: book one week", but the button said "Ask Kuno about one week", not book. Then: "I'm coming by ferry from Koh Tao, can you pick me up at the pier?" It answered with the airport pick-up, which is not my question. Half helpful.

## Legit or gimmick
The training is real: class photos with Rick in almost all of them, named students with faces, NinjaGym, magazines, Holland. I decided at the Real People photo boxes. But the selling smells a bit like a funnel: "Only 12 spots left for 2027", "save $2,000", $15,988, all before I found my own price. Real teacher, unsure it is worth it for me.

**Vote: UNSURE.**

## Buy, write or leave
Write, once: "Is there a drop-in or a 3-day try-out, and can I start before January?" If the answer is no, I leave and send the link to the hostel group with "friend for free in November".

## The one change
Give travellers a short, bookable way in near the top: a drop-in class or a 3-day taster (or the 1 week) with its price and "book" button, shown before the belts and Mastery.
