# Head-to-head: Jonas, the gamer at Nerd Hostel (28, Germany, remote software tester)

## Verdict in one line

/gns/: it is the only one that looks made for someone like me, and it shows real people on real mats.

## First 10 seconds

- **ricktew.com/gns/**: a cartoon ninja shouting "SHHT!" and "Real Ninja Skills. Zero Seriousness." For three seconds I thought meme page or T-shirt shop. The line under it saved it: live-in training on Koh Samui, 2 weeks, $2,222, Enroll Now. Gimmick at first look, "fun camp, probably real" by second ten.
- **ricktew.com/hininja/**: "Two weeks on Koh Samui. Train with Rick Tew." Clean, black and white, like a yoga retreat booking page. Clear, but it assumes I know who Rick Tew is. The top bar ("HI Ninja door / Take the AI Ninja door") made me wonder if I was on an AI company's site.

## Legit or gimmick

- **ricktew.com/gns/: LEGIT.** Decided at the photo grid under "What is it" (adults on red mats, a gym that exists), sealed by the 2000 tape in the file: grainy camcorder footage, guys in black running a field, a man doing forms on a rock in the mountains. Zero CGI, nerd-cool. Real, yes. Worth $2,222, not sure.
- **ricktew.com/hininja/: UNSURE.** Honest words, but not one picture of a class. A jump kick on a viewpoint (looked like Phi Phi to me, not Samui) and a man in flannel with a microphone, which reads "seminar", not "dojo". The press list plus "Tony Robbins school of coaching" felt guru, not gym. No video on the page when I visited.

## The same thing?

Only on day two. Nothing on /gns/ points to /hininja/ ("Which ninja are you?" is just the quiz). On /hininja/ the first paragraph is the same sentence word for word, same price, then "Its name is Gooffy Ninja ShhT!!". Noticed within one screen. Two landing pages for one product felt like a marketing experiment and made me more suspicious of both. Odd too: the sober page still says "The goofy is the whole point. Every session is silly."

## The price as I understood it

$2,222 for 2 weeks: a bunk in a shared dorm, "simple, hearty" meals (how many a day? not said), a 2-hour class 8 to 10am Monday to Friday with Rick or a NinjaGym Guide (Rick at least 2 a week), an optional 2-hour evening review. So 10 taught classes, about 20 hours. Extra weeks $1,000. Free month of the WinJitsu app, free T-shirt. All sales final, dates can move within a year. Fair for me? Steep: over $100 per taught hour, and Muay Thai camps on the islands cost a fraction. I would only pay because it is a story I can post.

## My own question

"I already have a bed at my gamer hostel and I work German hours (afternoon and evening here). What does it cost without the dorm?" Both pages answer it in the FAQ "Already have a place on Samui?": same price, plus a private 1-on-1 with Rick. The /gns/ quiz result says it plainer ("basically thrown in free"). Clear and honest, just not what I wanted. Mornings fit my work; neither page mentions wifi or working remotely.

## Which page I would book from

**Vote: /gns/.** "This one gets me, train by day, game by night, and the old tapes prove the guy is real; the other one looks like a brochure for my parents." It is also the one I would forward to the hostel chat.

## What would make the other page win

The /gns/ photo grid of people training, plus one current photo of a class and the game room, right under the /hininja/ headline. Right now /hininja/ asks me to trust words alone.
