# Priya, 35, Manchester, holiday traveller (phone first, then laptop at 1280 x 800)

## Verdict in one line
I believe Rick is real and the classes are gentle enough for me, but I can't see exactly what my $2,222 buys in bed, meals and safety net, and "all sales are final" means I write first.

## First 10 seconds
Phone: "Rick Tew HI Ninja", a red Book Now, "Martial Arts / Ninja Course / by Rick Tew" over a real class photo in a wooden hall. A martial arts course on Koh Samui with this man. The round chat button covered "Koh" in the first paragraph. The yellow "Attend with a friend for free if you book in November" caught my eye. I would not press Book Now yet; I'd scroll for what it is and what it costs.

## Would I book
Ask first. I'd pick 2 weeks ($2,222), my January leave. What stops me:
- **Safety net.** "All sales are final; your dates can move, within a year of buying." Fine for a flight change, but my leave is fixed. If I'm ill or lose my leave, I lose about £1,700. Nothing on insurance or a cancelled class.
- **Where I sleep.** How many women share the dorm? Bathroom, air con, lockers? "No private rooms", and my own hotel doesn't lower the price.
- **Meals.** "Simple meals if you need a little boost": one a day? None?
- **Value.** 10 mornings of 2 hours plus optional evenings, against a yoga retreat with a room and food.

I'd need one plain box: what's included, the women's dorm with a count, meals, and what happens if I can't come.

## The photos
Yes, they make it real: wooden halls, red mats, garden lawns, the sea terrace, the bunk room. Nothing looks like stock. What convinced me: the hero of Rick with students lined up; the hero changing to a woman about my age in a relaxed guard between two men; the woman kicking the red bag; women on the pink mats; Sibel's circle photo; the beach and scooter shots (my holiday).

Off: many look old and soft, about 2010. On the laptop six boxes change at once, which is busy, and twice I caught one mid-fade, two pictures stacked like a double exposure. One box turned into a quote card. Lots of Rick doing splits on rocks and castles: impressive, a bit much.

Someone like me? Yes, a few times. The changing boxes showed me more women, but annoyed me while reading captions.

## The bonuses
**WinJitsu:** Rick's "mental martial art": coaching on focus and goals, his five books, a free month of an app. **Pocket Dojo:** a lesson app, belt by belt, to keep practising at home. Both say "with every stay", so they read as bonuses. But:
- The WinJitsu box is about four phone screens, with his Pai backstory and a quote. It felt like an author plugging books in the middle of my holiday.
- The books "join your belts from orange on": do I get them on 2 weeks?
- The "Every stay" list under the prices names the WinJitsu app and a T-shirt, not Pocket Dojo. Is it really included?
- A free month of an app: does it charge me after?
- The Pocket Dojo box ends on a red "See the Home Study Program" button, which looks like a sale.

They didn't make the price feel more worth it; they distracted me.

## Welcome
Mostly yes. "Not a boot camp. Every drill has an easier version." "Not a fight camp. No sparring unless you ask." "Built for beginners." "Women who want self-defense... there is a women's dorm." "The old camps were hard core... This intro is not that."

Pause: "Knife, after a waiver" in the yellow belt list, "Combat Reality", a review's "how far he can push his students", the dark Mastery band ("the old-school way"), and "Tell me before you come" about injuries with only a form to do it.

## The chat
I asked: "If my flights change or I can't come, can I move my dates or get a refund?" Instant answer: "All sales are final; your dates can move, within a year of buying." Then a red "Pick your stay" button. Honest, but the same line again, and a sales button after a refund question felt pushy. "Are meals included?" repeated "simple meals if you need a little boost". "How many women share the women's dorm?" gave no number.

## Legit or gimmick
**UNSURE**, leaning legit on the training. I believed Rick was real at "Rick Tew. A real person, a real background.": the press list, NinjaGym founder, the video, years of photos. "What it is not" and the FAQ convinced me it's gentle. Unsure on value and terms: no outside reviews (initials only), no refund case, vague bed and meals.

- When: 8 to 10 in the morning, the day is mine, weekends free.
- Free airport pick-up; NerdHostel across the street; the bunk room photo.

## Words I did not understand

## Buy, write or leave
**Write.** The question form: women's dorm size and bathroom, meals per day, and what if I fall ill or lose my leave. A kind, specific answer and I'd book 2 weeks, probably with a friend in November.

## The one change
Put a plain "Your 2 weeks, exactly" box beside the $2,222, and show that card first: what's included (classes, pick-up, meals per day), where you sleep (dorm photo, how many share, bathroom, AC), and what happens if your flights change, you fall ill or you can't come.
