## Verdict in one line

Write first, not buy: the words promise a relaxed island holiday with about two hours of class a day, but the old camp photos, the "ShhT" jokes and no answer on where I sleep or what happens if my flights change stop me paying a non-refundable $2,222.

**Before scrolling (Q1):** 10 seconds for "two weeks on Koh Samui, train with Rick Tew", 15 to reach "It is relaxed... about 2 hours". It asks me to Enrol. Who it's for only shows up in the table further down.

## The colour

Vote: **BLUE**

Energetic and martial, and clean rather than cheap on white. It works on the price card, where the red border makes the price easy to find. What bothers me is red in huge condensed capitals: "TRAIN WITH RICK TEW." reads like someone shouting at me, which is my exact fear. Add the glowing ENROL pill that follows me, "LOCK IN YOUR SPOT" and the red BUY, and it feels like a boot camp poster. The dark form is fine, just heavy. Blue would make "it is relaxed" believable: blue says retreat and booking site, red says drill sergeant.
