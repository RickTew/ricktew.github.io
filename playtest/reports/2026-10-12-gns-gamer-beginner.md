## Verdict in one line
The gaming dojo and the quiz had me, but the GNS page looks like a cartoon merch drop until I open the secret file, and "Enroll Now" drops me on an AI business page.

## Legit or gimmick
First 10 seconds: a cartoon ninja sticker shouting SHHT, "Real ninja skills. Zero seriousness.", then $2,222. I read it as a ninja party hostel for anime fans. Gimmick-leaning, but funny enough to keep scrolling.

"The training is legit" did nothing. What nearly lost me: not one real photo of a human on the main page (only the logo, emojis, a drawn sunset, a t-shirt), "Zero seriousness", and a paragraph explaining the misspelling joke.

I decided it was real inside the file, on the "Rick Tew / Meet the sensei" tape: a guy in a blue gi and black belt correcting a room of adults. The Asia photo (him doing the splits on red mats in an actual gym) sealed it.

Votes: **GNS page: LEGIT** (only after the file; UNSURE before it). **HI page: LEGIT** (real photo on screen two, plain hours, says who teaches).
