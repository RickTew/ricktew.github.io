# Head-to-head, 21 Oct 2026: Ken, the ninjutsu-curious history teacher (ken-skeptic)

Start: ricktew.com/gns/. Phone first, then both pages on a laptop, about 40 screenshots, plus a peek at /hininja/history/ and /rtms/.

## Verdict in one line

/gns/: it's the only page that shows me the receipts (the old tape, the magazine spreads, the belt list), and the jokes don't bother me once I can see the teaching underneath.

## First 10 seconds

- **ricktew.com/gns/**: a cartoon ninja going "SHHT!" and "Real Ninja Skills. Zero Seriousness." First thought: gimmick, maybe a party hostel. The line under it saved it: a named teacher, a place, a schedule, a price. I kept going because of "The training is legit" and the photos of adults on red mats.
- **ricktew.com/hininja/**: clean and serious, "Two weeks on Koh Samui. Train with Rick Tew," a guy jump-kicking on a cliff. Real training, clearly. The top bar ("You are in the HI Ninja door / Take the AI Ninja door") made no sense to someone who searched for ninjutsu.

## Legit or gimmick

- **ricktew.com/gns/: LEGIT.** Decided inside "The Real ShhT" file: the field record, then the RTMS 2000 tape. I pressed play and got real VHS: a young Rick on a stage, a camp road trip, nine minutes of it, plus a seven-minute "Meet the sensei." The Europe file has magazine spreads. The belt tabs (named rolls, falls, kicks per level) read like a real syllabus, which a belt mill would not bother writing out.
- **ricktew.com/hininja/: UNSURE.** Same record, but only as text in boxes. No footage, no gym, no students. Anyone can type "In the press: Black Belt." I believed it only because I had already seen the tapes.
- Open on both: "Modern American Ninjitsu" is Rick's own system, and neither page says who taught Rick or what rank he holds. Honest not to fake a Japanese lineage. But the only CREDENTIALS row is a Tony Robbins coaching certificate, not a martial arts one.

## The same thing?

Yes, in the first screen of /hininja/: the hero sentence is word for word GNS, then "Its name is Gooffy Ninja ShhT!!" and the same Four 2s. Not confusing; it read like GNS with the proof taken out. Odd: the "serious" page still says "wrapped in a deliberately silly delivery" and "The goofy is the whole point."

## The price as I understood it

$2,222 for 2 weeks, all in: a bunk in a shared hostel dorm, simple meals (how many a day? not said), a 2-hour class 8 to 10am Monday to Friday, an optional 2-hour evening review, a free month of the WinJitsu app, a T-shirt. Extra weeks $1,000. Arrive any day; no refunds, but my two weeks can move within a year. Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week; a NinjaGym Guide takes the rest. About $160 a day for a bunk at 46 is steep, but fair if the teaching is real. The "2 classes with Rick" part makes me pause.
