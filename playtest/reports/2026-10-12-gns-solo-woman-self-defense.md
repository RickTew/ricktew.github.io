# GNS round, 12 Oct 2026: Aisha, solo traveller who wants real self-defense

Start: https://ricktew.com/gns/ (Instagram ad). Phone, then laptop.

## Verdict in one line

The training looks real once I dug, but the GNS page jokes about the one thing I came for, never says a woman will be safe or welcome, and its Enroll button dropped me on an AI business page; the HI page nearly sold me.

## Legit or gimmick

First ten seconds on GNS: cartoon ninja, "SHHT", "Zero seriousness." Party hostel with kicks, aimed at guys. The next paragraph ("genuine, structured ... self-defense") pulled me back; "deliberately silly delivery" pushed me out. I decided it was real at the Orange Belt card (stances, falls, strikes, footwork: that is a syllabus), and properly at the Field Record inside the file (teaching since 16, own dojo at 19, Black Belt magazine). "Zero seriousness" next to "self-defense" almost made me close it. On HI I believed it at "Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week": nobody fakes a sentence that modest.

Votes: **GNS: UNSURE. HI: LEGIT.**

## The files

On the phone I scrolled past "Declassify the file 👀": it read like an easter egg, not credentials. On the laptop, maybe. Most women like me would not. Inside, three things changed my mind: the Field Record list, the Strikes & Kicks tape (a man in a gi working a bag, real technique, not a skit), and the Asia file photo of Rick at NinjaGym today. The boulder-climbing TRMS tape did nothing for me. If ONE thing should be visible without pressing: the Field Record, right under "The training is legit. The vibe is fun." That sentence makes a claim; the record proves it.
