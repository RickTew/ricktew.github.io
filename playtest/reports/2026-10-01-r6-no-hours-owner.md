# Lena, the owner with no hours - 2026-10-01 (round six)

Bakery with a catering side, eleven staff, two shifts. Phone, standing up, after 10pm, ten minutes before sleep. One question: if I hand this over, how many hours a week come back, and what do I still have to do?

## How this session ran

- Headless Chromium, iPhone 13 profile (390 x 664, touch), live page https://ricktew.com/aininja/. The page is 28,209 px tall: **42.5 phone screens**.
- Both mail endpoints were routed to abort before the page loaded, in every script. Requests to `functions/v1` seen: **0**. Blocked: **0**. The send button was never pressed, so nothing left the browser.
- Used: two FAQ taps, three solution rows, the chat box (five questions), the quiz (all twelve) and the mailbox twice: once from "I want this Tew" on the Your Dojo card and once from the quiz's "Talk to me about it". Both were filled and **not sent**. I typed only into visible, labelled fields.
- About 18 minutes in character.

## First impression (top screen, before scrolling)

- **What is it:** a man who builds "AI ninjas" for a business, and either I run it or he runs it with me. That took about 10 seconds.
- **Who it is for:** owners who need their time back. "One real business needed its owner's time back" hit me straight away.
- **What it asks:** "I want this Tew!!" or "Take the quiz".
- **Not known yet:** what I would actually hand over, and the price. I got both at screens 3 to 4, about 1.5 minutes in. The top screen is not judged further, per the brief.

## Verdict in one line

She would write, but only after the chat box sent her to the quiz to get the one thing the page never says in plain sight: how many hours come back (about 10 a week, says the quiz). The price, who does the computer work and the next step were all clear by screen 4 of 42.

## Moments that worked

- **"Hate tech, tired of it, or no time for it."** It names time, not only attitude. "You have no time to learn it, and none to fix it." That is me.
- **The price comes early and is flat.** It is there by screen 3 to 4, on two cards. "Any number of people." matters with 11 staff: a per-seat price would have scared me off. "The AI is in the price." means no meter.
- **"Nothing for you to log into."** It appears on the R2 card, in the five-words box, in the Dojo section and in the FAQ. My allergy words ("log in", "set up", "your account") only show up to say I will not have to do them, except in the Claudeforce section, where they describe the other guy.
- **"Do I have to manage any of the tech?"** "no dashboard, no console, no certificate to renew, no password to keep somewhere safe, and no bug that is yours to fix. When something needs changing, you tell me." This is the best answer on the page for me.
- **"How it works" in four lines.** Short and plain.
- **The three desk rows.** One line each, in plain words. "No more 'did that payment come in?'" is a real thing I live with.
- **The chat on "what do I still have to do after I pay".** "One press... At the start I need an hour or two of your answers". This is the clearest "what stays on my plate" anywhere, and it only exists in the chat.
- **The chat on "can I start tonight".** "Tonight? The mailbox at the bottom of this page, and I reply myself, usually within a day." My exact question, answered.
- **The quiz result's honesty.** "It does not add up yet, and I would rather say so than sell it." That made me trust him more than any testimonial would have, and it spoke in hours: 10 a week.
- **"Talk to me about it" after the quiz.** It carries all my answers into the message box and leaves two blanks ("My company:", "What I would love off my plate:"). That is a two-minute job.
- **The mailbox speaks my language.** The default subject is "I want my hours back: the inbox, the bookings, the admin", and the box is labelled "WHERE YOUR HOURS GO".
- **"Cancel anytime, effective at the end of the paid month."**
