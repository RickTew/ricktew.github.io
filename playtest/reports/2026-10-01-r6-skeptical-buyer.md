# Marco, the skeptical buyer - 2026-10-01 (round six)

Session: headless Chromium, 1280 x 800 laptop, https://ricktew.com/aininja/ read one screen at a time (about 26 screens, 20,164 px tall). About 20 minutes in character. I used the chat box (8 questions), took the quiz (all 12), filled the mailbox twice and opened the Terms from the footer, because I read terms. Both mail endpoints (`ricktew-contact`, `ricktew-intake`) were blocked before the page loaded, in every script: zero requests reached them, nothing hit `functions/v1` at all, and neither "Drop it in my mailbox" nor "Send me the breakdown" was pressed. The made-up address used was marco@example.test.

## Verdict in one line
I would write, carefully: the prices match everywhere I checked and the quiz told me to my face that $2,222 a month "does not add up yet" for me, but the price of the one thing I came for (a support desk on its own) is not on the page, and the only proof of a client desk is unnamed.

## Moments that worked
- **Prices in two screens, and they hold.** $2,222 a month appears on the card, the FAQ, the Dojo section, the Claudeforce band, the FAQ again, the chat box, the quiz and the Terms. Same number every time. $222, then $99 a month or $999 a year, also matches everywhere. I audited for a mismatch and found none in the plan prices.
- **Cancel terms on the card itself.** "Monthly, cancel anytime, effective at the end of the paid month." Same words in the FAQ, the chat box and the Terms ("No long-term contract, no cancellation fee"). No catch that I could find.
- **The quiz result.** "By your own numbers that is about $1,050 a month back against $2,222 a month. It does not add up yet, and I would rather say so than sell it." That single sentence did more for my trust than the whole story section. Nobody who burned me ever said that.
- **"If three of you need a contact list and nothing else, buy their Starter at $25 a seat. I will not beat that, and I will not pretend to."** A seller pointing me at the cheaper competitor. Noted, with respect.
- **"What happens if you disappear?"** answered straight, with what is mine ("your domain, your mail, your content, your videos, your customer records") and what is not.
- **Chat box on "not included":** it listed the exclusions and the Tew Tip said "Read the Not included lines first. They are where the surprises would have been." That is my own rule.
- **Chat box on NDA and liability:** it named Terms section 7 (confidentiality) and the liability cap ("the fees of the three months before a claim, sections 6 and 8"). Specific, checkable, and the Terms said the same.
- **The Terms are short** (about 1,100 words) and plain. Section 4 says the build stays his in so many words. I prefer an unpleasant fact written down to a pleasant one implied.
- **"No testimonials yet, on purpose ... I will not invent a quote to fill a box."** Disappointing, but honest.
- **Live sites I could open**: ninjagym.com, sabaisen.com, evercoolthailand.com and the rest. A build I can open beats a quote.
- **The mailbox is a demo of the product I want.** "Your message lands with The Ninja Agent, an AI ... It writes back within a minute ... and it always says it is an AI. I read every message and follow up myself, usually within a day." Writing to him is the test drive of the Front Desk. That is clever and it is honest about it.
- **"Can I get just one desk?"** and **"What happens in the first month?"** in the lower FAQ were exactly my two questions, answered plainly.

## Would I write?
Yes. Not because of the story, but because of the quiz admitting the numbers do not work for me, the cancel terms matching in four places, and the Terms putting the unpleasant parts in writing. Writing is also the cheapest way to watch his Front Desk work.

First line I would type: "Two person consultancy, I want a desk that drafts replies to client mail. Your own quiz says $2,222 a month does not add up for me, so what does one Front Desk cost as a scoped build, and what do I keep if I stop paying?"

The three questions I need answered before paying anything:
1. **Price of one desk, in writing.** Is it the "$222 per 2-hour block" in Terms section 1, how many blocks for a Front Desk, and is there a monthly fee after it is built?
2. **Proof I can check.** Can I see a live Front Desk drafts queue for someone other than you (screen share, redacted), or speak to the "coaching team" whose inbox it runs?
3. **Exit and data.** If I cancel after three months, in what format do I get my answer library and the drafts back, and while it runs, where does my clients' mail sit on your stack and who can read it?
