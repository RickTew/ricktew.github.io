# Ninja Menu round, 25 Oct 2026: Jonas, the gamer at Nerd Hostel

## Verdict in one line

The picker is the most fun part of the page and I finished it in 90 seconds, but at the Book button I hit $2,222, no start date in sight (it turns out to be 15 January 2027) and "Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week", so I would write, not book.

## First 10 seconds

Phone: headline "Two week intro to martial arts by Rick Tew", one paragraph, a red "Take the quiz", and right under it a card that says "TAKE THE QUIZ" again. Red "Ask first" in the header. No picture on screen one. I got it: beginner martial arts on Samui, "not a boot camp". I pressed "Take the quiz" (the only thing to press); it scrolled a thumb's width to the card I could already see. "HI Ninja": hi as in hello? The red "Ask first" said: do not just book.

Laptop is better: picker beside the headline, photo grid on screen one.

## The quiz

Fun. Not a quiz, a loadout builder, which for a gamer is better. I built Nunchaku, Sword / Half Staff, Stealthing, Kip, Grappling. Five is right.

Confusing: the belt colours. Four of my five picks were green or blue belt. In a two-week intro, do I actually do nunchaku, or is blue a warning? Nothing says. The one-liners helped; "metsubishi", "Magnificent 7's" and "1-40 Form" did not. I wanted to see each move, not read it.

## The moment to book

"Your Ninja Week is ready... Now pick how long you stay." Clear. But under my chips: "Week 2" with five empty circles and "5 of 10 picked". Ready, or half done? On 1 month: "5 of 20", still only Weeks 1 and 2 shown.

Stay choices: 2 weeks $2,222, 3 weeks $3,222, 1 month $3,997, a belt (2 months) $7,994. "Book my two weeks" opens name, email, "Save my plan and book, $2,222"; arrival date at checkout; all sales final.

"Add a week for only $1,000" appears only after Book. Fair maths, but "only" smells like sales, and the button under it, "ADD A WEEK, $3,222", reads for a second like one week costs $3,222. My question at Book was "when?", and that screen does not say.

## The program as I understood it

- Sensei Seminar: 8 to 10 am, Monday to Friday, Rick mixes the group's picks. Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week, Guides (people who trained there before) the rest. Found only in "A day on Samui" and the FAQ, after building my plan.
- Training: 2 hours a day, plus an optional 2 in the evening.
- Review class: optional, 8 to 10 pm, practise the morning.
- WinJitsu: Rick's "mental martial art", coaching for focus and goals, included, plus a month of the app. Hours of coaching: guessed, not stated.
- Sleep: shared men's or women's dorm at NerdHostel, six bunks, no extra cost, no private rooms. Food: "simple meals if you need a little boost" (guessed: some basic meals, how many unknown).
- Belt program: 2 months a belt, $7,994 each, yellow to red.
- Earliest arrival 15 January 2027 (price box and FAQ only).
