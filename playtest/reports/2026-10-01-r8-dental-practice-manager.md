# Helen, the dental practice manager - 2026-10-01 (round eight, quick check)

Read on a laptop, headless Chromium at 1280 x 800, live page https://ricktew.com/aininja/. About 18 minutes in character. The page measured 11,000 px, about 14 screens. Both mail endpoints were blocked before the page loaded. The block logged zero attempts in every run, so no request went through. The mailbox send button, the quiz's "Send me the breakdown" and the chat's "Ask Rick this" were never pressed.

## Verdict in one line
Helen would write, but only after the owner dentist says go. She also took the quiz and used the chat box. The page gives her the price, the "your team presses send" rule and a HIPAA business associate agreement line. It does not tell her whether it works with the practice software, whether text-message reminders cost extra, or which AI company sees patient mail.

## Moments of friction (the core of the report)

1. **Top screen** ("I'm not a marketing expert..."). I expected to learn what he does for an office like mine. After about 20 seconds I knew it was one man who builds AI help for small businesses. I did not know what the help actually does until the plan cards on the next screen, about 1.5 minutes in. Noted for timing only, no change proposed. Severity: cosmetic.

2. **The stat strip under the top screen** ("30 years a ninja", "25 years a nerd", "6,242 rounds of changes since March", "100 solutions built"). I expected credentials. Instead I got numbers I could not repeat to the owner. What is a "round of changes"? Why does a ninja count matter to a dental office? I skimmed past. Severity: cosmetic.

3. **"Hate tech, tired of it, or no time for it."** The checklist ends "You do not want it explained. You want it done." I am the opposite: I am good with systems, and I have to explain every spend to the owner in two sentences. For a moment it felt like the page was not for me. The price cards right under it pulled me back in. Severity: annoyance.

4. **The paragraph under the plan cards:** "Email first ... WhatsApp, LINE, Instagram or texts are a build, not a switch, scoped in writing before the clock starts." Our recall and appointment reminders go out by text. So the main thing I would want is "a build". Is that inside the $2,222 or extra? And "before the clock starts" sounds like hourly billing. I also do not know what LINE is. Severity: annoyance (it is a real gap in what I would tell the owner).

5. **The first questions, "Is it safe to let an AI read my inbox?"** The answer I needed is the very last sentence of a long answer about prompts and bounces: "For a health practice, I sign a HIPAA business associate agreement." I only found it because I open every fold. Just before it: "Who reads your customers' mail: only me, plus the AI that drafts the replies." Which AI company? Does it sign a BAA too? Where does the mail sit? The page never names a country or a company. It also tells me he runs a gym on Koh Samui. For patient mail, that is the vague part that stops me writing "safe" in my note. Severity: annoyance. Without the BAA sentence this would have been would-quit.

6. **The first questions, "Does it replace the apps I already pay for?"** "It works alongside what you already use, like your booking app or your till." Our system is practice management software that holds the schedule, recalls and insurance. "Booking app or till" is not our world, and "the free audit maps what connects" leaves the main question open. The Booking Desk row adds to the worry: "People book you without an account." Would that be a second calendar next to ours? Double bookings are my nightmare. Severity: annoyance.

7. **The solutions, eleven rows.** Three are my world (Front Desk, Booking Desk, Money Desk). One I read for the chat box (Answer Engine). The other seven I did not open: Marketing Room, Ad Room, Publishing House, Quiz Funnel, Video Room, Colleagues by Email, The Notebook, plus "The helpings". Colleagues by Email ("AI agents you mail like staff") and The Notebook ("Your AI learns. You hold the veto.") lost me. Severity: annoyance.

8. **"Your own Digital Dojo. Built by a sensei."** By now this is the third time I have read that it runs on his stack, that a bigger job is a custom price, and that R2 Hosting is $222 then $99. "How it grows, step by step" repeats the white belt, propose-only idea from "What do the words mean?". The one new thing in the note card is The Intake, which I was not ready for. Severity: annoyance. This is where I would have closed the tab in real life (see below).

9. **Two FAQ blocks.** "The first questions" up top and "The questions that come up every time" near the end. I expected one list. The second one opens with "Will an AI talk to my customers without me knowing?" and answers "Never." Two sentences later it says "Sending on its own is your choice, task by task." So it is not "never". It is "not unless you switch it on". I am fine with the real answer, but a careful reader catches the word "Never" being walked back. Severity: annoyance.

10. **The second FAQ, "Can somebody's AI assistant deal with you instead of them?"** I did not understand why I would want this, and I did not open it. "Why the belts?" and "Does anyone test it before my customers see it?" are not my questions either. Severity: cosmetic.

11. **Chat box: "Is patient information safe with you? Are you HIPAA compliant?"** It answered "I do not have that one, and I would rather say so than guess." I tried "HIPAA?" alone: same miss. "What about patient privacy?" DID return the BAA line. So the box knows the answer but not the word every practice owner will type. If I had opened the chat before the FAQ, I would have read that as "he does not do HIPAA" and closed the tab. Severity: would-quit for anyone who asks the chat first. Annoyance for me, because I had already seen the line.

12. **Chat box answers to different questions:**
    - I asked "Does this work with our dental practice software? We use Dentrix." It answered "Does it replace the apps I already pay for?". That is not my question. I asked about connecting, and it answered about replacing.
    - I asked "Which AI company reads our patient emails, and do they sign a BAA too?" It answered "Which AI do you use?", declined to name the company, and said nothing about a BAA.
    - I asked "Where is our data stored?" It answered the general privacy entry: "runs on my stack". No place, no country.
    - I asked "Can it handle recall reminders and cancellations for a dental office?" It answered "What is The Booking Desk?". That is close, but it is about taking bookings, not recalls inside our existing schedule.

    Severity: annoyance.

13. **Quiz, question 7 "What runs the work today?"** None of the five fits "industry software we license and it works fine". I picked "A proper system I own. It just needs help", but we do not own it. Severity: cosmetic.

14. **Quiz result.** "About 18 a week are draft-and-file work ... worth roughly $2,350 a month by your own numbers", followed by "That reads as Your Dojo, $2,222 a month". The owner will read that as break-even: we pay $2,222 to save $2,350. That is honest math, but it is the first thing he will circle. The result also says "Customers, students, the room". We have patients, not students. Severity: annoyance (it shapes my note).

15. **Mailbox prefill.** I pressed "I want this Tew" on the Your Dojo card. The message box filled nicely: "I want this Tew: Your Dojo, $2,222 a month." with three blanks. But "What is this about" switched to "Which fits me, Your Dojo or R2 Hosting?", even though I had just picked one. Severity: cosmetic.

16. **Under the mailbox:** "It writes back within a minute ... it always says it is an AI." Fine for a sales question. I would still not type anything about a patient into it. Severity: cosmetic.

## Moments that worked
- **The two plan cards on the second screen.** "$2,222 a month, flat" and "Any number of people. The AI is in the price." I copied both straight into my note. For a practice with seven staff, "flat, any number of people" is the line that makes the price explainable.
- **"The jobs it takes: customer replies drafted, bookings with reminders and reschedules, payments filed and invoices written."** That is our front desk in one line.
- **How it works, step 3: "A person checks it. Nothing goes out without a press."** That is the sentence I need near patient mail.
- **"Attachments are never opened ... it cannot touch money, and every draft it ever made stays on file."** Exactly the kind of rule I can repeat to the owner.
- **"For a health practice, I sign a HIPAA business associate agreement."** It exists, and it is plain. It just needs to be easier to find.
- **"What happens if you disappear?"** "your customer records. They are yours in writing, they leave with you, and I keep no copy." Very reassuring for a records person.
- **"I stick to writing on purpose: everything we agree is on the record."** I like a vendor who wants a paper trail.
- **"Does it replace the apps I already pay for? Not by default."** That was the right first answer, even if it does not name our kind of software.
- **The free audit first, and monthly cancel "effective at the end of the paid month".** Low risk to explain.
- **"What do the words mean?"** The four boxes are short and clear. After them I understood Dojo, ninja, belt and sensei.
- **The quiz** asked about scheduling, the same questions and money admin. It recommended The Front Desk, The Booking Desk and The Money Desk on Your Dojo, which matches what I answered. "No email needed" was true.
- **The chat box's honest miss** on insurance questions ("I would rather say so than guess") made me trust its other answers more.
