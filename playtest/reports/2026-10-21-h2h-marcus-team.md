**/hininja/: UNSURE.** The honest lines built trust ("Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week", "Not a fight camp"), but there is no picture of the gym, the dorm or another student, and no video. ## The price as I understood it

The date-move rule makes it workable for a studio that crunches.
