# Ninja Menu round, 25 Oct 2026: Priya, the holiday traveller

## Verdict in one line

I enjoyed building my week and I get the idea, but I would write to Rick before paying $2,222: the meals, the bunk dorm and "All sales are final" sit right at the Book button with no plain answer next to them.

## First 10 seconds

A two-week intro to martial arts on Koh Samui, "not a boot camp". That line kept me. It is for beginners on holiday and wants me to "Take the quiz". I saw the red button at once and pressed it, out of curiosity more than trust. On the laptop the quiz already sits beside the headline, so pressing the button seemed to do nothing.

## The quiz

Fine, close to fun. Five picks is right. The one-line explanations really helped: Flat Fall Back "without getting hurt" and Palm Heel "hard to hurt your own hand" were picked because of those lines. My week: Jam, Block & Run; Flat Fall Back; Horse Stance; Climbing; Palm Heel Strike.

Confusing: it is called a quiz, but nobody asks me anything; it is a menu. The belt colours: is "Blue belt" Climbing too hard for a beginner, or can I pick anything? Nothing says. Ninja Tools (knives, blinding powder) felt a bit macho for my holiday. On the phone my plan sat two screens below, under ten cards.

## The moment to book

"Your Ninja Week is ready" was clear: now pick how long you stay. The four choices: 2 weeks $2,222, 3 weeks $3,222, 1 month $3,997, a belt (2 months) $7,994. But "ready" sat above an empty Week 2 row and "5 of 10 picked", so I thought I wasn't done. Book showed a short form (name, email): plan to Rick, then checkout, then arrival date. Good. "Add a week for only $1,000" did nothing for me (I have two weeks of leave) and "only" read salesy. 

## The program as I understood it

- Sensei Seminar: the 8 to 10am class, Monday to Friday, built from the whole group's picks. Rick teaches at least 2 a week; Guides (earlier students) the rest.
- Hours: 2 each morning, plus an optional 2 hours in the evening. So 2 to 4 a day.
- Review class: optional, 8 to 10pm, practise the morning (or go instead if you hate mornings).
- WinJitsu: Rick's "mental martial art", coaching on focus, goals and self-doubt. Included, plus a free month of the app. How many coaching sessions in two weeks: guessed, not stated.
- Sleep: shared women's dorm at NerdHostel, across the street, six beds in bunks, no extra cost. Food: "simple meals if you need a little boost". Guessed: maybe some basic meals? I genuinely don't know.
- Belt program: 2 months and $7,994 a belt, yellow first, up to 5 belts in about a year.

## Make it pop

**The one change:** in the "Your Ninja Week is ready" card, right above Book, a four-line "Your 2 weeks include" list: a bed in the women's dorm (or your own place, same price), how many meals a day, 10 mornings 8 to 10 (Rick at least 2), and "Flights change? Move your dates, within a year." That is everything I scrolled the whole page for.

Three more:
1. First screen: call it "Build my Ninja Week (1 minute)" rather than "Take the quiz".
2. Technique list (phone): keep a small bar at the bottom, "2 of 5 picked, pick from another area", and one line saying beginners can pick any belt colour.
3. Ready card: hide the empty Week 2 circles, or say "Week 2: Rick builds it from the group".

Cut: "New to AI? Rick can help with that too" (on a martial arts holiday page it threw me), and the $1,000 add-a-week line appearing three times.

## Legit or gimmick

The place is real: the dorm photo, the gym grid, the press list. I decided it was real at "Rick Tew. A real person, a real background." But no reviews from past guests anywhere, the photos and video look a decade old, and "by Rick Tew" turns into Rick for 2 of 5 mornings. Worth $2,222 plus a bunk? Not yet. **Vote: UNSURE.**

## My own question

What exactly is included, and what if my flights change? Flights: answered, briefly ("your dates can move, within a year of buying"), but it is tucked under "All sales are final", so I read the scary half first. No word on what happens if I can't come at all, or on insurance. Included: answered for training and the bed, not for food. Where on Samui, and how I get there from the airport: not answered.

- "Not a boot camp" on the first screen, and the "What it is not" box. "Every drill has an easier version."
- A women's dorm, and a real photo of it.
- The one-line technique explanations; "1 picked" on the tiles.
- Prices plain, all four in one picker.
- "Your plan goes to Rick, then the secure checkout, where you add your arrival date."
- "Questions first? Ask Rick, with your picks" filled my picks into the question box. Lovely.
- Laptop: the ready card and Book fit on one screen.
