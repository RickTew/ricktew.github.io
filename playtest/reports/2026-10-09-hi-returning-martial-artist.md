## Verdict in one line
Write first, not buy: the teacher's record looks real, but $2,222 for a hostel bunk, a FAQ saying Rick may teach only two classes a week, and not one word about bad knees keep my card in my wallet.

(About five seconds to get "two weeks on Koh Samui, train with Rick Tew, 2 hours a day, relaxed"; it wants me to Enrol. Who it is for: holiday people, I think.)

## The colour
Red on white reads martial: dojo banner, belt, flag. Energetic, not cheap. It works on the headline, Enrol and the dark form's red button. It bothers me where every small thing is red too (labels, the big 2s, glowing pills): the price card looks like a supplement ad. Calm blue would feel like a yoga retreat or an insurance site. The colour is fine; the jokes feel salesy. Vote: **RED**.

- A day in four cards: clear in seconds.
- Price on the page, all-in, plus the What's included table.
