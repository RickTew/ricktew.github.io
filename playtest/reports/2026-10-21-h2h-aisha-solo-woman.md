# Head-to-head, 21 Oct 2026: Aisha, the solo traveller who wants real self-defense

## Verdict in one line

/hininja/: the calm page is the one I would write to, because it looks like a real place where my safety is taken seriously, but it only gets my money once it shows me the real class with women in it that I only found on /gns/.

## First 10 seconds

**/hininja/ (phone, then laptop):** a two-week martial arts stay on Koh Samui with a teacher called Rick Tew. For travellers wanting some training on holiday. It wants me to Enrol. Real, low key. What threw me was the top bar: "Which ninja? You are in the HI Ninja door. Take the AI Ninja door." AI? Is this a tech company?

**/gns/:** a cartoon ninja with a finger on its lips, "SHHT!", then "REAL NINJA SKILLS. ZERO SERIOUSNESS." A party hostel or T-shirt brand, for backpackers wanting a laugh. It wants Enroll Now or a quiz. First read: gimmick.

Pressed: the FAQs that touched me, the /gns/ sealed file ("Meet the sensei" tape, Asia file) and the quiz. Skipped: family, stay longer and own place FAQs (not me), the Pocket Dojo, WinJitsu, coaching, RTMS and history cards (I came for a stay), the other tapes and belts.

## Legit or gimmick

**/hininja/: UNSURE.** The tone, the "What's included" table and "Not a fight camp. No sparring unless you ask." built trust. Then "Who teaches it" says "The goofy is the whole point. Every session is silly and all about fun," and the only pictures are Rick mid jump kick on a viewpoint and Rick holding a microphone. No class, no gym, no students. I saw no video either.

**/gns/: LEGIT, for the training.** Decided at the collage, "Inside NinjaGym on Koh Samui: red mats, real training": eight real shots, at least four of women training. The "Meet the sensei" tape and the Asia file photo sealed it. The training is real. The wrapper is not for me.

## The same thing?

Yes, on the first screen of /hininja/: the "Our Gooffy version" button, then "Its name is Gooffy Ninja ShhT!!" in the next section. Same $2,222, same day plan, the "What it is" paragraph word for word. Not confusing, but odd: the serious page sells a program whose name reads like a swear word.

## The price as I understood it

$2,222 for two weeks, "all in": training, a bunk in a shared hostel dorm (women's dorm on request) and simple meals. A 2-hour class 8am to 10am, Monday to Friday, with Rick or a NinjaGym Guide; Rick teaches at least 2 a week. An optional 2-hour evening review. So about 10 formal classes, maybe 4 with Rick. All sales final; my dates can move to another two weeks within a year. /gns/ adds a free month of a WinJitsu app and a T-shirt. Neither says US or Canadian dollars, or what happens on weekends. For a bunk and about 20 hours of formal teaching it feels steep; I would pay it for real self-defense and a safe bed. "Train a little, see the island a lot" under the hero made it sound like a lot for a little.

## My own question

Who teaches, are there other women, where do I sleep, how much contact with strangers.

- **Who teaches:** /hininja/ FAQ answers it plainly. Nobody says who the Guides are, or if any is a woman.
- **Other women:** only /gns/, in the collage. /hininja/ shows no student at all.
- **Where I sleep:** both say "A women's dorm on request." in one line. Nothing on the hostel: distance from the gym, locks, walking back at night. The /gns/ quiz offered no women's dorm; "I need my own room" meant find and pay for it myself.
- **Contact:** "No sparring unless you ask" helps. But self-defense practice means being grabbed. By whom? Neither says.
- **Self-defense:** one word in a list on both pages. The best answer was the /gns/ quiz result: "real self-defense, awareness and escapes ... Confidence, for you, has to be usable on a Tuesday." That should not be hidden behind a quiz.
