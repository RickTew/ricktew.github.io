## Verdict in one line
Dana would not write today. She would screenshot the two price cards and the quiz result and send them to the friend who sent her the link with "is it really two grand a month?": the page is honest enough that she trusts the man, but the thing she came for (the evening email pile, off her plate, on her phone, through WhatsApp) turned out to be either $2,222 a month, "quoted in writing after the free look", or "a build, not a switch". ## Did I understand what is sold, what it costs, and what happens next?

- **What is sold: yes, by the end, and not from the top.** The sentence that told me was under the ticks: "I do the computer work. You never log in, renew, fix or learn anything; when something needs changing, you tell me." and then the big card: "I manage the AI ninjas that draft every reply and file every payment. The top of the page ("I'm the person behind your AI ninjas") did not tell me.
- **What it costs: yes for the two things I do not want, no for the thing I do.** "$222 to build, then $99 a month" is a website. "$2,222 a month, flat" is the whole business. Just the inbox is "quoted in writing after the free look", with no number anywhere, and the quiz confirmed the gap: "$500 a month back against $2,222 a month. It does not add up yet." WhatsApp is extra and unpriced ("a build, not a switch").
- **What happens next: yes.** The mailbox says "Your message lands with The Ninja Agent first. So: a robot emails me, then Rick, then a free look at my hours, in writing, no call. I would not be embarrassed to write. I just do not have a reason to yet.
