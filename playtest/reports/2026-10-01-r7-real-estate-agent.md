# Jason, real estate agent with one assistant: 2026-10-01 (round seven)

Read on a simulated iPhone 13 (390 x 664, touch, headless Chromium), starting cold at https://ricktew.com/aininja/. About 20 minutes in character. The page is about 19,500 px tall, so roughly 29 phone screens. Both mail endpoints were blocked before the page loaded. The log shows zero blocked requests, so nothing was even attempted. Nothing was sent: I never pressed the mailbox's send button or the quiz's "Send me the breakdown". The only things I typed were a made-up name and a made-up address (jason@example.test), into the two visible labelled mailbox fields.

## Verdict in one line
In real life I close the tab at the kids' gym story. Read to the end, I would write one short test message, and only to time the "writes back within a minute" Ninja Agent. I still would not be a buyer. The words lead, CRM and follow-up never appear in the offer, and "your team presses every send" reads to me as "as slow as my assistant".

## Moments that worked
- Prices about 3 screens down, in plain numbers: "$222 to build, then $99 a month" and "$2,222 a month, flat". No "book a call to learn pricing". That is rare and it kept me reading.
- "The AI is in the price." and "Monthly, cancel anytime, effective at the end of the paid month." are clean terms. No meter, no contract.
- "How it works" is four lines I understood in five seconds: "You tell me the job. My AI ninjas do the legwork... A person checks it... I manage all of it and report to you in writing."
- "What happens if you disappear? You lose the window, not the room... your customer records... yours in writing, they leave with you, and I keep no copy." My database is my business, and this answer matters.
- The quiz turned my hours into a dollar figure, and its result was the first place on the page to say what I care about: "whether anyone follows up before they go cold."
- "Talk to me about it" on the quiz result dropped all twelve answers, plus "Estimated value: USD 2600 a month" and "Suggested plan: Your Dojo ($2,222 a month)", into the mailbox. It also left two blanks, "My company:" and "What I would love off my plate:". That saves a busy person typing.
- The chat box's honest misses: "I do not have that one, and I would rather say so than guess." Its "how much" answer was straight and complete.
- The mailbox is itself a live demo: an AI that answers within a minute and says it is an AI. That is exactly the product I came looking for.

## The moment I knew
Section **"Hate tech, tired of it, or no time for it. You still want it to work."**, at the paragraph under the Your Dojo card ("The 'I want this Tew' button writes your pick into my mailbox at the bottom of this page. You add a line and send it."). That is about **4.5 screens down**, roughly 15 percent of the page. At that point I could say all four:
- **What he sells:** AI drafts my replies and files payments as a service, or a hosted website.
- **Who does the computer work:** him ("Built, hosted and kept running by me").
- **What it costs:** $2,222 a month flat, or $222 then $99 a month.
- **What to do next:** the button, then the mailbox.

**Still missing at that moment:**
- whether it handles portal leads at all
- how fast a lead gets an answer, if a person must press send
- whether it works with my CRM
- what just the one lead desk costs
- why it beats a part-time assistant at about the same money

**Yes, but as a test, not as a buyer.** I want to see the "within a minute" reply for myself, because speed is the whole question. The first line I would type into the mailbox:

"Solo agent plus one assistant. Can your ninja answer a new portal lead in under five minutes without one of us pressing send, does it log the lead in my CRM, and what does just that one desk cost?"

## Did I understand what is sold, what it costs, and what happens next?
- **What is sold: mostly yes.** "All of that, plus your admin handled as a service. I manage the AI ninjas that draft every reply and file every payment." **Gap:** nothing says leads, follow-up, portals or CRM. The only "lead" on the page is the Quiz Funnel row.
- **What it costs: yes.** "$2,222 a month, flat" and "$222 to build, then $99 a month". **Gap:** the one-desk price ("quoted in writing after the free audit"), and nothing to compare against an assistant's wage except the quiz math.
- **What happens next: yes.** "The 'I want this Tew' button writes your pick into my mailbox... You add a line and send it." Under the mailbox: "It writes back within a minute... I read every message and follow up myself, usually within a day." Then "The audit first, free".

## The one change
On the Your Dojo card or in "How it works", say in one line what happens when a new enquiry lands. Something like: "a reply is drafted in seconds; your team presses send, or you let one lane answer on its own, the way my mailbox below answers within a minute." Name leads and "your CRM" in the same breath. Today, the speed answer, the auto-reply option and the CRM answer sit in three different places: a folded FAQ at the bottom, grey small print under the mailbox, and a till answer. A speed-first buyer like me never gets that far.
