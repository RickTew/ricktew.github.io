# Ninja Menu round, 25 Oct 2026: Aisha (31, Toronto, solo, wants real self-defense)

Phone first, then laptop. Arrived from an Instagram ad.

## Verdict in one line

I could explain the plan to a friend, but not the two things I came for: who actually teaches me, and whether my self-defense picks get taught at all, so I would write first, not book.

## First 10 seconds

"Two week intro to martial arts by Rick Tew", relaxed, "not a boot camp". That line calmed me. The red "Take the quiz" button and the quiz card share the first phone screen, so I saw it at once and would start it: "Self-Defense" is one of the tiles. I had no idea what "HI Ninja" means.

## The quiz

Fine, close to fun. I pressed Self-Defense first and the list reads like real material: Natural Reactions ("until it happens on its own, without thinking" is exactly my freeze), Jam, Block & Run, Phases of Combat. The one-line explanations helped. My week: Natural Reactions, Jam Block & Run, Phases of Combat, Palm Heel Strike, Flat Fall Back. Five felt right.

Confusing: the belt colours. The one I wanted most, Personal Security Course ("Real defense in real places"), says RED BELT, so I assumed a beginner cannot pick it and skipped it. Jam, Block & Run is GREEN: can I learn that in week one? Ninja Tools lost me: knife defense sounds useful, but Blinding Powder and nunchaku next to it feel like cosplay. Body Movement has cartwheels and kip-ups, not for me. On the phone my picks sit below a ten-item list, so I scrolled down to check each one had landed. "Sensei Seminar" only ever meant "the morning class" to me.

## The moment to book

"Your Ninja Week is ready", and right under it WEEK 2 with five empty circles and "5 of 10 picked". Ready, or half done? Do I owe five more? I pressed Book anyway; Week 2 was still empty on the form. The stays: 2 weeks $2,222, 3 weeks $3,222, 1 month $3,997, a belt (2 months) $7,994. "Add another week for only $1,000" on the booking screen put me off: nobody has answered my questions and it is already upselling. The month does not fit a three-week trip.

## The program as I understood it

- **Sensei Seminar:** 8 to 10 every weekday morning, mixing the whole group's picks. Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week; the other days "Guides", former students "a step or two ahead". So "by Rick Tew" means about two of ten classes.
- **Hours:** 2 a day, plus an optional evening review class, 8 to 10 pm, to practice the morning. Weekends free.
- **WinJitsu:** Rick's mindset coaching (focus, goals, self-doubt), included in every stay, plus a free month of an app. How often, and one to one or in a group: GUESSED, not said.
- **Sleeping:** a shared women's dorm at NerdHostel, a gamer hostel across the street, six bunks, no extra cost. No private rooms.
- **Food:** "simple meals if you need a little boost". GUESSED: not really included. The About page says "all in", which does not match the price card.
- **Belt program:** for the serious, 2 months per belt at $7,994, yellow first. Not me.
- **My picks:** if the group mostly picks kicks and rolls, how much of my self-defense gets taught? Not answered.

## Make it pop

**The one change first:** see the last section.

1. **First quiz screen:** start with "What brings you?" (feel safer, get fit, just curious). "Feel safer" fills a starter self-defense week. That is a quiz; right now it is a menu.
2. **Technique lists:** replace the belt colour with "beginner friendly", or one line: "any level can pick any technique; you train your version."
3. **Booking screen:** drop "for only $1,000" and move "Questions first? Ask Rick, with your picks" above the name field.

**Cut:** "New to AI? Rick can help with that too" and "build a business with Rick" (unfocused), Blinding Powder, the Gooffy Ninja T-shirt line.

## Legit or gimmick

**UNSURE.** The photo grid of women training on mats, the real dorm photo and the press list made Rick a real person to me. The FAQ "Who teaches the classes?" is where I dropped: most of my self-defense would come from former students. Then the About page called the camp "Gooffy Ninja ShhT!!, zero seriousness", and safety started to look like a joke.

## My own question

Who teaches me, will other women be there, and how much body contact with strangers? Teacher: answered honestly in the FAQ, not what I hoped. Women: a women's dorm, yes; women in class, only from the photos; group size never stated. Contact: "No sparring unless you ask" helps, but Jam, Block & Run needs a partner. Who, and can I choose? Not answered.
