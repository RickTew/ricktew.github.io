# GNS round, 12 Oct 2026: Sofie, backpacker, one real skill per country

Phone only. Started on the GNS page from a hostel friend's link.

## Verdict in one line

The training is real, but the GNS page locks every proof of it in a "sealed file" and its Enroll button drops me on an AI business form, so I would read the HI page and write from there.

## Legit or gimmick

First 10 seconds on GNS: cartoon ninja saying SHHT, "Zero seriousness", $2,222. Party camp for gamers, maybe a joke. No photo of a real person or the gym on the whole page. "Zero seriousness" next to three times my daily budget almost made it a gimmick.

I decided it was real inside the file: the TRMS 2000 tape (a real flying kick in a field, old video, no polish), then the Asia file's magazine photo of students kneeling in a palm garden in Thailand. On HI I believed it on screen 2: a real photo of Rick kicking above a bay, and "It is relaxed ... about 2 hours of training a day ... Rick teaches at least 2 classes a week".

Votes: **GNS: UNSURE** (LEGIT only after the files). **HI: LEGIT.**

## The files

Would I press "Declassify the file" on my own? Maybe, on a second scroll, because I was hunting for proof. Most hostel friends would read "classified" as one more joke.

What changed my mind: the TRMS kick; Rick doing the splits on a red mat today; and the Europe file. He lived in Holland 6 years, Delft, Rotterdam, Naarden-Bussum, Dutch TV, TU Delft. For a Dutch girl that is huge, and it is two taps deep.

ONE thing to show without pressing: the Asia file's Thailand photo (students in black kneeling under palms), where the drawn sunset sits in "Eat. Train. Think. Play. Sleep."

## Which page

**Vote: HI.** Its first paragraph answers my real question (holiday or hard camp?), shows a real photo, names who teaches, and says "A women's dorm on request". GNS is what I would forward to the hostel group as a laugh; HI is what I would read before paying.
