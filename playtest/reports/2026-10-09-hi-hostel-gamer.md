# HI round, 9 Oct 2026: Jonas, the gamer at Nerd Hostel

## Verdict in one line

I got it in about 8 seconds and the price is honest, but $2,222, all sales final, only old-camp photos, and a FAQ saying Rick teaches "at least 2 classes a week": I would write first, not buy.

## The colour

**Vote: EITHER.**

Red says "martial", fine. The amount is the problem. It is on two ENROLs, "TRAIN WITH RICK TEW" in giant red caps, a red label over every section, the glowing red price card, four red "2"s, BUY and SEND. It feels like a supplement sale and says "hardcore camp" while the words say "relaxed". It adds pressure more than it costs trust. Blue would feel like a booking site, but for a ninja gym it looks like a bank, and the top bar says blue is the AI thing. The dark form at the bottom works. Keep red for Buy; make headlines black.
