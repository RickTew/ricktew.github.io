# Mei, the yoga studio owner - 2026-10-01

Round seven. Played on an iPhone 13 size (390 x 664) in headless Chromium, touch on, about 20 minutes in character. Start page https://ricktew.com/aininja/ . The page is about 19,500 px tall, so about 30 phone screens. Both mail endpoints (`ricktew-contact`, `ricktew-intake`) were blocked before every page load and every attempt was logged: zero requests were made to either one, in any script. The mailbox was filled with a made-up name and mei@example.test in the visible labelled fields only. Send was never pressed.

## Verdict in one line
In real life Mei closes the tab about 8 screens down, sure that "$2,222 a month, flat, any number of people" is for real companies and that the $222 plan is a website she already has. The only place that told her, kindly and honestly, that a studio her size should start smaller is the quiz result, 23 screens down after 12 taps, and even that smaller start has no price.

## Landing cold (top screen)
- What is this page? Some man (Rick Tew) who builds AI things for a business, and "either you run it yourself or I run it with you".
- Who is it for? Business owners who want time back. The cafe owner's story ("this guy took my admin away") fits.
- What is it asking me to do? Press "I want this Tew!!" or take a quiz.
- How long until I knew what is offered: about 20 seconds for the vague version ("I build the same for your business"). I did not know WHAT he builds, or that it means my messages and bookings, until the two price cards on screen 3 to 4, about a minute of skimming in. (Not proposing a change to the top screen, as asked.)

## Moments of friction (the core of the report)

1. **Photo caption, screen 2: "Rick Tew, AI version".** I expected a photo of the person. The caption tells me the photo is made by AI. So is he real? On a page about letting him into my DMs, the first face I see is a fake one. Felt: a small trust wobble. SEVERITY: annoyance.

2. **The number strip under the photo: "26+ years a ninja", "22+ years a nerd", "6,242+ rounds of changes since March", "100+ solutions built".** Big numbers are exactly what pushes me away. I have no idea what a "round of changes" is or why 6,242 of them should matter to me. The numbers also counted up while I looked at them (24 became 26). SEVERITY: annoyance.

3. **Sticky bar at the bottom of every screen ("Take the quiz", "Ask", x).** It takes about one eighth of my phone the whole way down and sits over the five boxes' buttons and captions. I would have closed it with the x. SEVERITY: cosmetic.

4. **"Full plan · Your Dojo, $2,222 a month, flat".** I expected to see whether there is something for a studio like mine. "Any number of people", "your team checks it and presses send" reads like an office with staff. My "team" is six contract teachers who teach and leave. $2,222 is more than a month of one teacher's pay. Felt: this is for real companies, not me. SEVERITY: would-quit (together with 5).

5. **"Basic plan · R2 Hosting, $222 to build, then $99 a month".** I hoped this was my small start. It is "Your website or small app, with the forms that bring work in". I already have a booking page through my booking app. My problem is the DMs, the waitlist and the cancellation messages, and neither card names any of those. The one line that might be me, "Smaller than that? One desk on its own, without the monthly plan, is quoted in writing after the free audit", has no number at all. SEVERITY: would-quit.

6. **"The first questions", the answer I needed most is folded.** "Does it replace the apps I already pay for?" answers exactly my worry ("It works alongside what you already use, like your booking app or your till."), but it sits behind a plus, fourth in a list of seven. I opened it because the question was mine. A faster skimmer would not. SEVERITY: annoyance.

7. **"Five words · five boxes" and "Read the five in one paragraph".** Two lines above I was told "You do not want it explained. You want it done." Then the page teaches me five words (Dojo, ninja, seat, belt, sensei) with cartoon ninjas before I can read the rest. I did not open the paragraph. SEVERITY: annoyance.

8. **"Five months. Solutions and goals. One reason." (the story).** This is where I would really have left. It turns into a kids' martial arts gym in Thailand with "30,000+ sessions since opening", "850+ kids served each month", "5,000+ app bookings", "1,000+ members in the app", then later "6+ hours of teaching", "1,900+ rounds of changes, for them".
