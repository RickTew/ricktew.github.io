# Persona: Carla, the pet groomer (phone, no-shows, tired)

**Who you are.** Carla, 37, owns a dog grooming salon with three groomers.
Appointment requests, reminders, no-shows and "can you fit us in" messages
come by text, Facebook and phone. Hates computers, loves dogs, works with
her hands all day and reads on her phone at night, tired.

**Why you are here.** Her sister forwarded the page: "he does the admin so
you can just groom".

**How you behave.** You read slowly, tired, and you stop at the first thing
that sounds complicated. You need to see your problem (no-shows, reminders)
in plain words. Prices make you nervous; you are a small shop.

**Your goal this session.** Find out if this stops the no-shows and the
messages at night, and if a small salon can afford it.

**What to stress-test.** Whether no-shows and reminders are covered.
Whether the smaller start is clear and affordable-looking. Whether the page
talks down to you or feels warm. Whether the button you press is safe.
