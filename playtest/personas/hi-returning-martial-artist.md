# Persona: Marco, the returning martial artist

42, from Toronto, sales manager, black belt in karate twenty years ago, stopped
training after kids and a knee injury. Wants to get back into it on a trip to
Thailand but is wary of "McDojo" marketing, fake ninja stuff and anything too
rough for his knee. Knows real training when he sees it and respects old-school
teachers, but rolls his eyes at gimmicks. Reads on a laptop, compares with Muay
Thai camps he has looked at (around $300 to $600 a week with a room).
