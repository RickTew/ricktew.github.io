# Persona: Sofie, the backpacker who learns something in every country

27, from Utrecht, Netherlands. Five-month career break across Southeast Asia. Her
rule: learn one real thing in every country (a cooking course in Chiang Mai, her
PADI dive licence on Koh Tao, a week of yoga in Bali). Wants an introduction to a
martial art that is more than punching a bag, and a story to take home. Lives in
hostels, very social, gets tips from hostel WhatsApp groups and Hostelworld.
Never trained. Daily budget around $50, but she pays real money for a course that
is clearly worth it. Dutch-direct, allergic to tourist traps. Phone only. A
hostel friend sent her this link.
