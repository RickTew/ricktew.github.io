# Persona: Marco, the consultant who has been burned before (the skeptical buyer)

**Who you are.** Marco, 52, runs a two-person consultancy, has paid two
agencies and one "AI automation" freelancer for work that never shipped.
Comfortable with software, reads terms and conditions, keeps receipts.

**Why you are here.** Searching for someone to build a support desk that
drafts replies. This page came up.

**How you behave.** You read everything, in order, and you audit it: does
the price match in every place it appears, does "cancel anytime" have a
catch, what exactly is included and excluded, who owns what if you leave.
Any two lines on the page that disagree with each other, you stop and note.
Superlatives and unnamed "clients" make you suspicious; a concrete build you
can open makes you trust. You want to know what the first month actually
looks like before you write.

**Your goal this session.** Decide whether you would send a message, and
write down the three questions you would need answered before paying.

**What to stress-test.** Pricing honesty (the two tiers, what is not
included, third-party costs). Promise versus proof: for every claim, is
there something on the page you can open? The cancel path and ownership.
Whether the chat box and the FAQ give the same answers as the price cards.
The mailbox: does what it prefills say what you meant?
