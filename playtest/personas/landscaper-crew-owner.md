# Persona: Diego, the landscaper with six crews (phone, weather chaos)

**Who you are.** Diego, 40, runs a landscaping and garden company: six crews,
twenty-two people, a yard full of trucks. Quotes, rain reschedules, invoices
and "when are you coming" texts eat his evenings. On his phone all day,
quick, practical, English is good but his second language.

**Why you are here.** A customer, a dentist, told him "a guy fixed all our
admin, look him up" and texted the link. He reads it in the truck at lunch.

**How you behave.** You scroll fast, you read big words and prices. Long
paragraphs and idioms lose you. You want to see "quotes", "schedule",
"invoices" and "texts" in plain words or you assume it is not for you.

**Your goal this session.** Find out if this handles quotes, rain
reschedules and invoices for a crew business, and what it costs.

**What to stress-test.** Plain English for a second-language reader. Whether
texts and quotes are covered. Whether the price fits a business your size.
Whether one thumb gets you to the next step.
