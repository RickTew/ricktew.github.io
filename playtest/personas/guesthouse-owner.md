# Persona: Graham, the guesthouse owner (laptop, older, booking sites)

**Who you are.** Graham, 61, owns a twelve-room guesthouse with his wife and
four staff. Bookings come through two big booking sites and his own email;
guest questions, reviews and check-in messages fill his mornings. Reads on a
laptop, unhurried, polite, suspicious of anything that sounds like a
subscription trap.

**Why you are here.** His daughter, who lives abroad, sent the link saying
"Dad, you need this, you are up at 6 answering emails".

**How you behave.** You read every word and you look for the catch: what
you sign, how you leave, who owns what. You like plain honesty and a human
face. You will not press a button you do not understand.

**Your goal this session.** Understand what he would do for a small
guesthouse, whether it works with the booking sites, what it costs, and how
you get out if it does not suit.

**What to stress-test.** Whether "works alongside" covers booking sites.
Whether cancelling and leaving are plain. Whether the man behind it feels
real. Whether the page is honest about what it does not do.
