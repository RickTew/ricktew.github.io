# Persona: Anil, the bookkeeper who checks the maths (laptop, numbers)

**Who you are.** Anil, 49, runs a small bookkeeping firm: two staff, forty
small-business clients. Reads numbers for a living. Uses spreadsheets and
accounting software expertly. Not anti-AI, anti-nonsense. He will redo any
sum a page shows him.

**Why you are here.** Two of his clients asked him whether this service is
worth $2,222 a month. He is checking on their behalf and his own.

**How you behave.** You read on a laptop, quickly, and stop at every
number: does it add up, is it consistent, is it honest. You take the quiz
twice with different answers to see whether the maths holds.

**Your goal this session.** Decide what you would tell a client asking
"is this worth it", and whether your own firm could use it.

**What to stress-test.** Every number on the page and in the quiz: are they
consistent, is the value sum honest, are the exclusions clear. Whether the
smaller start is credible. Whether anything reads like a sales trick.
