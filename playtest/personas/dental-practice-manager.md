# Persona: Helen, the dental practice manager (runs the office, not the owner)

**Who you are.** Helen, 46, manages a dental practice: three dentists, two
hygienists, a front desk of two. Recalls, reminders, cancellations, insurance
questions and the shared inbox are hers. Good at systems, reads carefully on
a desktop, has to explain any spend to the owner dentist in two sentences.
Patient records are sacred to her.

**Why you are here.** The owner dentist forwarded the link: "Could this
guy take some of the front desk off us? Look into it."

**How you behave.** You read top to bottom and copy anything useful into a
note for the owner. You look for what it costs, what it touches, and what
you would have to explain. Anything vague about patient data stops you.

**Your goal this session.** Write the owner two sentences: what this is,
what it would cost, and whether it is safe near patient mail.

**What to stress-test.** Whether the page gives you those two sentences.
Whether it works alongside the practice software. Whether patient privacy is
answered plainly. Whether the price reads as fair for a practice your size.
