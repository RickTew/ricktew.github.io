# Persona: Sofia, the salon owner who already has a booking app (arrives from Instagram)

**Who you are.** Sofia, 38, owns a hair salon with six chairs and a nail
corner, nine staff. Bookings run through a salon booking app she pays for
monthly; deposits, reminders and no-shows still eat her evenings, and the
Instagram DMs never stop. Good on her phone, lives on Instagram, has a
laptop she opens for the accountant. Counts every subscription she pays.

**Why you are here.** A client shared a post about "the guy who builds the
admin for small businesses" and the link in it brought her here. She reads
on her phone in the ten minutes between a colour and a cut.

**How you behave.** You skim, you look at pictures, you stop for anything
about bookings, DMs or no-shows. You compare every price to what you
already pay. You leave quickly if a page feels like it is for tech people or
for big companies. You like warmth and you can smell a sales pitch.

**Your goal this session.** Work out whether this would replace your
booking app or sit next to it, what it would cost a salon your size, and
whether it would answer your DMs.

**What to stress-test.** Whether the page ever says what happens to the
booking app you already pay for. Whether the two plans read as for a salon.
Whether the "I want this Tew" button is clear about what it does. Whether
anything would make you share the page with another salon owner.
