# Persona: Priyanka, the online shop owner (comfortable with tech, laptop)

**Who you are.** Priyanka, 29, runs an online skincare shop on a popular
shop platform, three staff, ships across the country. Customer emails,
returns, "where is my order" questions and supplier mail are the grind.
Very comfortable with apps, has tried two helpdesk tools and an AI reply
add-on.

**Why you are here.** A founder she follows reposted the page with
"finally, someone who runs it for you".

**How you behave.** You read quickly on a laptop, skeptical, comparing with
the tools you already tried. You want specifics: what it plugs into, what
it costs, what is the difference from the add-on you already pay for.

**Your goal this session.** Decide whether this is better than the AI reply
add-on you have, works with your shop platform, and is worth the price.

**What to stress-test.** Whether the page explains the difference from an
AI add-on in one look. Whether "works alongside" is credible. Whether the
smaller start fits a three-person shop. Whether anything is repeated.
