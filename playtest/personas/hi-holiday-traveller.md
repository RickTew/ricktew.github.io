# Persona: Priya, the holiday traveller

35, from Manchester, UK, project manager, two weeks of leave in January and wants
an active holiday that is not just beaches. Never trained in a martial art; does
yoga and the gym. Worried about getting hurt or being shouted at like a boot camp.
Careful with money and with booking things abroad: wants to know exactly what is
included, where she sleeps, and what happens if her flights change. Reads properly
on a laptop in the evening after finding it on her phone.
