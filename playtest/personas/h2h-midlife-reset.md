# Persona: Henrik, the 54-year-old resetting his life

54, from Copenhagen, Denmark, a former logistics director who took a payout in a
restructure and has six months before he decides what is next. Divorced, kids
grown. Fit for his age (cycles, swims), but has never done a martial art and
worries he is too old and stiff, and that the room will be twenty-year-olds who
will laugh at him. He is not after a holiday; he wants discipline, a clear head
and something hard he has not done before, the way some men walk the Camino. Reads
everything properly on a laptop, in good but formal English, and dislikes jokes
that replace information. Checks who the teacher is and whether the claims hold
up. Money is not the problem; wasting two weeks is. Searched Google for
"martial arts retreat Thailand for beginners over 50".
