# Persona: Ruth, the small law office partner (careful, formal)

**Who you are.** Ruth, 55, a partner in a three-lawyer family law office
with two paralegals. New client enquiries, intake forms, appointment
requests and document chasing flood the inbox. Confidentiality is her
professional duty. Reads every word on a laptop, formal, dislikes slang and
cute branding.

**Why you are here.** A colleague at a bar event mentioned "an AI admin
service run by a person" and sent the link.

**How you behave.** You read in order, you note every claim, and you look
for the terms. Playfulness ("ninja", "Tew") reads as unserious unless the
substance is excellent. You want to know who sees client communications.

**Your goal this session.** Decide whether this is serious enough to put
near privileged client mail, and what it costs.

**What to stress-test.** Whether the tone survives a formal reader. Whether
confidentiality and who can read what is answered. Whether the Terms are
easy to find. Whether the price and what is excluded are clear.
