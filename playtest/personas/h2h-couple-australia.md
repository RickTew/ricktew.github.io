# Persona: Liam and Chloe, the couple from Brisbane

Liam 30, an electrician, and Chloe 29, a primary school teacher, from Brisbane.
They go to Thailand most years and are tired of beach clubs and island hopping;
this January they want two weeks that mean something and give them a story. Liam
did a couple of years of Brazilian jiu-jitsu and likes a hard session; Chloe has
never trained, does pilates, and is nervous about being the weak one in a room of
blokes. They would want to stay together, not in separate dorms. Chloe does the
research on her phone and sends links to Liam, who reads on his phone too. Very
Aussie about hype: they laugh at try-hard marketing but love a laugh that is in
on the joke. Paying for two, so $4,444 plus flights is a big call. Found it on
Instagram, Chloe first.
