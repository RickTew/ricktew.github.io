# Persona: Lena, the long-stayer on Koh Samui

39, from Hamburg, a UX designer working remotely. On Thailand's long-stay visa;
lives about six months a year in a rented bungalow in Lamai and works mornings at
a coworking space. Wants a structured hobby and a real local community beyond the
expat Facebook group, and something that gets her moving. Already has a home on
the island, so she would want the classes, not a hostel bunk. Her younger brother
visits in February and might want to join. Reads on a laptop in the evening. Saw
a post in the Samui expats Facebook group and clicked. Practical, friendly, dislikes
being sold to.
