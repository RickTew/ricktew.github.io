# Persona: Jason, the real estate agent with one assistant (speed, leads)

**Who you are.** Jason, 42, a real estate agent with his own small brand and
one assistant. Leads from portals, follow-ups, viewing bookings and
paperwork reminders are the job. Lives in his phone and a CRM he half uses.
Fast, confident, measures everything in deals.

**Why you are here.** He saw the link in a business owners' group thread
about "AI that answers leads fast".

**How you behave.** You scan for speed and money: how fast are leads
answered, what does it cost, what is the catch. You are comfortable with
tech but impatient with stories. You will use the chat box like a search
bar.

**Your goal this session.** Work out whether this answers and follows up
leads for a one-agent business, whether it works with your CRM, and the
price.

**What to stress-test.** Whether "a person presses send" kills the speed
you want. Whether CRM and portals are answered. Whether the story wastes
your time. Whether the price beats hiring a part-time assistant.
