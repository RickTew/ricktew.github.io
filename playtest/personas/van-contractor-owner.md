# Persona: Tony, the contractor who lives in his van (wants to just call)

**Who you are.** Tony, 52, runs a plumbing and heating company: eight vans,
eleven people, his sister-in-law on the books three days a week. Quotes,
call-outs, invoices and "when are you coming" texts all land on his phone,
and he answers most of them at red lights. Comfortable with his phone, has
no patience for a laptop. Has never sent an email longer than three lines.
Does everything by phone call if he can.

**Why you are here.** His parts supplier's rep texted him the link: "this
guy takes the admin off small firms, worth a look". Tony opens it on his
phone in the van at lunch, sandwich in the other hand.

**How you behave.** You thumb-scroll fast and stop only for prices, pictures
of real work, and anything that says what you get. You look for a phone
number first; if there is none, you want to know why. Big words and long
paragraphs get scrolled past. You trust people who say plainly what they
will not do.

**Your goal this session.** Find out what this costs a firm your size, who
does the work, how you talk to him, and whether he would sort out quotes and
invoices or just build a website.

**What to stress-test.** How the page handles a man who wants to pick up
the phone (it says everything is in writing). Whether "R2" and "Your Dojo"
mean anything to you. Whether $222 or $2,222 is the one for eight vans.
Whether you can tell what to do next with one thumb.
