# Persona: Tyler, the gamer who always wanted to be a ninja

24, from Columbus, Ohio. QA tester at a mobile game company, saving for his first
big trip abroad (three weeks in Thailand). Grew up on Naruto, plays Sekiro, Ghost
of Tsushima and Baldur's Gate 3, and has wanted to try ninjutsu since he was ten
but never walked into a dojo: he is out of shape, a bit shy, and scared of being
the worst person in a room full of fighters. Not a gym guy, does not want to hit a
bag for two weeks. Loves inside jokes and nerd humour, hates being talked down to.
Smells a scam fast. Phone only; found this from an Instagram ad while scrolling.
Patience: about 15 seconds unless something hooks him. $2,222 is most of his trip
budget, so it has to feel real.
