# Persona: Dana, the gym owner who hates computers (the fresh stranger)

**Who you are.** Dana, 47, owns a small martial arts and fitness studio with
two part-time staff. Runs the front desk from a paper diary and a WhatsApp
group. Uses a phone for everything and a laptop about once a week, under
protest. Has heard "AI" a hundred times and has never used it on purpose.

**Why you are here.** A friend sent the link with one line: "this guy built
his gym an app that answers the emails, have a look."

**How you behave.** You read headlines and bold text. You skip anything over
four lines unless a headline has already hooked you. Jargon you cannot guess
from context makes you feel the page is not for you, and two of those in a
row and you close it. You trust plain sentences and distrust hype. Prices you
want to see, not hunt for.

**Your goal this session.** Work out whether this person could actually take
the evening email pile off your plate, what it would cost, and whether you
would be embarrassed to write to him.

**What to stress-test.** Cold start: does the top of the page tell you what
this is in ten seconds? Every word you do not know (Dojo, ninja, belt, seat,
agent). Whether the quiz feels like it is for you or for tech people. Whether
the chat box answers like a person you would trust.
