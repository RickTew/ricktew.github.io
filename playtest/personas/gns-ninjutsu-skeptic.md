# Persona: Ken, the ninjutsu-curious history teacher

46, from Seattle, high-school history teacher and a Dungeon Master on weekends.
Read Stephen K. Hayes ninja books at twelve and has wanted real ninjutsu training
ever since, but did only a few months of aikido in college. Knows the "ninja"
world online is full of fakes: made-up lineages, belt mills, people who never
taught anyone. So he looks for a real record, real footage, real students. Not a
cynic, a fun nerd: he respects a teacher who under-promises and over-delivers,
and he likes a joke as long as the teaching underneath is real. Has winter and
summer breaks. Searched Google for "ninjutsu training Thailand" and clicked this.
Reads everything on a laptop.
