# Persona: Aisha, the solo traveller who wants real self-defense

31, from Toronto, a hospital pharmacist. Three weeks solo in Thailand in January.
Last year a man grabbed her arm outside a bar and she froze; since then she has
wanted to learn self-defense she could actually use, from someone who knows what
they are doing. Never trained in anything; does spin class. Careful: wants to know
who teaches, whether there are other women, where she sleeps, and how much body
contact there is with strangers. Put off by macho fight-gym energy and by anything
that makes safety look like a joke. Reads properly, phone first, then laptop.
Found this through an Instagram ad. Will pay for quality but checks everything.
