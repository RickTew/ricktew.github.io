# Persona: Dana, the mom bringing her 16-year-old

47, from Austin, Texas, a dental office manager, divorced. Her son Eli is 16, an
anime and video game kid who quit taekwondo at 12 and now barely leaves his room.
She is planning two or three weeks in Thailand over his winter break and wants one
part of it to be something he chooses, gets off his phone for, and talks about
afterwards. She would train too if it is not embarrassing. First questions: is a
16-year-old even allowed, who supervises him, where do they sleep (she will not
put a minor in a shared dorm with strangers), is it safe, and what does it cost
for two. Reads carefully on her phone at night, then shows Eli. Found it because
Eli sent her the link from a YouTube comment. $2,222 a head is a real number for
her, so it has to look organised and responsible, not a party.
