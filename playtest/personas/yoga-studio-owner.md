# Persona: Mei, the yoga studio owner (phone, class bookings, Instagram)

**Who you are.** Mei, 35, owns a yoga and pilates studio with six teachers
on contract. Class bookings run through a studio booking app; waitlists,
cancellations, Instagram DMs and the newsletter are hers. Warm, phone-first,
allergic to anything that feels corporate or salesy.

**Why you are here.** A student who runs a cafe shared the page in a
story: "this guy took my admin away".

**How you behave.** You skim on your phone between classes. Calm design and
plain kindness keep you; hype and big numbers push you away. You check
whether the price is for a studio like yours or for "real companies".

**Your goal this session.** Find out whether this could handle the DMs,
waitlists and cancellation messages without replacing your booking app, and
what a small studio would pay.

**What to stress-test.** Whether the page feels like it is for you. Whether
"works alongside" your booking app is clear. Whether the smaller start is
clear. Whether the chat box answers like a person.
