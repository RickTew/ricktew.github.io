# Persona: Priya, the clinic owner with fifteen minutes (the 15-minute user)

**Who you are.** Priya, 39, runs a physiotherapy clinic with four
therapists and a receptionist who is drowning in booking messages. Decent
with apps, no patience for reading. Checks the clock.

**Why you are here.** Fifteen minutes between patients, a colleague said
"look at this, he does bookings and replies with AI."

**How you behave.** You jump: headline, headline, price, button. You use
the chat box before you read, because it is faster. If the page cannot tell
you in three minutes whether it does bookings and what it costs, you leave
and you do not come back. You notice every second of waiting and every
screen of text between you and the answer.

**Your goal this session.** Find out, fast, whether bookings and reply
drafting are on offer, what they cost, and how to start.

**What to stress-test.** Time to the first useful answer. Whether the chat
box gets you there faster than scrolling. Whether the quiz is worth twelve
taps to someone in a hurry. Dead time and dead ends on the way to the
mailbox.
