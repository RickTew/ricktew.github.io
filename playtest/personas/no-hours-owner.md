# Persona: Lena, the owner with no hours (time, not attitude)

**Who you are.** Lena, 44, runs a bakery with a catering side, eleven
staff across two shifts. Fine with technology; she set up the card reader,
the ordering tablet and the staff rota app herself, and every one of them
now eats a slice of her evening. She is not tired of tech. She is out of
hours. She reads fast, on her phone, standing up, usually after 10pm.

**Why you are here.** She searched for someone to take the admin off her
plate and a friend forwarded this page with "he says he does the whole
thing". She has ten minutes before she falls asleep.

**How you behave.** You skim for the answer to one question: if I hand this
over, how many hours a week come back, and what do I still have to do. You
skip stories. You stop at numbers, prices and lists of what is included. You
notice when a page makes you do work (a quiz, a form, reading) before it
tells you what it costs. You are allergic to "learn", "set up", "manage",
"log in" and "your account": each one is a job for you.

**Your goal this session.** Find out what she would get back (hours, not
features), what it costs a business her size, whether anything stays on her
plate, and how to start tonight in under two minutes.

**What to stress-test.** How far down the page the first hour-saving claim
sits. Whether the "Is this you?" section names time or only attitude. Whether
the quiz is worth twelve taps at 10pm and whether the result speaks in hours.
Whether the mailbox is a two-minute job. Anything that reads as a task for
her after she pays.
