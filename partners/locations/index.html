Location search: Bangkok and beyond

The working list of sites for the first NinjaGym centers. Each one shows its area, the space, the rent or deal on offer, how to reach its leasing office, and where the hunt stands.

Nobody contacts a mall, broker or landlord until Rick says yes, one contact at a time. Every fact here has its source. Unverified marks anything not yet read at the source.

What we look for

The roadmap's rules for every site

Near schools

Lean centers near schools, where the after-school and weekend demand already exists. Lower rent and entry budget, quicker to fill.

Affluent areas

Bigger-budget centers anchor affluent neighborhoods, with the space and family spending for a full climb zone, game room and events.

Spacing matters

Centers spread across Bangkok's districts, never stacked, so each one adds new families instead of splitting the same ones.

The numbers to beat: the roadmap's model assumes rent of about ฿60,000 a month per center (its slider runs ฿20,000 to ฿260,000). NinjaGym centers are drop-and-go: parents leave their ninja training and go shop, so a mall with a kids' floor or a supermarket fits the model.

The lead, from 2024

Free rent for a kids' martial arts school, in 2024

Panipak Taekwondo, JAS Green Village Prawet

JAS Asset gave Panipak Taekwondo, a kids' Taekwondo school, its space on the 2nd floor of JAS Green Village Prawet at no cost for the whole 3-year contract, to help make new Taekwondo athletes.

Read it as a one-off: the opening was built around Olympic gold medallist Panipak "Tennis" Wongpattanakit. No other free-rent offer to a kids' or education tenant was found at any mall, and that mall now has a kids' martial arts school. JAS's other malls (below) share one leasing line, so one call covers seven of them.

Sources: mitihoon.com, 2 September 2024 (the same press release is on share2trade). Whether the school still trades there in 2026: unknown.

Takeovers

Where Thai lease takeovers are posted

A lease takeover (เซ้ง) can bring a fitted space and a lower start. No kids' martial arts school was found for sale or closing anywhere in Thailand (24 Oct 2026). Most takeover ads sit in Facebook groups that search engines cannot read, so these are checked by hand.
