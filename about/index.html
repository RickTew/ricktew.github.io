01 // Who he is

Back to map ↑

The martial artist who turned movement into a science.

Rick Tew ran away to California at eight. He fell for ninjitsu at fourteen, was on television at fifteen, and opened his first dojo at nineteen. At twenty-one he moved to Europe to build his own system, and has run live-in martial arts programs for over 20 years in the US, Latin America, Europe and Asia. Thirty years in, the mission is still simple: help you get a Black Belt in what you do.

03 // Train the mind

WinJitsu, the mental martial art.

"Win" plus "Jitsu" is the True Art of Winning. It trains the mind to beat its own negative Ninja: fear, doubt, stress and laziness. Written down as the five-book "Be a Black Belt in What You Do" series, one book per finger of the hand. Together they make a fist to break through, and a grip to hang on.
